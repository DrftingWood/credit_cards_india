You can get the card in just minutes through a fully digital onboarding process on the Scapia app. The virtual card can be activated immediately, and your physical card will reach you in 2-5 days.

Here’s a quick look at the top features and benefits of this premium card:

  • Enjoy unlimited domestic lounge access and airport privileges by spending Rs. 20000 a month on your BOBCARD Scapia Credit Card
