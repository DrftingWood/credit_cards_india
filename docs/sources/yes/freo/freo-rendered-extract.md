The issuer's current product page is JavaScript-rendered. This extract records the visible page and terms text. The page links a card-specific MITC PDF through a generic `/pdf` endpoint; the link and indexed PDF URL returned an HTML/JavaScript shell rather than a PDF. The current consolidated MITC v18 is archived locally and used for common charges and surcharge terms. No shell is archived as a PDF.

## Current rendered issuer product page

- Issuance status: the issuer says it is no longer accepting applications and asks existing cardholders to contact the bank to upgrade into other card variants.
- Header benefits: unlimited 1% cashback, zero joining fee and 1% fuel-surcharge waiver.
- Cash back is valid only on personal spends. The current terms exclude fuel, rent (MCC 6513; maximum three rent transactions in rolling 30 days), wallet transfers, government MCCs, ATM withdrawals, utility payments, cash advances, fees/charges and EMI transactions. The issuer does not publish a detailed statement-credit timing or cashback transfer mechanism in the visible terms.
- Fees: joining fee zero; annual membership INR 399 plus applicable taxes. First-year membership fee is waived after INR 10,000 qualifying retail spend within 30 days. Renewal membership fee is waived after INR 100,000 qualifying retail spend during the card anniversary year.
- Finance: 3.99% per month / 47.88% annually on revolving credit, cash advances and overdue amounts.
- Eligibility: age 21-60; salaried or self-employed; net salary at least INR 25,000 per month or self-employed ITR at least INR 750,000. Existing YES BANK credit-card holders are ineligible. No bureau score or residency cutoff is stated.
- The page does not establish a single network or network tier, exact launch date, lounge access or concierge service.

## Current consolidated MITC v18 (05-Oct-2026)

Archived as `yesbank-mitc-v18-current-2026-10-26.pdf`; it is a copy of a current MITC PDF downloaded and signature-checked during the YES Bank FinBooster review.

- Foreign-currency markup for FREO is 3.40%; the v18 table shows the same before and effective 15-Jun-2026.
- Cash advance costs 2.5% of the withdrawal amount or INR 650, whichever is higher, effective 15-Jun-2026.
- The card's monthly finance rate of 3.99% is also stated on the current FREO page.
- Fuel surcharge waiver is 1% for INR 400-INR 5,000 transactions, up to INR 125 per statement cycle. The general MITC excludes UPI fuel from the waiver and states GST on fuel surcharge is not waived.
- FREO is not listed in the MITC domestic lounge threshold table; no lounge entitlement is asserted in the card record.
