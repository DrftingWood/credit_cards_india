# Doctor’s SBI Card (IMA): issuer comparison (27 Oct 2026)

Compared the card record with SBI Card’s current IMA terms, product brochure, current MITC, card page, reward accrual cap policy and SBI’s issuance policy.

## Verified and recorded

- Current joining and annual fee are INR1,499. The renewal fee is reversed at INR2 lakh or more in the preceding year; MITC confirms 3.5% forex markup and current finance/cash advance charges. The historical start date of this fee schedule is not published.
- Base earn is 1 Reward Point per INR100. The cap policy gives 5X on Medical Supplies plus Travel Booking as one 7,500-point monthly cap and International spends as a separate 7,500-point monthly cap; base points continue after either cap. IMA terms separately provide 5X on all eligible Doctor’s Day (1 July) spends; no additional Doctor’s Day cap is stated.
- IMA terms exclude balance transfers, cash advances, financial charges, disputes, petrol/service station MCCs 5172/5541/5542/5983, Merchant EMI and Flexipay; general SBI terms add rent MCC6513, government MCC9399/9311 and wallet MCC6540/6541. Current record retains these exclusions and states MCC matching is best effort.
- IMA terms state 24-month point expiry. They restrict points from exchange for cash/credit or payment of card charges, while the older brochure says catalogue/outstanding-balance redemption. SBI’s general agreement permits statement credit for eligible cards but does not resolve IMA eligibility. The record retains catalog redemption only, with no fixed point value and this conflict documented.
- Current brochure supports the INR1,500 Yatra welcome voucher, INR5,000 voucher after INR5 lakh annual spend, INR20 lakh professional indemnity cover with 0% deductible, and Priority Pass with four international visits per year, maximum two per quarter. It also advertises domestic lounge visits as a limited-time Visa offer; not represented as a current benefit. The T&C supports the INR500–4,000 fuel transaction band and INR250 per-statement surcharge-waiver cap.
- SBI’s issuance policy gives salaried age 21–65 and self-employed age 25–70. The card is for registered doctors/IMA members; no card-specific income floor or score cutoff is published. Removed the unsupported aggregator INR6 lakh income claim and repaired corrupted age text.
- Launched 28-Mar-2018 per SBI’s hosted launch archive / contemporary launch record. Current T&C uses Visa/RuPay references while the brochure includes Visa Signature language; single network and tier remain unset and the multi-network tag is used.
