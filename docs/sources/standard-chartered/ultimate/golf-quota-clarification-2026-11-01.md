## Finding and record change

Section 11 of the live linked card-specific PDF provides one complimentary golf game per month per valid Ultimate card and one complimentary coaching session per month. Sessions are domestic, non-transferable, subject to listed courses and booking/course availability; normal advance booking is four days and convenience/other charges can apply even for complimentary bookings.

The product story describes complimentary games twice monthly. Read with the terms, this is consistent with one monthly game plus one monthly coaching session. The product page's value chart lists four golf sessions per year as an illustrative annual valuation; it is not the detailed quota. The YAML now models one game and one coaching session per month rather than four annual sessions.

Other card details retain the 2026-10-25 issuer review and were not rechecked.
