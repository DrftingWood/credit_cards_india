# LIC Axis Bank Signature issuer evidence extract (2026-10-26)

## Current product page

Source: [Axis LIC Signature product page](https://www.axis.bank.in/cards/credit-card/lic-axis-bank-signature-credit-card), retrieved 2026-10-26.

- Joining and annual fees are nil; finance charge is 3.75% monthly; cash withdrawal is 2.5%, minimum INR 500; forex markup is 3.5%.
- Base earning is 1 LIC Reward Point per INR 100. LIC premium paid through official LIC gateways and foreign-currency spend earn 2 points per INR 100. Fuel, wallet load and EMI conversion are excluded from earning.
- Current activation benefit: 100% cashback up to INR 300 on the first utility bill payment within 30 days of card issuance; detailed offer conditions should be checked before treating it as an evergreen offer.
- Fuel surcharge waiver: 1% for INR 400–4,000 fuel transactions, up to INR 400 per month; GST is not refunded and fuel earns no points.
- Product page summarizes 8 complimentary domestic lounge visits per calendar year. The current Axis lounge-program PDF specifies 2 visits per quarter and an INR 50,000 eligible-spend gate in the previous three calendar months. The detailed program rules are encoded.
- Lost-card cover is described as up to the card's credit limit; no separate numeric coverage amount is published.
- Purchases of INR 1,500 or more may be converted to EMI.
- Eligibility: primary applicant age 18–70 and resident in India. Card-specific income and bureau-score cutoffs are not published.
- Product-page fee list says reward redemption fee is nil, while the common LIC catalogue terms state INR 30 plus GST per redemption request; this conflict is retained.

## Reward catalogue, exclusions and shared terms

Sources: current [LIC reward catalogue terms](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/cards/lic-rewards-points-terms-and-conditions.pdf?sfvrsn=4e614ab9_22); [LIC exclusion revision effective 2025-06-20](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/terms-and-conditions-lic-axis-bank-signature-credit-card.pdf); and Axis terms update effective 2026-08-28, archived in the neighboring LIC Platinum evidence folder.

- Redeem by phone against listed products. The catalogue charges INR 30 plus GST per request, delivers e-vouchers within 10 working days and physical products within 21 working days, expires points 12 months after accumulation, and caps virtual gift cards at 75 per financial year.
- There is no fixed rupee value per point: catalogue item prices vary.
- The 2025 notice says excluded transactions are identified by spend category, not four-digit MCC. It names utilities/telecom, education, rent, wallet loads, government services, gold/jewellery, fuel, cash advances and repayments. The 2026-08-28 update also excludes gift cards and bridge/road/toll spend for rewards, cashback, fee waivers and milestones.
- The 2026-08-28 shared terms update DCC markup to 3.5% and sets INR 1,300 late fee at total due of INR 50,000 or more.

## Unresolved or unpublished

- Current network/network tier and exact card launch date are not established by checked current sources; both remain null. “Signature” is the product name and does not establish the network tier.
- No fixed point value, accelerator cap, separate lost-card numeric limit, income threshold or bureau-score threshold is published in the checked sources.
- Lounge product page says 8 visits per calendar year; the current program specifies 2 per quarter, with prior-three-calendar-month INR 50,000 spend qualification. This is encoded as quarterly entitlement.
- The INR 30 plus GST redemption charge in LIC catalogue terms conflicts with the product page's “No” fee entry.
