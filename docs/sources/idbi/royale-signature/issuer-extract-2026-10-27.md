## Current terms captured

- Product page lists nil joining, annual and renewal fee; 2.5% monthly revolving interest and cash-advance interest; cash-advance fee 2.5% or ₹500 minimum; 3.5% FX markup.
- Rewards: 3 Delight Points/₹100; issuer FAQ states 0.25 paisa (₹0.0025) per point, with 4,000 point cashback and 1,000 point services minimums. Points expire after three years. The listed conversion is unusually low and should be checked against the live redemption flow.
- Welcome/milestones: 500 points on activation, 750 on first ₹1,500+ transaction within 30 days, 1,500 at ₹2 lakh net annual spend, 2,000 at ₹4 lakh.
- Benefits: one Visa lounge visit each calendar quarter; 1% fuel surcharge waiver for ₹400–₹5,000 transactions, capped ₹500/month; ₹25 lakh air accident cover, subject to activity and claim conditions; zero lost-card liability after immediate report; Visa Signature concierge services.
- Eligibility: resident Indian, age 21–60 salaried and 21–65 self-employed; add-on age 18+.
