## Corrections made

- Cleared launch date and network/tier because reviewed current issuer sources do not establish the original launch date or current issued network/tier. The current product page has generic Mastercard FAQ text, which does not establish this card's network variant.
- Current page confirms invitation-only application, joining and annual fees INR12,499 plus GST, annual waiver after spend over INR1,000,000 (from year two), 2% forex markup and 3.75% monthly finance. Cash advance is 2.5%, minimum INR500 per current MITC. Effective-from dates are left null because issuer material does not give them for these general fee terms.
- Card-specific T&C: 6 Reward Points per INR200 eligible retail spend. Excludes government services, tax, rent, property management, wallet loads and fuel (MCC lists encoded in record); EMI points reverse. Caps: grocery/utilities/education 1,000 points per category/statement cycle; insurance 5,000 per statement cycle on the current product page. The linked card T&C's MCC appendix contains lower 1,000-point insurance wording, so the insurance cap conflict is noted in reward notes.
- Removed the former generic INR1 point valuation and realized INR1 value. Current card terms set INR1 per point for flights/hotel and named voucher redemptions, INR0.60 for other catalogue goods, and INR0.40 for statement credit (capped at 50,000 points/statement cycle). Removed an Air India transfer value pending current card-eligible official transfer evidence.
- Current iShop FAQ says card reward multipliers vary by card and offer, with general maxima of 6X flights and 12X hotels. Its linked current cap PDF confirms this card's iShop maximum 18,000 points/month and 10,000/day. Public card-specific multipliers were not available in a stable schedule, so those prior 6X/12X rules were removed pending confirmation.
- Current card page and card-specific T&C confirm unlimited domestic and international lounge access. Priority Pass is required internationally; add-on cardholders are covered. Guests pay USD29 plus GST per visit. Domestic lounge provider is not specified in reviewed current card terms.
- Unlimited golf is for primary cardmembers. Monthly rounds are credited after the 10th of the following month and available for two months; booking is subject to course/provider availability.
- Joining/annual benefits: 12,500 points and one-year Taj Epicure Plus and 12-month EazyDiner Prime memberships. Points are credited within 45 days after fee realization and activation; FAQ says membership details are emailed within 45 business days. Renewal benefits are subject to annual fee receipt. INR800,000 annual spend yields two EaseMyTrip flight vouchers worth INR3,000 each, with first at INR400,000 and second at INR800,000.
- Fuel waiver: 1%, transactions up to INR4,000, total cap INR1,000 per statement cycle in card-specific T&C. Current terms do not state a minimum transaction amount.
- Air accident cover: INR30,000,000. Card FAQ says primary cardholder only, card used within prior 60 days, international trip ticket purchased with card. Current group insurance policy table supports INR3 crore and primary-cardholder-only coverage, with a general active-card criterion of one transaction in the past 90 days; timing difference is documented. The previous INR5,000,000 travel-medical field was wrong: policy cover is INR50,000 for bodily injury arising from rail/road accident.
- Current card page states age 21–65 and invitation-only access; no income or credit-score threshold is published.
- Added card cancellation cover and current BookMyShow max-offer text to the benefit notes. Current product page says no cancellation charges up to INR12,000; card T&C limits coverage to eligible domestic non-refundable bookings and first two cancellations. BookMyShow offers up to INR750 off a second ticket twice monthly on eligible bookings.
