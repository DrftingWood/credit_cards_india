# LIC Axis Bank Platinum issuer evidence extract (2026-10-26)

## Current product-page claims

Source: [Axis LIC Platinum product page](https://www.axis.bank.in/cards/credit-card/lic-axis-bank-platinum-credit-card), retrieved 2026-10-26.

- Joining and annual fees are nil; monthly finance charge is 3.75%; cash withdrawal is 2.5%, minimum INR 500; foreign-currency markup is 3.5%.
- Base rewards are 1 LIC Reward Point per INR 100 on eligible transactions. LIC premium payments through official LIC gateways and foreign-currency transactions earn 2 points per INR 100. Third-party LIC payment applications do not receive the premium accelerator.
- Fuel, wallet load and EMI conversions do not earn points. Broader exclusions are listed in the linked reward terms and 2025 exclusion notice.
- Fuel surcharge waiver is 1% on transactions from INR 400 to INR 4,000, up to INR 400 monthly; GST on the surcharge is not refunded. Fuel purchases earn no points.
- Lost-card liability cover is described as up to the card's credit limit; the product page gives no separate numeric limit or full policy terms.
- Purchases of INR 1,500 or more may be converted to EMI.
- Eligibility: primary applicant age 18–70 and resident in India. The issuer does not publish a card-specific income or bureau-score threshold on this page.
- The page's fee table also lists DCC 3.5%, reward redemption fee INR 30, rent fee 1%, education-app fee 1%, and conditional utility, wallet, fuel and gaming fees. These do not all have dedicated schema fields.

## Reward catalogue and exclusions

Sources: current [LIC reward catalogue and terms](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/cards/lic-rewards-points-terms-and-conditions.pdf?sfvrsn=4e614ab9_22), retrieved 2026-10-26; [LIC reward exclusion revision effective 2025-06-20](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/terms-and-conditions-lic-axis-bank-platinum-credit-card.pdf).

- Redeem by calling Axis customer care against catalogue items. Each request costs INR 30 plus GST.
- E-vouchers are sent within 10 working days; physical products within 21 working days.
- Points expire 12 months after accumulation. The catalogue caps virtual gift cards at 75 per financial year.
- Redemption value is product-specific. The catalogue gives differing point prices for vouchers of the same rupee denomination; no universal rupee-per-point value is asserted.
- The June 2025 notice says exclusions are identified by spend category rather than four-digit MCC. Categories include utilities/telecom, education, rent, wallet loads, government services, gold/jewellery, fuel, cash advances and repayments. Current catalogue/update terms also name gift cards and bridge, road and toll fees.

## August 2026 issuer-wide terms update

Source: Axis issuer terms update effective 2026-08-28, archived as `axis-credit-card-terms-update-effective-2026-08-28.pdf`.

- DCC markup increased from 1.5% to 3.5%.
- Late fee for total payment due of INR 50,000 or more is INR 1,300.
- Gift-card and bridge/road/toll spend are excluded from rewards, cashback, fee waivers and milestone benefits as stated in the update.

## Unresolved or unpublished

- Current network and network tier are not specified on the product page; both remain null. “Platinum” is the product name, not evidence for a network tier.
- Axis issuance policy material identifies a 2011 LIC co-brand launch year, but the exact card launch date is not established; `launched_on` remains null.
- No fixed rupee value for a point, separate lost-card coverage amount, income threshold, bureau-score threshold or accelerator cap is published in the checked card-specific sources.
- The project-level `entry` tier is a classification, not an issuer claim.
