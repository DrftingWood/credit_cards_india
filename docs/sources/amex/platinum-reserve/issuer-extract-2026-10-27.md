## Reconciled terms

- Product page lists INR 10,000 first-year and renewal fees and a full renewal-fee waiver at INR 10 lakh spend in the immediately preceding membership year. Issuer schedule updated 31-Aug-2025 lists 3.5% monthly finance charge, 3.5% forex, 3.5% cash-advance fee with INR 250 minimum, and delinquency charge at 30% of MAD (minimum INR 500, maximum INR 1,000). A separate Reserve Option 1 has a INR 50,000 lifetime foundation fee plus INR 10,000 annual maintenance fee; the data record describes Option 2. The issuer does not state the effective start date of the current fees; the schema requires a date, so the card record uses the 27-Oct-2026 evidence snapshot date and states this limitation.
- Product page confirms 1 Membership Rewards point per INR 50, excluding fuel, insurance, utilities, cash transactions and merchant/POS EMI. Its footnote confirms SafeKey and post-purchase EMI continue earning. The previous blanket EMI and wallet-load exclusions were removed. Merchant-specific Reward Multiplier and RewardXcelerator offers are dynamic and are not represented as generic category earn.
- Current product page confirms an 11,000-point welcome gift after annual-fee payment and INR 30,000 net spend within 90 days; a monthly INR 1,000 voucher after enrollment and INR 50,000 calendar-month spend (up to INR 12,000/year); and the INR 10 lakh renewal-fee waiver.
- Lounge terms: 12 domestic visits per calendar year, at most 3 per calendar quarter; 2 complimentary international Priority Pass visits per calendar year, then currently USD 35/person/visit. The basic member must enroll. The domestic Centurion Lounges in Delhi and Mumbai have been excluded since 1-Sep-2023.
- Golf allows up to 2 complimentary visits per month at 30+ courses after INR 50,000 spend two calendar months prior. HPCL convenience fee is 0% below INR 5,000 and 1% at/above. EazyDiner discount varies from 25% to 50%. These remain qualified by terms and availability rather than flat guaranteed value.
- Issuer continues to list air-accident cover up to INR 1 crore subject to card-purchased eligible air ticket and purchase protection; policy terms and limits constrain the benefit. Current eligibility is resident Indian, over 18, income at least INR 6 lakh salaried or self-employed, Indian bank account, good credit history, serviceable-city residence, and more than 12 months trading if self-employed. No score floor is published.
- Amex's 15-Nov-2026 update changes milestone assessment to transaction posting date and credits vouchers/bonus points by month-end; monthly voucher enrollment will be temporarily unavailable 11-16 Nov. New applications in India are temporarily paused.
