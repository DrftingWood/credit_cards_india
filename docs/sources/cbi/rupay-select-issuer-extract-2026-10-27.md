# Central Bank of India RuPay Select Credit Card — issuer evidence extract (27 Oct 2026)

Compared CBI's current [Credit Cards page](https://centralbank.bank.in/en/credit-cards), the official [FY2019-20 annual report](https://centralbank.bank.in/pdf/Annual_Report_2019_20_%28English%29.pdf), current [credit/debit application forms directory](https://centralbank.bank.in/en/node/929), and the linked [Central Platinum Card form](https://centralbank.bank.in/pdf/central_platinum_card.pdf).

## Product availability and scope

The current CBI credit-card page does not list a standalone RuPay Select credit card or publish its terms. The annual report states standalone credit-card issuance was discontinued as co-branded issuance began in October 2019. The application-form directory lists Central Card, Classic, Gold, and Platinum forms; the Platinum form is specifically Visa Platinum. Neither the form nor CBI's RuPay Select debit-card pages establish terms for this legacy RuPay Select credit-card record. The record is set to `on-hold`; no product-specific discontinuation date or existing-card terms were found.
