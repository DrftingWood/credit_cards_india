# YES Bank YES First Preferred: issuer evidence extract

Reviewed: 2026-10-26  
Product page: <https://www.yes.bank.in/personal-banking/yes-first/cards/credit-card/yes-first-preferred-credit-card>

The product detail page is rendered with JavaScript and its static download presents a shell rather than the content. This note preserves the visible text and terms from the browser-rendered page and its glossary. The current consolidated MITC v18 and the June-2026 charges/features notice are archived alongside this extract. The older official value chart is indexed by YES Bank and cited below, but direct download returned the issuer's HTML shell; it is not treated as a local PDF.

## Current product page

- The issuer says issuance of YES First Preferred to new customers has been discontinued. For existing cardholders, a new card will be issued on expiry.
- The feature header advertises golf green-fee waiver, 8 Reward Points per INR 200, 25% BookMyShow discount, and Instant EMI.
- The current reward detail states 4 points per INR 200 on select categories and 8 points per INR 200 on all categories except select categories.
- Fees shown are INR 999 plus applicable taxes for first year and renewal. The first-year fee is waived after INR 50,000 retail spend within three months of setup; renewal is waived after INR 250,000 retail spend in the 12 months before renewal.
- Standard interest is 3.99% monthly (47.88% annually); the page separately states 2.49% monthly for a Fixed Deposit variant.
- Current select MCCs effective 15-Jun-2026: 4121 (taxicabs/limousines), 4131 (bus lines), 4900 (utilities), 5411 (grocery stores), 5499 (miscellaneous food stores). The page states a 300-point per-statement cap for utility MCC 4900.
- The page lists no reward points on rent, wallet, fuel, cash withdrawals, post-purchase/instant EMI, government, marketing/advertising, insurance, education, railways, toll, ferry and jewellery. Its indicative MCC list includes the current codes represented in the YAML.
- The product page confirms a 25% BookMyShow discount, but does not state a use count or discount cap in the current rendered section.
- The current product page does not establish a single network, network tier, exact launch date or card-specific age/income/score/residency thresholds.

## Current consolidated MITC v18 (05-Oct-2026)

Archived as `yesbank-mitc-v18-current-2026-10-26.pdf`; copied from the independently downloaded, signature-checked YES Bank MITC used in the FinBooster review. The SHA-256-verified current copy and the dated 05-Oct copy were identical in that review.

- Preferred family joining and annual membership fees: INR 999 each, excluding GST.
- Joining-fee waiver: INR 50,000 spend within three months of setup. Renewal-fee waiver: INR 250,000 in the preceding 12 months.
- Standard finance: 3.99% monthly / 47.88% annually. FD and NRI variants have different rates, so those are not generalized to the standard record.
- Cash advance: 2.5% or INR 650 minimum, effective 15-Jun-2026.
- Foreign-currency conversion markup: 2.00% effective 15-Jun-2026 for YES First Preferred. DCC is a separate 1% fee plus applicable GST.
- Domestic lounge access requires at least INR 75,000 eligible spend in the prior quarter. The threshold only applies to domestic lounges; international lounge access has no spend gate under the MITC.
- Reward redemption costs INR 100 plus GST per request. Flight redemption adds INR 99 plus GST. General YES Rewardz points expire three years after earning.
- Fuel surcharge waiver: 1% on INR 500-INR 5,000 transactions, capped at INR 250 per statement cycle. UPI fuel transactions are excluded, and GST on the surcharge is not waived.
- The network can change the MCC classifications; the MITC describes the MCC list as indicative and subject to revision.

## Additional benefits and older source caveats

The current product page's rendered "More from this card" detail and card feature content state two domestic lounge visits per quarter, four international visits per year, one golf lesson per calendar month, and four additional green-fee-waiver sessions at selected golf courses. The issuer does not identify the period for the four additional golf sessions. The international visit count is subject to third-party lounge-program conditions.

YES Bank's official [First Preferred value chart](https://www.yes.bank.in/content/published/api/v1.1/assets/CONT245A99A043C042B0B77BE647A6C987DD/native/value_chart_for_yes_first_preferred_credit_card.pdf?download=false) reports a maximum BookMyShow discount of INR 250 per month, two domestic visits per quarter, and four international visits per year. The chart is older than the current product page; the card record retains the INR 250 movie cap as a low-confidence legacy term and states that current cap/usage conditions need confirmation.

An older official legacy card page says accumulated Reward Points do not expire. Current MITC v18 states that eligible YES BANK credit-card points expire after three years. The YAML follows the current MITC and records this conflict.
