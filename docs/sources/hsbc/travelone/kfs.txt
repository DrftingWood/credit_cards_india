1.    Late Payment Fee
      The late payment fee is equivalent to 100% of the Minimum Payment Due (MPD). This fee is imposed only if the MPD is not settled within three days following the due date.
2.    Bill Statement
      The bank will dispatch your statement via your preferred delivery method (Email/Physical) on your designated statement date each month. You may also access it through Mobile
      or Internet Banking if it is not received by the statement date. Should your payment due date coincide with a Sunday or public holiday, interest will be calculated to include that day
      and debited on the subsequent working day. To alter your billing cycle, please contact HSBC through PhoneBanking, visit an HSBC Branch, use the HSBC Mobile Banking App, or
      access Internet Banking under the “Support - Chat with us” and Secured Message options.
3.    Reporting Billing Discrepancies
      Cardholders may report billing discrepancies by contacting the customer service centre, with numbers provided on the reverse of the monthly statement or at www.hsbc.bank.in, or
      by writing to: HSBC Credit Card Division, Dispute Desk, P. O. Box 5080, Chennai - 600 028. All statement contents are considered correct and accepted unless the customer notifies
      us of any discrepancies within 30 days from the statement date.
4.    Minimum Payment Due
      To avoid declined transactions and the imposition of a late payment fee, you must pay at least the minimum payment due (though you may opt to pay more) by the due date specified
      on your monthly card statement. Non-payment may adversely affect:
      •    Your credit rating, as reported to Credit Information Companies
      •    Other credit facilities obtained from HSBC
      •    Additionally, the Bank may cancel the credit limit and initiate recovery proceedings to reclaim the dues
5.    Consequences of Minimum Payments
      Consistently making only the minimum payment each month can extend repayment over months or years, resulting in compounded interest on your outstanding balance. The
      Minimum Payment Due (MPD) is calculated as the higher of ₹100 or the sum of:
      •    100% of all interest, fees, and taxes billed in the current statement
      •    100% of Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) amounts billed in the current statement (if applicable)
      •    The higher of past due amounts or any over-limit amount (past due refers to unpaid MPD from the previous cycle)
      •    1% of the billed statement balance (excluding any EMI balance, fees, interest, and taxes billed)
6.    Contactless Payments
      All cards now support contactless payments, where a PIN is not required at Point of Sale (POS) terminals, up to the pre-defined limits set on your card.
7.    GST and Applicable Taxes
      Any charges mentioned in this Schedule of Fees and Charges are exclusive of GST or any other applicable taxes, which are billed alongside the fee on the billing statement and
      levied as per the applicable GST or other taxes.
8.    Payment Methods
      Payments can be made via Cheque/Draft, NACH, Cash, Standing Instruction, NEFT, Internet Banking, and PayU.
9.    Lost or Stolen Credit Card
      In the event of a lost or stolen credit card, the cardholder should immediately report it to HSBC in writing or by contacting HSBC PhoneBanking Officers at 1800 2673456 or 1800
      121 2208, or by sending an SMS with 'BLOCK' to 575750.
10. Credit Limit
      The credit limit represents the maximum amount the Card Member is authorised to spend on their Credit Card, subject to the Bank’s policy and Terms and Conditions. The credit
      limit and cash withdrawal limit [up to 20% (60% only on HSBC Premier Credit Card) of the credit limit or as determined by the Bank from time to time] are communicated in your
      monthly card statement. The available credit limit is included in the monthly statement. The Bank reserves the right to reduce the credit limit. Utilisation of the card implies
      acceptance of the credit limits granted from time to time. The approved credit limit is shared between the primary Cardholder and any add-on Cardholders.
11. Interest-Free (Grace) Period
      The interest-free credit period ranges from 48 days (for Visa Platinum/RuPay Platinum/Premier/Taj Co-brand Cards) to 45 days (for Live+/RuPay Cashback/Travel One Cards). For
      example, a customer with a billing date of the 5th of the month can spend on their card from 6 May to 5 June, with the bill generated on 5 June and the Payment Due Date on 21
      June for Live+/RuPay Cashback/Travel One) and 24 June for Visa Platinum/RuPay Platinum/Premier/Taj Co-brand Cards. This is applicable provided the credit card outstanding, as
      shown on the monthly credit card statement, is fully settled within three days (Grace Period) of the payment due date. The interest-free credit period is not applicable for cash
      advance transactions. Please note that the 'interest-free credit period' is suspended if any balance from the previous month's bill remains outstanding.
