# Axis Bank Magnus Credit Card — issuer review (2026-10-26)

Reviewed the live Magnus product page and its Travel & Stay, More Benefits and Health & Wellness pages; current Magnus-specific terms effective 28-Aug-2026; current MITC; and Axis's live EDGE transfer-program terms. Issuer documents/PDFs are archived beside this extract.

Axis's 18-Sep-2019 launch announcement supports the launch date. Magnus terms effective 1-Sep-2023 state that the annual fee for customers onboarded from that date changed from INR10,000 to INR12,500 and set the new-cohort waiver at INR25 lakh. Older customer cohorts had transition rules, so the current standard acquisition price should not be read as a statement-specific fee guarantee for every legacy account. A separate LTF offer for eligible Burgundy Salary customers started in July 2026 and has its own terms; the data does not model that acquisition construct separately.

## Current record checks

- **Product and charges:** active Magnus product; joining and annual fees INR12,500 plus taxes; INR25 lakh annual-fee waiver on eligible spends in the preceding card-anniversary year. Current page excludes rent, wallet, gift card, Priority Pass, EMI, government, utility/telecom, insurance, gold/jewellery and fuel from waiver spends. Current page lists 3% monthly finance charge, 2% foreign currency markup, no cash-withdrawal fee, 1% rent transaction fee (maximum INR1,500/transaction), and 1% fuel-surcharge waiver on INR400–4,000 fuel transactions capped at INR400/month. The 28-Aug-2026 terms set DCC markup to 2% plus taxes and reclassify gift-card purchases out of Wallet into a separate category; gift cards remain reward-ineligible and do not count for waiver spends.
- **Rewards:** base 12 EDGE points/INR200 through INR1.5 lakh cumulative eligible calendar-month spend. The 35-point band applies above that threshold up to eligible spends equal to assigned credit limit plus INR1.5 lakh, then 12 points/INR200. Travel EDGE earns 60 points/INR200 through INR2 lakh/month, then 35 points/INR200 above that limit. Rent can earn rewards only up to INR50,000 cumulative monthly spend; excess rent is ineligible. Excluded categories include wallet, gift card, government, utilities/telecom, bridge/road fees/tolls, insurance, fuel, gold/jewellery, cash advances and repayments. Fees/charges do not count toward the INR1.5 lakh accelerator threshold. EMI-converted purchase points are debited and not reinstated if EMI is cancelled.
- **Reward value and transfers:** no fixed INR value is asserted; value varies by redemption. The live card page advertises 20 travel partners and a 2:5 points-to-miles headline, while the live transfer table lists partner-specific ratios. The current partner matrix is captured in the data record. The 5 lakh annual transfer ceiling remains, split between Group A (1 lakh) and Group B (4 lakh).
- **Airport access:** domestic access remains unlimited subject to INR50,000 spend in the previous three calendar months, waived for issue month plus the next three months. Effective 28-Aug-2026, Priority Pass cannot be used at domestic Indian lounges; present the Magnus card. International access is unlimited via Priority Pass with up to four additional guest visits/year; the guest must travel with the primary cardholder.
- **Welcome and insurance:** INR12,500 choice of Luxe, Postcard Hotels or Yatra voucher, for the primary cardholder after a purchase within 30 days and redemption within six months; page says welcome benefit applies only when joining fee is paid. Current issuer benefits list purchase protection INR2 lakh, baggage loss/delay and personal documents USD500, and credit shield INR5 lakh. Air accident insurance of INR2.5 crore was unsupported and removed. Golf and a INR25,000 EDGE Miles milestone were not listed in current Magnus benefit pages and were removed.
- **Eligibility:** indicative primary-cardholder age 18–70, resident of India, and net salary INR24 lakh/year or net annual ITR filed for INR24 lakh; issuer retains discretion to approve/decline. No credit-score cutoff is stated. Launch date is 18-Sep-2019 per the archived Axis launch announcement.

## Effective-date note

The 28-Aug-2026 Magnus terms also announce that complimentary memberships linked to the card will renew only with explicit consent effective 15-Nov-2026. This is a future effective term as of this review date. The same change note describes the domestic-lounge presentation method and DCC/gift-card revisions effective 28-Aug-2026.

## Open source limitations

The Axis EDGE transfer page is dynamic and its partner matrix is not fully exposed in the browser text view; it was extracted from the issuer page source and transcribed into the record. The card page's broad 2:5 transfer headline differs from the detailed matrix's partner-specific 5:1 and 5:2 ratios; the matrix was used for individual partner rows. The Magnus page also advertises an LTF Burgundy Salary offer introduced from July 2026, with separate terms and no welcome benefit, but that acquisition construct is not a separate product record in the dataset.
