## Current terms and corrections

- No joining fee; ₹250 AMC from year two, waived when previous-year usage exceeds ₹50,000; 3% foreign currency markup; 1.66% monthly purchase interest; cash advance fee ₹50 and cash advance interest 2.25% monthly.
- Issuer feature sheet says 1 point valued at ₹1 per ₹100. Exact exclusions and redemption process were not verified; the legacy excluded-category list was removed.
- Removed unsupported fuel waiver and cap. Current MITC lists a petrol surcharge, not a RuPay Select waiver.
- Older product sheet states airport lounge access but no current quota; no current credit-card-specific entitlement was found. RuPay insurance document located expired 31 March 2026; no current cover claimed.
- Launch date, income/credit criteria and precise current lounge/insurance terms remain unresolved.
