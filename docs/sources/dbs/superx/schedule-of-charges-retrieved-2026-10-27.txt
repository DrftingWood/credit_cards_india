(Not applicable for DBS Vantage Card) 
1% of transaction amount or Rs. 199, whichever is higher on

applications/websites like (but not limited to) CRED, PayTM, Cheq, 
Mobikwik. For education payments made through college/school 
websites or their Point of Sale (POS) machines, there will be no 
additional fees applicable on such transactions. International 
education payments are excluded from this charge. Applicable for 
Education Merchant Category Codes– 8211, 8220, 8241, 8244, 
8249, 8299 
Fee on Wallet Load transactions 
(Not applicable for DBS Vantage Card) 
Effective 1st April 2026, a fee of 1% of transaction on transactions 
of ₹5,000 or more will be applicable on wallet-load transactions. 
Applicable for Merchant Category Code – 6540 
 
Fee on Travel Transactions (Applicable 
MCCs: 4111, 4131, 4784) 
(Not applicable for DBS Vantage Card) 
Effective 1st April 2026, a fee of 1% of total spends if cumulative 
spends exceed ₹10,000 in a statement cycle will be applicable on 
select travel transactions.  
Applicable for Travel Merchant Category Codes: 4111 - Local and 
suburban commuter passenger transportation, including ferries, 
4131 - Bus lines, 4784 - Tolls and bridge fees.
