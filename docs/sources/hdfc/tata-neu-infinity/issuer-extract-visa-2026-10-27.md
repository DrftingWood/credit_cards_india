## Field decisions and corrections

- The issuer launch release dates the launch to 24-Aug-2022 and confirms Visa and RuPay variants. This is the Visa variant. No issuer evidence inspected here establishes a Visa Signature tier; `network_tier` is null.
- The local Visa image path had pointed to `/cards/hdfc/tata-neu-infinity.webp`, which is byte-identical to `/cards/hdfc/tata-neu-infinity-rupay.webp` (SHA-256 `d1970ec0efac7158818292d33877ad6cb13568170c010f0a0d00c2202ff16fbd`). The shared artwork displays RuPay SELECT branding. Cleared the Visa image path pending a verified Visa asset.
- Current standard fees are INR 1,499 joining and annual, with renewal waiver at INR 300,000 annual spend. Current Personal MITC v4.15 lists 2.5% foreign-currency markup, 3.75% monthly finance charge, and a cash advance charge of 2.5% / INR 500 minimum. The card-specific FAQ states 2% FCY markup; the newer MITC value is modeled and the conflict remains in the YAML notes.
- NeuCoins are closed-loop points redeemable at INR 1 on eligible Tata Neu/Tata partner purchases, not cash. The current statement-cycle reward ceiling is 50,000 NeuCoins. Eligible non-EMI spend on Tata Neu and partner Tata brands earns 5% total (1.5% base + 3.5% additional); it does not require NeuPass. HDFC terms require eligible merchant MID/TID recognition and apply separate merchant monthly/annual spend ceilings. Because the calculator does not model these merchant-level ceilings, the 5% rate is recorded but excluded from calculator scoring.
- Historical reward rule corrected: removed a false Tata Neu app-only channel and online-category restriction, and removed a duplicate app-only 1.5% rule. Added eligible merchants and Annex 1 spend-ceiling detail from the 14-Aug-2024 terms. HDFC says Titan/Tanishq were added 1-Jan-2023, Qmin 1-Mar-2024, and Tata Pay's monthly ceiling increased 1-Aug-2024. These merchant additions are not all launch-date claims.
- A separate additional 5% NeuPass benefit applies only to selected Tata Neu app/website categories, requires enrollment and primary-cardholder mobile matching since 15-Jan-2025, and varies by category. It is documented separately and not scored as a general earn rate.
- HDFC current eligibility FAQ supports ages 21?60 salaried and 21?65 self-employed, gross monthly salary greater than INR 100,000, self-employed ITR above INR 1,200,000, and Indian-resident eligibility. No minimum credit score is published.
- Domestic lounge: since 10-Jun-2025, spend INR 50,000 in a calendar quarter unlocks two vouchers (up to eight/year); claim within 120 days, use within 180 days. Visa international lounge: four visits outside India per calendar year, one per quarter; Priority Pass is issued on request. Primary/add-on members share the complimentary quota. India visits, guests, and excess visits cost USD 27 plus GST.
- Welcome benefit: 1,499 NeuCoins for a first transaction within 30 days, unavailable on lifetime-free/first-year-free cards; card-specific terms say claim within 60 days, while the product page separately says eligible users can claim up to 180 days from the statement date.
- Current fuel waiver is 1% on fuel transactions from INR 400 to INR 5,000, capped at INR 500 per statement cycle. The FAQ shows INR 250 while terms updated 14-Aug-2024 show INR 500. The transition date is not established; the pre-14-Aug-2024 historical value is null and the later historical value is INR 500.
- Product page lists insurance of INR 1 crore air accident, INR 15 lakh overseas emergency medical, and INR 9 lakh lost-card cover. Policy wording is limited to the primary cardholder and gives activation/travel/claim conditions. The linked policy says its period runs through 22-Jul-2027 but separately says benefits are valid only through 22-Jul-2026; this source conflict remains documented.
- HDFC announces a future change from 25-Nov-2026: card-earned NeuCoins must be claimed manually within 12 months; once claimed into Tata Neu they remain valid a further 12 months. This future rule is noted and not applied to current behavior.
