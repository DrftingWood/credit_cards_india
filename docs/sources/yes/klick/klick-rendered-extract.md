# YES Bank Klick: issuer evidence extract

Reviewed: 2026-10-26  
Current issuer page: <https://www.yes.bank.in/personal-banking/yes-individual/cards/credit-cards/klick-credit-card>

The issuer page is JavaScript-rendered. Its visible FAQ says to apply through the Kiwi app and states existing YES BANK credit-card holders are ineligible. The current YES Bank credit-card listing continues to list Klick for application. The issuer links the Klick RuPay user guide archived here. Both archived PDFs were checked for a `%PDF-` signature.

## Product and rewards

- Klick is a virtual-only RuPay card; no physical card is issued.
- The current issuer guide states 2 YES Rewardz points per INR 200 on non-UPI transactions. It does not give a UPI reward rate. RuPay UPI use is restricted to merchant payments; peer payments are not permitted.
- Lifetime free: nil joining and renewal fees.
- Current issuer page terms list reward exclusions: rent (MCC 6513), wallet loads (6540), fuel (5172, 5542, 5540, 5983), cash withdrawals/advance (6011), government-service MCCs (9034, 9211, 9222, 9223, 9311, 9399, 9402, 9405, 9700, 9701, 9702, 9751, 9754, 9950), and marketing/advertising MCCs (5960, 5962, 5964, 5965, 5966, 5967, 5968, 5969, 7311). Effective 15-Jun-2026 the issuer also lists insurance (6300, 6381), education (8211, 8220, 8249, 8299, 8241, 8244, 7911), railways (4112, 4011), toll (4784), ferry (4111), and jewellery (5094, 5944). Utility MCC 4900 reward points are capped at 150 per statement cycle.
- Current consolidated MITC v18 states YES Rewardz points expire three years after earning and charges INR 100 plus GST per redemption request. The point value in the record is a maximum catalog valuation, not a realized cash value.

## Current consolidated MITC v18 (05-Oct-2026)

- Joining and annual fees for virtual Klick: NIL.
- Forex markup: 3.50% from 15-Jun-2026.
- Revolving finance: 3.99% monthly.
- Cash-advance fee is not applicable to any virtual credit card. The MITC does not establish that cash advances are available on Klick.
- Fuel surcharge waiver: 1% on INR 500-INR 3,000 fuel transactions, capped at INR 250 per statement cycle. UPI fuel transactions do not receive the waiver. GST on the surcharge is not waived.

## Remaining unknowns

Exact launch date, network tier, age/income/bureau/residency thresholds, and cash-advance availability were not established in the reviewed issuer evidence. No lounge or concierge entitlement is asserted. A separately indexed Klick guide mentions 4 points on certain UPI transactions above INR 2,000; this differs from the current product-page headline and the linked current Klick guide, so no UPI earn is encoded pending issuer clarification.
