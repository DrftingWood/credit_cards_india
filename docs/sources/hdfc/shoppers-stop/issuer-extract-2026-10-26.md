## Current terms encoded

- Launched March 30, 2022. Current product page supports RuPay linkage to UPI; it does not identify a RuPay tier.
- Joining and renewal fees ₹299 + tax with no waiver on current pricing. HDFC's fee table lists no waiver for the card. Foreign currency markup is 3.5%, monthly finance charge 3.75%, cash advance fee 2.5% (minimum ₹500). Product page discloses 1% international DCC markup and 1% rent fee from the second rent transaction in a calendar month.
- Rewards: 3% on eligible Shoppers Stop transactions (500 point monthly cap) and 1% on eligible non-Shopper transactions (1,000 point monthly cap); minimum eligible transaction ₹150. Shoppers Stop qualification depends on registered merchant TID/MID. Rewards expire after two years.
- Redemption rates: ₹1/point at Shoppers Stop, ₹0.20 statement credit, ₹0.25 catalogue and airmiles, ₹0.20 SmartBuy flights/hotels. Cashback redemption fee is ₹50 under the current MITC; reward/catalogue handling is ₹99 per redemption request. SmartBuy flights/hotels may be paid up to 50% in points.
- Current exclusions include wallet, rent, government, fuel, supermarkets, tax, EMI, miscellaneous vending food, package stores/liquor, bakeries, cash advances/travellers' cheques and insurance; jewellery is explicitly excluded from the non-Shopper category. Remaining exclusions are listed in the YAML source notes.
- Welcome offer is a ₹500 Shoppers Stop voucher, welcome message within 3 working days after onboarding, and complimentary Silver Edge membership valued at ₹350.
- Weekend reward: 500 points on a single ₹15,000+ Shoppers Stop purchase on Friday, Saturday or Sunday; once/month and five awards/year.
- Fuel surcharge waiver is 1% on eligible ₹400–₹5,000 transactions, capped at ₹250 per statement. No lounge access is published for this card.
- Eligibility: Indian nationality; salaried age 21–60 and monthly income ₹20,000; self-employed age 21–65 and annual ITR strictly greater than ₹6,00,000.

## Evidence notes

The product page and card-specific terms agree on the main reward earn schedule, category caps, welcome voucher, weekend offer and two-year expiry. The general product eligibility page is the card-level source for ages and income. The card is RuPay according to current product page UPI-linking guidance; a tier is not published.
