## Corrections made

- Corrected launch date from 1 Oct to 15 Oct 2026 using SBI Card's official filing.
- Cleared the single-network RuPay and Platinum-tier assertions. Current card terms and product page say the card is issued on RuPay and Mastercard; no card network tier is stated.
- Kept the current ₹499 joining/renewal fees, ₹2 lakh annual fee-reversal threshold, 3.5% forex markup, standard SBI Card finance/cash-advance fees and zero add-on fee, with current sources documented. Flagged the May-2026 KFS's conflicting ₹1 lakh waiver threshold because the current product page and MITC both say ₹2 lakh.
- Split cashback from instant checkout discounts. Cashback is 1% on other eligible spends (₹500/month cap), up to 5% at Apollo, ixigo, Uber and Urban Company (₹1,000/month cap), and posts to the SBI Card account within two working days of statement generation. On eligible ixigo flight/hotel bookings with `IXIGOSBI`, 4% is an instant discount and 1% is cashback.
- Removed the former 10% and 5% Eternal-app offers from cashback accelerators. Current issuer terms specify 10% instant discount on Zomato, District and Blinkit beauty/electronics and 5% on other eligible Blinkit categories, with a combined ₹2,000 monthly cap. These discounts are not statement cashback and qualifying instant-discount purchases do not earn cashback.
- Current card-specific T&C and product page omit Bistro from the cashback table, although the 15 Oct launch release mentions Bistro. Kept Bistro out of current qualifying rewards pending current card-specific terms that include it.
- Corrected the fuel-surcharge waiver cap from ₹100 to ₹200 per statement cycle, retaining the ₹500–₹4,000 transaction band and 1% rate advertised by SBI Card.
- Replaced the incomplete exclusion/MCC data with the card booklet's full excluded category list and MCC list. Kept welcome membership but removed unsupported ₹999 value and 30-day window; left concierge unknown because SBI does not advertise it as a card feature.
- Retained the current card-specific statement cashback auto-credit behavior; did not treat checkout discounts as cashback. Kept eligibility income and minimum credit score unknown.

## Review limits

The card-specific booklet and product page establish the current advertised terms; merchant qualification still depends on issuer/network merchant identification and applicable offer conditions. The online product page and booklet currently differ on whether Bistro participates: the launch release mentions Bistro but the current page and card-specific cashback table do not. The database follows the current card-specific terms and records this conflict.
