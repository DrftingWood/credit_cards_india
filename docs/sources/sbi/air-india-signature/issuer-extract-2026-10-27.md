# Air India SBI Signature: issuer comparison (27 Oct 2026)

## Sources reviewed

- [SBI customer notices](https://www.sbicard.com/en/customer-notices.page.page): new Air India SBI Signature issuance discontinued effective 10-Oct-2024; Air India earn reduced from 30 to 10 Reward Points/INR100 effective 31-Mar-2025; finance charges became 3.75% monthly from 01-Nov-2024.
- [SBI Signature combined card terms](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/Air-India-Signature-Combined-TC-16JULY2025.pdf): 4 Reward Points/INR100 eligible spend; current Air India bonus conditions, exclusions/MCCs, point validity, redemption minimum, loss cover and detailed reporting windows.
- [SBI Air India earn addendum](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/personal/credit-cards/travel/air-india-sbi-signature-tc.pdf): 10 points/INR100 on Air India base fare using primary/add-on cards via website, app, offices or call centres. 4 points post first; remaining 6 within 60 working days after merchant-ID mapping, with Maharaja Club membership/KYC and exact name match.
- [SBI milestone change letter](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/personal/credit-cards/travel/kyc-flyingreturns.pdf): revised milestone schedule effective 11-Apr-2016; thresholds INR5 lakh, INR10 lakh and INR20 lakh, with bonus awards of 20,000, 30,000 and 50,000 points respectively (cumulative totals 20,000/50,000/100,000). Annual spend runs from account opening.
- [SBI MITC](https://www.sbicard.com/en/most-important-terms-and-conditions.page): current joining, annual and renewal charge INR4,999; DCC 3.5%; unsecured monthly finance charge 3.75%; standard cash advance fee schedule applies.
- [SBI Priority Pass FAQ](https://www.sbicard.com/en/faq/complimentary-priority-pass-membership.page): Signature card is eligible for one complimentary membership worth USD99, valid 24 months. Other than SBI PRIME/ELITE quotas, all visits are chargeable; visits in India through Priority Pass are not complimentary and visits can cost up to USD27 plus tax.
- [SBI add-on-card FAQ](https://www.sbicard.com/en/faq/add-on-card.page): up to three add-on cards at no additional fee.
- [SBI current airport lounge page](https://www.sbicard.com/en/personal/benefits/airport-lounge-access.page) and [SBI notice dated 9-Dec-2025](https://www.sbicard.com/en/customer-notices.page.page): the 10-Jan-2026 domestic quota revision is selective and explicitly excludes Air India SBI Signature; SBI says remaining card quotas are unchanged.
- [Air India SBI co-brand page](https://www.airindia.com/in/en/maharaja-club/partner-offers/sbi.html): 1:1 SBI Reward Point to Maharaja Point conversion, 10,000-point minimum for Signature, posting within 7 days, converted-point expiry 24 months after credit; current points, thresholds, domestic lounge quota, annual fee, welcome and anniversary benefits.
- [SBI card-upgrade page](https://www.sbicard.com/en/personal/benefits/card-upgrade.page): still advertises 20 Reward Points/INR100 on Signature Air India spend, conflicting with SBI's later 31-Mar-2025 change notice and Air India's current 10-point terms; that outdated summary was not used for the accelerated rate.
- [SBI 2013 launch release](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/who-we-are/news/2013/SBI_Cards_launches_new_co-branded_credit_card_with_Air_India.pdf): exact launch date 16-Sep-2013.

## Record changes and caveats

- Marked new issuance discontinued from 10-Oct-2024 and removed the apply URL; existing card accounts retain their legacy terms.
- Confirmed the 2013 launch date and updated source dates. Preserved joining/annual/renewal fees of INR4,999; exact historical effective date for the current fee is not published.
- Corrected currency to SBI Reward Points, ordinary earn to 4/INR100, and Air India accelerated earn to 10/INR100. Added the staged 4+6 credit, base-fare limitation, member-number and name-match conditions; added evidenced exclusions and MCCs.
- Removed unsupported per-point INR values because SBI terms state points have no cash or monetary value. Retained the verified 1:1 transfer rate into Maharaja Points.
- Confirmed 20,000 welcome points; 20,000/30,000/50,000 milestone awards at INR5 lakh/10 lakh/20 lakh; 5,000 anniversary points. Removed synthetic INR values for point benefits.
- Kept eight annual domestic Visa lounge visits, max two per quarter: Signature is excluded from the 2026 quota revision, and the notice says other cards remain unchanged. Priority Pass membership is complimentary once, valid 24 months, but Signature gets no free visits.
- Added INR100,000 lost-card liability. SBI's detailed policy says three days before and seven days after reporting; Air India's current page says 48 hours before and seven days after, so the pre-report window conflict is recorded.
- Cleared the numeric fuel-surcharge waiver because the current SBI/partner sources reviewed do not establish card-specific applicability, transaction range or cap. Card-specific income and score cutoffs also remain unpublished.
- Removed the unsupported 15-day welcome posting window. Set the additional-card fee to unknown because the Signature agreement says the annual fee is at the prevailing rate while SBI's general add-on FAQ says no additional fee.
