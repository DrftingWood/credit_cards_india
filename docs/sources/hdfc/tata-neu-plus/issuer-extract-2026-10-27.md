# HDFC Tata Neu Plus issuer review (27 October 2026)

Reviewed HDFC's current [product page](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/credit-cards/tata-neu-plus-hdfc-bank-credit-card), [eligibility page](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/credit-cards/tata-neu-plus-hdfc-bank-credit-card/eligibility), card-specific terms (updated 24-Oct-2026), card FAQ (updated 24-Oct-2026), partner-brand fair-use schedule, NeuPass terms (updated 19-Sep-2026), personal MITC v4.15 (updated 17-Oct-2026), and the issuer's 24-Aug-2022 launch announcement. PDFs retrieved 27-Oct-2026 are retained beside prior versions.

## Current record values and evidence

- The launch release is dated 24-Aug-2022 and states that Plus launched on both Visa and RuPay. This file represents Visa; the separate RuPay record represents the UPI-capable variant. HDFC's launch release does not establish a Visa tier, so `network_tier` is null.
- Current product terms list joining and renewal fees of INR 499 plus tax, with renewal waived after INR 1 lakh annual spend before renewal. Personal MITC v4.15 supports the INR 1 lakh renewal threshold; HDFC product page supplies the card-specific fee.
- Personal MITC v4.15 gives 3.75% monthly finance charge for other variants and a 2.5% or INR 500 (whichever higher) cash-advance fee. Current FCC terms list 3.5% foreign-currency markup. The HDFC product page also lists the renewal fee and annual waiver.
- Card-specific T&Cs, updated 24-Oct-2026, call NeuCoins reward points. Non-EMI partner Tata-brand transactions earn 2% total (1% base plus 1% additional); other non-Tata transactions and merchant EMI earn 1%. The 2% partner reward depends on eligible merchant IDs and fair-usage ceilings by merchant/category, with monthly and annual ceilings in Annex 1. It is not a flat uncapped 2% on every Tata-related transaction.
- The card product page describes a separate additional 5% NeuPass/Tata Neu Rewards benefit on selected Tata Neu categories. Current NeuPass terms require the primary cardholder's registered number across the account and, for some categories, benefit activation before partner-app purchase. Eligible categories can change. The reward record keeps the bank-attributable rate at 1%/2%; the conditional program-side 5% is described but not counted as card-attributable reward.
- UPI rates are for RuPay only: 0.25% through any eligible UPI ID and an additional 0.75% on Tata Neu UPI ID transactions, with a shared 500 NeuCoin monthly UPI cap. They are not applied to this Visa record.
- Exclusions include fuel, wallet/gift/prepaid loads and vouchers, cash advances, payment of outstanding balances and card charges, Smart EMI/Dial-an-EMI, rent, government transactions, third-party-app education transactions, and online skill-based gaming MCC 5816 (from 1-Jul-2025). Direct school/college website or campus POS education payments remain eligible. Grocery is capped at 1,000/month; insurance, utility, and telecom/cable each at 2,000/month.
- NeuCoins redeem at INR 1 on eligible Tata Neu/Tata-brand transactions; they are not bank-convertible cash. Before 25-Nov-2026, earned coins auto-transfer within seven working days of statement generation and are valid for 12 months (including lock period) from the end of the month credited. HDFC's live page announces a new process effective 25-Nov-2026: manual in-app claim, a 12-month claim window from statement, another 12 months after credit to Tata Neu, minimum claim 100, maximum three successful requests/day. This is future-dated relative to the retrieval date and remains documented but unapplied.
- From 10-Jun-2025, domestic lounge access is one voucher per calendar quarter after INR 50,000 spend in that quarter; claim within 120 days of qualification and use within 180 days of issue.
- Welcome benefit: 499 NeuCoins as first-year membership-fee reversal after first transaction within 30 days; claim through Tata Neu within 60 days. Lifetime-free/first-year-free accounts are ineligible. Validity is 365 days from claim.
- Fuel waiver: 1%, transactions INR 400-5,000, maximum INR 250 per statement cycle.
- Published eligibility: Indian nationality; salaried age 21-60 and net income INR 25,000/month; self-employed age 21-65 and ITR strictly greater than INR 6 lakh/year. Minimum credit score is not published.

## Corrections made

Corrected launch date from 1-Aug to 24-Aug-2022; cleared unsupported Visa Platinum network tier; changed NeuCoins from cashback to points and redemption from cash to eligible Tata Neu voucher/use; removed the inaccurate blanket INR 1 lakh partner-reward cap and unsupported exclusions; documented merchant-specific reward ceilings, category caps, detailed exclusions and the Visa/RuPay boundary; recorded current lounge, welcome, and eligibility terms; replaced stale source dates; and removed the obsolete cashback tag.
