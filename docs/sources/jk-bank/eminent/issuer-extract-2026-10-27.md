# J&K Bank Eminent current issuer review (27 Oct 2026)

## Sources checked

- [Current J&K Bank credit card suite page](https://www.jkb.bank.in/transactions/services/credit-cards), which includes Eminent card fees, reward rates, benefit terms and effective dates for the 2026 suite refresh.
- [J&K Bank card T&C / charges page](https://www.jkb.bank.in/terms-and-conditions), linked from the current issuer card suite, for schedule-of-charges and general card terms.

## Changes applied

- Replaced unsupported 2022 launch/effective dates with current published fee, reward and benefit effective dates: 1-May-2026, 1-Apr-2026 and 12-Mar-2026 respectively; exact card launch date remains unknown.
- Confirmed nil joining, ₹1,500 annual fee, annual fee waiver at ₹3,00,000 spend, and 1.99% foreign currency markup. Current schedule lists finance charge 3% monthly and cash advance charge 3% with ₹300 minimum. GST applies to fees/charges.
- Replaced old 2 points/₹100 plus 4 points/₹100 dining/travel model with current 1 point/₹100 base and 3 points/₹100 on online travel bookings, apparel, dining and electronics. Removed unsupported 2,000-point category cap and old MCC exclusions. Fuel and cash withdrawal remain excluded from general bank rewards terms.
- Removed unverified ₹0.25/₹0.20 point values and catalogue/cashback conversion. Current reviewed issuer source does not publish INR conversion; point validity is three years from transaction date.
- Updated lounge benefits to two domestic visits per quarter and one international visit per half-year (two annually). Current suite page lists ₹500/month BOGO movie benefit, ₹500 welcome voucher, ₹750 quarterly milestone above ₹1,50,000, ₹1,000 annual dining milestone above ₹6,00,000, ₹150 monthly food-delivery benefit after ₹500 spend, and select golf privileges.
- Updated fuel waiver wording to current issuer statement: 1% up to ₹10,000 of fuel transactions per month. Issuer wording does not clarify whether ₹10,000 is the fuel spend ceiling or waiver-value ceiling; transaction range is also unpublished.

## Unresolved

- The exact INR value / redemption options for Reward Points are not stated on the current issuer page; reward portal details require a separate terms capture.
- Fuel cap meaning and eligible transaction minimum/maximum are unclear.
- Movie booking platform, lounge partner terms and specific golf benefits are not detailed on the card page.
- Product-specific age, income and credit score requirements and exact launch date remain unpublished in reviewed sources.
- The issuer webpage has some templated legacy language and does not provide a separate card-specific MITC PDF in the page content reviewed; current page statements are recorded with their published effective dates.
