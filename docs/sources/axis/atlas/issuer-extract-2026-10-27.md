## Field comparison

- **Fees:** Confirmed Rs 5,000 joining and annual fee; no spend waiver. Confirmed monthly finance charge 3.75%, cash advance fee 2.5% (minimum Rs 500), forex 3.5%, and DCC markup 3.5% effective 21 Aug 2026. Redemption fees remain Rs 99 for EDGE portal redemption and Rs 199 per partner transfer. Fuel waiver cap is Rs 400 **per statement cycle**; the record previously labeled the cycle monthly and now uses statement.
- **Rewards:** Confirmed 2 EDGE Miles per Rs 100 other eligible spend and 5 per Rs 100 on Travel EDGE, direct airline and direct hotel spends. Direct travel accelerator applies up to Rs 200,000 travel spend per month; after that eligible spend earns 2 per Rs 100. OTA/travel-agent transactions earn base. EDGE Mile face value is Rs 1 for Axis Travel EDGE bookings; transfer partner ratios are dynamic. Annual transfer cap 150,000 per customer ID (30,000 Group A / 120,000 Group B). Added the issuer-listed telecom exclusion to reward exclusions and extended the schema exclusion vocabulary to support it. The calculator has no telecom spend input, so this accurate exclusion is stored but cannot affect user-specific score calculations. Card-specific terms exclude gold/jewellery, rent, wallet, government, insurance, fuel, utilities, telecom and EMI as applicable to earning/tier thresholds.
- **Milestones and tiers:** Confirmed 2,500 miles at Rs 300,000, additional 2,500 at Rs 750,000, and additional 5,000 at Rs 1,500,000 within anniversary year. Gold and Platinum tier thresholds are Rs 750,000 and Rs 1,500,000. Tier benefits and annual fee paid mileage remain conditional on tier/fee payment; the data model notes rather than calculates all conditions.
- **Lounge:** Confirmed Silver allowance of 8 domestic and 4 international visits/year; Gold 12/6; Platinum 18/12. The current features T&Cs state visit totals include cardholder and guests; Silver example is 6 primary plus 2 guests or all 8 for primary. Because the issuer does not define a fixed guest count per visit, `guests_per_visit` is unknown instead of zero. Domestic card swipe and international card swipe are supported; the current page says lounge booking app journey is suspended.
- **Fuel:** Confirmed 1% surcharge waiver on transactions Rs 400-Rs 4,000, capped at Rs 400 per statement cycle. The issuer notes some fuel transactions may incur a flat Rs 10 + GST surcharge rather than 1% + GST.
- **Welcome:** For cards sourced from 20 Apr 2024, 2,500 EDGE Miles after one transaction within 37 days; credit within 7 days; paid cards only. Earlier card cohorts have distinct offers and are retained in issuer T&Cs; the active record captures the current offer cohort.
- **Eligibility:** Confirmed indicative age 18-70, Indian residency, salaried net income Rs 12 lakh and self-employed net income Rs 15 lakh; no credit-score minimum published.
- **Network and launch:** Retained Visa Signature based on Axis official Atlas card artwork and Visa MCC references in Atlas terms, but current product prose does not explicitly restate network/tier. No exact Atlas public launch date was confirmed from current issuer materials; `launched_on` stays unknown.

## Data-model and currentness limits

The lounge schema can store visits/year and guest count/visit but cannot express a guest share that is consumed from a common annual pool. `guests_per_visit: null` avoids asserting zero or a fixed maximum. The source page says the lounge-app booking journey is suspended; underlying card-swipe access remains listed. Partner-mile values remain dynamic; fixed transfer ratios are not inferred. This review does not establish dataset-wide factual accuracy.
