# Federal Bank Signet (Visa) review — 27 Oct 2026

## Summary

Reviewed the Federal Bank Visa Signet record using current card page, MITC, APR revision notice, general CVP welcome terms, Visa campaign page, lounge program, eligibility FAQ and Visa launch announcement. Corrected APR schedule, launch date and residency, and clarified lounge entry/spend treatment. The base welcome offer remains separate from a time-limited Visa campaign.

## Field decisions and evidence

- **Launch date:** Set to 3 Sep 2021, the date of Federal Bank's [Visa credit card launch release](https://www.federal.bank.in/documents/10180/85678895/Credit%20Card%20launch-%20Press%20Release). The release announces all three variants, including Signet.
- **Network/tier:** Retained Visa and Platinum. Federal Bank's [current 2026 Visa offer page](https://www.federal.bank.in/federal-bank-visa-credit-card) explicitly identifies the offer card type as “VISA Platinum Signet”. Card page cautions that displayed card/network options are illustrative and actual selection occurs during application; therefore this record is for the Visa Signet variant.
- **APR:** Updated to 3.75% / 45% below INR 50,000 AMB; 2.69% / 32.28% for INR 50,000–300,000; 1.99% / 23.88% for INR 300,001–1,000,000; and 0.69% / 8.28% above INR 1,000,000. Federal Bank's [revised APR notice](https://www.federal.bank.in/revised-credit-card-charges) makes rates effective 10 Jan 2026, quarterly updated by prior-quarter average monthly balance in the operative account. The top tier and middle band were absent in the record, and the third tier's maximum was overstated.
- **Fees:** [Current MITC](https://www.federal.bank.in/-/credit-card-tc-1) lists Signet fee schedule, INR 750 legacy joining/annual fee exceptions (waived for customers onboarded from 3 Apr 2023), INR 75,000 legacy annual spend waiver, INR 100 per add-on card up to four, 2.5%/INR 500 cash advance minimum and 3.5% forex markup. Existing fee values retained for the currently advertised new-card cohort.
- **Rewards:** Current [Visa Signet page](https://www.federal.bank.in/visa-signet-credit-card) confirms 3x electronics/apparel, 2x entertainment, 1x other. Existing reward unit/base and exclusions were retained from current MITC-backed record; no cap publication found.
- **Welcome:** Visa Signet page and [CVP welcome terms](https://www.federal.bank.in/credit-cards-cvp-terms-and-conditions) confirm INR 3,000 spend in first 30 days for INR 200 Amazon voucher. CVP terms require active card and individual offer notification; once per customer lifetime; offer communication within 90 days and validity 12 months after receipt. The page's embedded related-card panel shows INR 500, which conflicts with the Signet-specific headline and CVP table; INR 200 is retained.
- **Temporary Visa campaign:** The bank's [Visa offer page](https://www.federal.bank.in/federal-bank-visa-credit-card) shows INR 250 voucher for Visa Platinum Signet if a new Visa card makes an eligible transaction of at least INR 300 between 7 Feb and 31 Oct 2026. Kept in source notes, distinct from base welcome offer.
- **Lounge:** [Current lounge terms](https://www.federal.bank.in/federal-bank-debit-cards-credit-cards-airport-lounge-program) apply to Signet Visa/Mastercard/RuPay: one domestic visit after INR 20,000 spend in the prior cycle, with 21st–20th spend periods. Primary cardholder only; add-on spending counts, add-on holders cannot enter; cash/ATM and unsettled spend billed outside period excluded; INR 2 validation charge may apply; guest charges vary. Record notes updated.
- **Residency:** Set to `either` using the bank's [general credit-card FAQ](https://www.federal.bank.in/credit-card-faq): Indian residents and NRIs may apply. It states age 21 for unsecured cards, but the Signet card-specific secured classification was not verified; age remains null.
- **Other benefits:** Product page confirms INR 200 welcome voucher, quarterly INOX BOGO, quarterly-spend Swiggy voucher and lounge headline. Swiggy voucher value and detailed conditions remain unpublished on reviewed card page. No fuel waiver or accident insurance is advertised on current Visa Signet page.

## Limits / open points

- Visa card/network is selected during application and examples on the product page are illustrative.
- The VISA Platinum label is supported by a current issuer campaign naming the card type, not a card-specific product specification sheet.
- No published accelerator cap, Swiggy voucher value, or detailed lifetime offer model beyond the CVP conditions was found.
- This validates one card record and does not yield a database-wide factual-accuracy percentage.
