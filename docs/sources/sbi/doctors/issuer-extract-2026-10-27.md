# Doctor’s SBI Card: issuer comparison (27 Oct 2026)

Compared the product record with the card-specific non-IMA T&C and brochure, current SBI MITC, cardholder agreement/reward cap policy, current lounge-access page and issuance policy.

## Verified and recorded

- Current joining and annual fee are INR1,499; annual fee waiver at INR2 lakh or more in preceding-year spend. Current MITC confirms finance, cash-advance and forex terms; historical fee effective date is not established.
- Base earn is 1 Reward Point/INR100. Medical Supplies and Travel Booking 5X share a 7,500-point monthly cap; International 5X has a separate 7,500-point monthly cap. Base points continue after each cap. Doctor’s Day (1 July) earns 5X on eligible transactions, with no separate cap stated.
- Terms exclude balance transfers, cash advances, financial charges, disputed transactions, petrol/service station MCCs 5172/5541/5542/5983, Merchant EMI, Flexipay, wallet reloads MCC6540/6541 and rent MCC6513. MCC matching is best effort. Points expire 24 months after accrual.
- Terms say points have no cash or monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash or credit or used to pay card charges; brochure says catalogue/outstanding-balance redemption. Record retains catalog redemption only with no fixed point value and documents the conflict.
- Brochure supports INR1,500 Yatra welcome voucher, INR5,000 voucher after INR5 lakh annual spend, INR20 lakh professional indemnity cover with 0% deductible, four international Priority Pass visits/year (max two per quarter), and fuel surcharge waiver from INR500–4,000 with INR250 statement-cycle cap. Post-quota Priority Pass visits cost USD27 plus taxes.
- Current SBI lounge page lists Doctor’s SBI Card for domestic lounge access but does not specify the quota. The brochure’s two-per-quarter Visa domestic offer is expressly limited-time, so no quota is asserted.
- Card terms illustrate Visa/RuPay. The brochure’s Visa Signature branding is variant-specific; single network and tier are cleared. SBI issuance policy gives salaried age 21–65 and self-employed age 25–70. No card-specific income minimum or score cutoff is published.
