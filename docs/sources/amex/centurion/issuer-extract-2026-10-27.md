## Reconciliation

- Removed unsupported 1999 launch date. Reconfirmed ₹7 lakh joining and ₹2.75 lakh annual fees, cash advance fee, no revolving interest for charge-card balances, delinquency formula, and included supplementary-card package.
- Refreshed foreign exchange, Membership Rewards, lounge and Member Services references. Current lounge terms confirm unlimited Global Lounge Collection access for Centurion members; domestic guest entry is charged at the lounge rate and international guest rules vary.
- RewardXcelerator terms run through 31-Jan-2027: participating-merchant 20X is 6 standard + 114 bonus MR per ₹200; 10X is 6 standard + 54 bonus per ₹200. No minimum except ₹10 lakh for Mercedes-Benz bookings. The dynamic merchant list is not scored as a general accelerator.
- Kept card-wide base earn unmodeled because Amex general material and Centurion RewardXcelerator examples do not establish a single matching earn pace. Membership Rewards points do not expire while the account is active and in good standing.
- Added current temporary changes: supplementary applications paused from 27-Oct-2026, existing cards continue, in-progress applications through 03-Nov; eligible elite-tier enrollment pause 11–16 Nov; updated Centurion Member Services numbers. Amex states new charge-card applications in India are temporarily paused during platform updates.

## Unresolved

Account-specific base earn, current dynamic merchant list, hotel/lifestyle memberships and invitation criteria are not public. Lounge locations and guest policies can change by location.
