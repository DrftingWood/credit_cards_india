- Shared reward T&C confirm 1 Reward Point per INR 100 base earn, 5X on department-store MCC 5311 and cinema MCC 7832, capped at 1,000 accelerated points per statement cycle. Minimum transaction is INR 100; statement redemption requires 1,000 points. Record conversion is INR 0.20/point. SmartDeal travel portal extras are modeled separately from general card spend.
- The YAML carries issuer-listed MCC exclusions, fuel exclusion, expiry/forfeiture treatment and RuPay UPI core-rate caveat. These are retained as documented in the issuer source notes.
- Benefits recorded from the issuer product page include 500 welcome points after INR 5,000 spend within 60 days, 1% fuel surcharge waiver on INR 400–5,000 transactions capped at INR 250 per statement, and up to three lifetime-free add-on cards. Lounge access remains null because no current Easy-specific lounge entitlement was established.
- Eligibility remains resident Indian, age 25–55 in the product materials; no numeric income or credit-score threshold was published. The universal form lists multiple network variants, while this data row represents Visa because the schema cannot express them together.
