## Field comparison and findings

- Fees: INR 2,000 + GST joining fee charged on activation; no joining-fee waiver. INR 2,000 annual fee; renewal waiver at INR 300,000 eligible retail spend in the card-anniversary year. KFS v1.9 confirms 3.75% monthly finance charge for retail cards, 2.5% cash advance fee with INR 500 minimum, 3.49% forex markup, 15% late-payment fee with INR 10 minimum/INR 1,300 maximum, and cash limit up to 20% of credit limit for Ananta.
- Earn: 5 Reward Points per INR 100 shopping/dining/travel, capped at 5,000 accelerated points per statement cycle; 2 points per INR 100 other retail; 1 point per INR 100 insurance, utilities and telecom. Current product page excludes cash, rent, education/government, EMI, fuel, BBPS, transportation and quasi-cash. The agreement states points accrue only in multiples of INR 100 and confirms Ananta's 25,000 total statement-cycle cap. The product page also describes a 100-point per-transaction cap on utility/telecom rewards; this limitation is recorded in the YAML.
- Rewards: points expire after two years. AU FAQ says catalogue redemption is 0.25 paise per point and e-vouchers 0.20 paise per point, with INR 99 + GST fee. The Ananta milestone table assigns INR 1,250 value to 5,000 bonus points (INR 0.25 per point), a 100x conflict with generic FAQ values. No generic value is modeled pending issuer clarification.
- Lounge: 4 complimentary domestic QR entries per calendar quarter, unlocked by a flight booking of at least INR 5,000 via AU Rewardz. Only primary-cardholder bookings qualify; one-way tickets create one QR and return tickets create two; canceled/refunded bookings invalidate the QR; guests are chargeable.
- Milestones: anniversary-year spend slabs at INR 250,000, 500,000, 750,000 and 1,000,000 grant 5,000, 10,000, 15,000 and 20,000 bonus points respectively (50,000 total). Fuel/cash spends do not count. The rupee value conflict above is retained explicitly.
- Fuel waiver: 1% on INR 400–5,000 fuel transactions, capped at INR 200 per statement month for Ananta; GST on surcharge is not refunded.
- Network/tier: current Ananta issuer page says partner offers depend on the selected network variant but does not identify the available network or tier. Both fields remain unknown.
- Eligibility: resident Indian; salaried age 21–60, self-employed age 25–65; add-on cardholder over 18. No numeric card-specific income or bureau-score cutoff found.
- Insurance: the product page lists protection types, but card-specific coverage amounts and claim limits are not established; the YAML keeps those limits unknown.

## Open issuer limitations

The issuer's generic redemption FAQ and Ananta-specific milestone table conflict by 100x on point value. AU's current product page does not publish the network variant, exact reward MCC mapping, offline merchant cap, or Ananta-specific insurance limits. These remain unknown rather than inferred.
