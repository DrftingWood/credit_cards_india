Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) about DCB Bank Payless Credit Card 
 
1. What is a secured credit card? 
A secured credit card is a type of credit card that is backed by a Fixed Deposit or more than 
one Fixed Deposit of the card holder. The Fixed Deposit/s is/ are collateral for the credit limit 
assigned to the card holder. 
DCB Payless Credit Card is a secured credit card, and the card holder must open Fixed 
Deposit/s with DCB Bank to apply for the Card. The Bank’s terms and conditions are 
applicable. 
 
2. Am I eligible to apply for DCB Payless Credit Card, what are the criteria?  
Age: An applicant must be between 18 years and 75 years at the time of applying for DCB 
Payless Credit Card. 
Indian Resident: The applicant must be an Indian resident. 
Minimum Collateral Amount: You need to have DCB Fixed Deposit with a minimum 
amount of INR 15,000. 
Additionally the Bank’s assessment criteria for issuing credit cards will be applicable. 
The Bank reserves the right to issue a DCB Payless Credit Card to the applicant basis an 
assessment of their credentials. The final decision is the Bank’s discretion, in line with the 
mentioned internal policies, and notwithstanding the applicant fulfilling  the criteria.

4. Which all Fixed Deposits can be used for applying for DCB Payless Credit Card? 
DCB Fixed Deposits that are held in the name of a single person are eligible for DCB Payless 
Credit Card. The Fixed Deposits opened/ placed by Hindu Undivided Family (HUF), Non 
Resident Indian (NRI), Partnership firm/ Society/ Trust, minors or opened jointly by 
applicant/s, Tax Saver Fixed Deposit – are not entitled for DCB Payless Credit Card. 
 
5. What are the benefits of having a credit card against a Fixed Deposit? 
Income proof not required:  A customer can apply for the Card without providing proof of 
income. Many professionals can apply, this is also useful for homemakers, students, 
freelancers, self-employed , amongst others.

6. What is the minimum limit and maximum limit on DCB Payless Credit Card? 
The minimum DCB Payless Credit Card limit is INR 13,500 on fixed deposit of 15,000 and 
maximum limit is INR 9,00,000 on fixed deposit of INR 10,00,000 or above. Card holder can 
get up to 90% value of the linked DCB Fixed Deposit amount as the credit limit. The 
limit may vary and is set by the Bank.  
 
 7. Is GST applicable on my DCB Payless Credit Card? 
As per government regulations, all credit card related charges incur tax in the form of GST. 
The current GST for credit cards, as dictated by the GST Council, stands at 18%. Any change 
in the GST in the future shall be applicable as per the Government norms. 
 
8. How do I generate my DCB Payless Credit Card PIN?

Login to DCB Mobile Banking app →Go to ‘Credit Card’ section → Select ‘Set new PIN’ → 
Enter and re-enter PIN of your choice → enter the last 6 digits of your card number and CVV

9. What are the benefits of increasing DCB Payless Credit Card limit? 
A few of benefits of increasing your credit card limit are: 
Improved credit score: Your credit score is calculated based on several factors including 
your credit utilization ratio. By increasing your credit limit, you can increase your available 
credit and improve your credit score. 
Increased purchasing power: This can be especially beneficial in emergencies or for large 
purchases that may be difficult to pay for all at once. 
Enhanced rewards: More spends on your card results in more rewards points.  
 
10. Can I increase my DCB Payless Credit Card limit? 
You can increase your credit card limit instantly by linking additional DCB Fixed Deposits.  
Steps to increase Credit Card limit: 
1. Login DCB Mobile Banking app 
2. Click, ‘Credit Card’ option 
3. Select ‘Increase limit’ option 
4. Link additional DCB Fixed Deposit 
5. Upon successful linking of DCB Fixed Deposit, the Credit Card limit will be increased 
instantly.

12. What is the interest rate on my DCB Payless Credit Card? 
DCB Bank Payless Credit Card applies affordable interest rates in the eventuality that the 
due date for card payment is missed by you. If you cannot clear your monthly dues entirely 
or partially then the penal interest rate of 36% per annum or 3% per month is applicable on 
the outstanding dues.  
 
13. What is credit score? 
A credit score is a three digit value designed to represent the likelihood you will pay your bills 
on time. A credit score is a three digit number, typically between 300 to 850. 
 
14. What is billing cycle?  
The billing cycle refers to the period for which your credit card bill is generated. All the 
transactions that happen during the billing cycle will reflect in your next statement. 
 
15. Can I change my billing cycle? 
You may change your credit card billing cycle by logging in DCB Mobile Banking app. 
According to the Bank’s policy you can change the billing cycle only once in the lifetime of the 
Card. You will be allowed to change the cycle only when you have cleared all your 
outstanding dues. The Bank offers choice of 4 billing cycles i.e., 1st, 7th, 14th, 21st, 
of the month.

17. What is Total Amount Due (TAD) and Minimum Amount Due (MAD)? 
Total Amount Due is the amount due for payment as on the billing date. It includes your 
opening balance, new spends, fees & finance charges, if any, minus your last payment or 
any other due credits.

Minimum Amount Due is the amount that the card holder is required to pay on or before the

19. How do I activate DCB Payless Credit Card? 
You need to enter the activation code in DCB Mobile Banking app in the ‘Credit Card’ section. 
Activation code is sent along with the welcome kit. To start using the Card instantly, you can 
set your credit Card PIN through DCB Mobile Banking app.  
 
20. Can I make part-payment of my monthly bill? 
Yes, you can make part-payment or pay the minimum amount due from your credit card bill 
to avoid late payment fees.  
Do remember that finance charges will be applicable on the amount due and not paid by the 
due date.

25. How many Fixed Deposits can I link to get my DCB Payless Credit Card? 
You can link as many DCB Fixed Deposits as you want, provided these adhere to the 
guidelines for eligible Fixed Deposit for DCB Payless Credit Card. The maximum credit limit 
will be INR 9,00,000.  
 
26. Does my linked DCB Fixed Deposit earn interest even I default on monthly card 
payment dues? 
Yes, you will earn interest on your DCB Fixed Deposit even if you default on your monthly bill 
payment. But if you fail to pay your minimum dues till 85th day, outstanding amount will be 
recovered from linked DCB Fixed Deposit and remaining amount will be credited to your 
repayment account. 
 
27. Can I break the DCB Fixed Deposit against which the DCB Payless Credit 
Card is issued? 
Yes, you can break the DCB Fixed Deposit post clearing all the outstanding dues on 
your DCB Payless Credit Card. 
 
28. How can I close my DCB Payless Credit Card? 
You can close your DCB Payless Credit Card  using DCB Mobile Banking app  post 
clearing your outstanding dues.
