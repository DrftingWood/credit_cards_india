# SBI Card Unnati issuer review (2026-11-01)

Reviewed SBI Card's current Unnati page and campaign content, Unnati e-kit terms, current MITC, current cardholder agreement and SBI Card's official corporate timeline.

## Verified terms

- Fees: NIL joining fee; INR499 annual fee waived for the first four years, then INR499 from year five. Current MITC fee table confirms NIL annual fee and INR499 renewal from year five.
- Finance/charges: This is a secured card. Current SBI MITC gives secured-card finance charges of 2.75% monthly (33% p.a.), not the 3.75% unsecured-card rate. The general fee tariff gives cash advance at 2.5%, minimum INR500; foreign-currency markup is 3.5% for cards not listed in the MITC exceptions.
- Rewards: 1 Reward Point per INR100; 2,000 points minimum to redeem, with 2,000 points worth INR500 statement credit (INR0.25/point). Points expire after 24 months. Cash advance, balance transfer, encash, Flexipay and fuel do not earn points. Rent MCC 6513 was excluded from 1-Apr-2024; government MCCs 9399 and 9311 from 1-Jun-2024. The e-kit terms also list fuel MCCs 5172, 5541, 5542 and 5983.
- Redemption fee: SBI's current MITC charges INR99 plus tax on statement-credit redemption, subject to account-status exceptions; this was absent from the previous structured redemption entry and is now represented.
- Milestone: INR500 cashback on annual spend of INR50,000 or more, applicable in all membership years.
- Fuel: 1% surcharge waiver for transactions INR500–3,000, capped at INR100 per statement cycle; taxes/other charges are excluded.
- Secured eligibility: The product is against an SBI term deposit lien. Current public issuer sources reviewed do not publish card-specific minimum deposit, age, income or credit-score cutoffs. Network is not established in the accessible product terms.

## Corrections and limits

- Corrected monthly finance from 3.75% to the MITC's 2.75% rate for secured cards.
- Added the issuer-published government-transaction reward exclusions and MCCs, the INR99 statement-credit redemption fee and redemption minimum, and repeatable annual milestone terms.
- Cleared the previously recorded exact launch date 28-Mar-2017. SBI's official timeline establishes launch in 2017, but no exact day was located.
- The `2018-01-01` effective dates in this record are dataset baseline markers, not issuer-published start dates. The available sources do not establish exact start dates for all current terms.
