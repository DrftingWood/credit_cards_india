## Current terms encoded

- Launched June 26, 2025, per HDFC launch release. RuPay; no current issuer evidence for a network tier.
- Joining and renewal fees ₹499 + tax. Renewal waived at ₹1,00,000 eligible spend over the preceding 12 months; no joining-fee waiver. Cash advances, fees/charges, and EMI spend do not count toward waiver.
- Foreign currency markup 3.5%; finance charge 3.75% monthly; cash advance fee 2.5% or ₹500, whichever is higher.
- Earn 2% on select PhonePe categories, 1% on select online brands, and 1% on RuPay UPI Scan & Pay; each category is capped at 500 points per calendar month and total monthly cap is 1,500 points. PhonePe Pincode rewards are excluded because the issuer states they expired December 5, 2025.
- Statement credit is ₹1/point, requires 500 points minimum, has a ₹7,500/month cash-redemption maximum, and no redemption fee. SmartBuy value is ₹0.30/point.
- For applications on/after August 1, 2026, the live product page offers five ₹100 PhonePe scratch cards for the first five PhonePe UPI-on-credit-card transactions (each at least ₹100), only if joining fee applies. Prior applications had a ₹249 PhonePe gift card plus ten ₹25 scratch cards.
- HDFC's salary listing gives a ₹20,000/month minimum; stored as annualized ₹240,000. The FAQ says applications are exclusively through PhonePe. Exact age, self-employed income and bureau-score thresholds are unpublished.

## Unresolved point-expiry conflict

The card-specific FAQ and reward terms state that points expire after two years. The newer consolidated MITC says Uno points are valid for one year in the context of upgrade/migration, replacement, renewal or re-issuance. The exact scope differs, and the card record leaves expiry unmodeled pending issuer reconciliation.
