4. Benefits

The following benefits apply to the Edge+ CSB Bank Mastercard Credit Card from the date of launch of that card.

i. Flight Cancellation Coverage:

  • Scope of coverage: Both domestic and international flights.
  • Flight types covered: Both refundable and non-refundable tickets.
  • Admissible claim amount: The admissible claim amount is the portion of the ticket cost that the Cardholder is unable to recover from the airline or the booking channel, after applying any refund, credit or waiver actually received or receivable.
  • Maximum cap: INR 5,000 per claim for domestic flights (per single PNR) and INR 10,000 per claim for international flights (per single PNR). Where the admissible claim amount exceeds the applicable cap, the claim is restricted to the cap.
  • Money deductible: In the case of non-refundable tickets, a deductible of 30% is then applied to the amount arrived at above, for both domestic and international flights, and the balance 70% is payable to the Cardholder. No money deductible applies to refundable tickets.
  • Order of computation: The unrecovered amount is determined first, the applicable cap is applied next, and the 30% deductible is applied last. Illustration: on a non-refundable domestic ticket with an unrecovered amount of INR 10,000, the claim is restricted to the cap of INR 5,000, the 30% deductible of INR 1,500 is applied, and INR 3,500 is payable.
  • Time deductible: A claim is admissible only where the cancellation takes place within the last 24 hours before the scheduled departure in the case of a domestic flight, and within the last 72 hours before the scheduled departure in the case of an international flight.
  • Usage limit: A maximum of 2 claims per Cardholder per Program Year. "Program Year" means each period of 12 consecutive months commencing on the start date of the Program, and the same Program Year applies to all Cardholders irrespective of the date on which a Cardholder becomes eligible.
  • Availability: Automatically available to eligible Cardholders, with no separate enrolment or activation required.
  • Eligibility: Available only to the primary traveller, and only where the booking is made on the Jupiter platform using the Edge+ CSB Bank Mastercard Credit Card.
