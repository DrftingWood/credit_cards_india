rounds/golf lessons on a payment basis by paying the fee at the time of placing the booking. The fees vary across

and play charges for golf rounds will  always include the green fee, cart and caddy fee. For golf lessons, the pay

2. Reservations 
 
• All Axis Bank Cardholders holding Axis Bank Burgundy Private Credit Cards may place their booking for a golf 
round/golf lesson by visiting the online designated website https://www.extraordinaryweekends.com/ and  through 
the Axis Bank Concierge at the toll free number 1800 103 4962.   
• Burgundy Private Cardholders will be required to enter their mobile numbers registered with Axis Bank to access 
the golf booking portal without which they will not be able to access /login into the portal.  
• For golf rounds, Burgundy Private Credit cardholders may bring guests only on a payment basis by paying the 
applicable charges. Cardholders will be allowed to bring a maximum of 3 (three) guest(s) per booking for golf 
rounds. Guest(s) must always play in the same group as the requested cardholder.  
• The applicable charges for the guests will always include the green fee,  cart and caddy fee. The fees vary across 
each location depending on the time of the week (weekend/ weekdays/ public holidays) and the season.  \n 
• For golf lessons, Burgundy Private Credit Card holders may bring 1 (one) guest per golf lesson by paying the 
applicable charges.  
• Cardholders who wish to play on the same flight with another Axis Bank Cardholder must provide the registered 
mobile number and email address of the accompanying Axis Bank Cardholder on the portal without which the 
request will not be processed.  
• Cardholders who wish to play on the same flight as a club member must inform the exact tee -time held by the 
club member which will be verified with the golf club prior to processing the cardholder’s booking. If the tee-time 
is not found, the booking will not be processed.  
• A club member cannot make a booking directly at the golf club/golf learning facility/with a golf coach and transfer 
the confirmed booking over to the cardholder.  
• A cardholder may only request a domestic golf booking for a game/lesson, whether on a weekday/weekend or 
public holiday at least 3 (three) calendar days in advance and for a maximum of 10 (ten) days in advance. The 
date of the booking and the date of the play will not be counted in advance notice.  
• A cardholder may only request an international golf booking for a game, whether on a weekday/weekend or 
public holiday at least 10 (ten) calendar days in advance and for a maximum of 30 (thirty) days in advance. The 
date of the booking and the date of the play will not be counted in advance notice.  
• Requests for golf games will only be accepted for daylight timings to ensure that the start of the golf game must 
enable the completion of 18 (eighteen) holes of play. Golf lessons will be 30 minutes duration and all golf booking 
requests for golf lessons will be accepted as per the local rules and timings available at the club/ driving range/ golf 
lesson facilities.  
• Golfers must have at least a valid golf handicap or in lieu thereof at clubs/golf courses where permissible, a 
certificate of golfing proficiency issued by a certified and approved golf instructor may be acceptable. All golfers 
must produce their handicap certificate if requested by the club. In the absence of a valid handicap certificate, the 
club may not permit the golfer to play and/or may ask them to leave the club anytime during the course of play.  
• Axis Bank and/or Apexlynx will not be responsible or accountable to get a handicap certificate issued to the 
benefit of any cardholder from any golf club.  
• All golfers must carry valid ID proof for verification when visiting the participating golf club/learning facility. In 
the event the ID cannot be verified, the club may not permit the golfer to avail the benefit.  
• Golf lessons at the various golf clubs/ learning facilities are undertaken by certified golf instructors. Cardholders 
may provide their preference for instructors however the instructor will only be assigned as per the availability 
and arrangement between Apexlynx and the golf club/ learning facility.  
• Golf clubs/golf learning facilities and/or golf coaches are neither obliged nor will they entertain any correspond-
ence/enquiries and/or attempts for bookings, whether initial or repeat requests, directly from cardholders.   
• Acceptance of all golf booking requests and/or golf lesson timings shall remain at the sole discretion of the re-
spective golf clubs, golf instructors, and driving ranges, and will be subject to slot availability.  
• All domestic golf booking requests submitted with an advance notice of 4 to 10 days will be processed by Apexlynx 
no later than 4 days prior to the intended date of play, in accordance with the processing timelines and require-
ments prescribed by participating in golf clubs and golf learning facilities. 
• The status of domestic golf bookings may be provided to  cardholders within 1 –2 calendar days prior to the 
scheduled date of play or lesson. 
• Cardholders will not be granted access rights to the stipulated clubs without booking through the designated online 
golf booking portal or through the designated Axis Bank concierge.  
 
 
3. Number of bookings  
 
• Any golf benefits as applicable to a cardholder that remain unutilized will not be carried  forward. There will be 
no “rain check” i.e. no carry forward of credit for any golf round due to any inclement weather or for any other 
reason and there will be no refund made of any guest charges or any other prepaid charges.

bookings are not allowed.

weekday rounds of golf is a minimum of two (2) golfers per flight.  \n 
• Unless otherwise prescribed by local club/golf course rules/seasonal rules the minimum flight conditions for 
Weekend/Holiday rounds of golf are a minimum of three (3) players per flight.

themselves at the clubs where individual requests are accepted.  
• The responsibility to pair an individual golfer will solely be at the discretion of the golf club. Neither Ape xlynx 
nor Axis Bank will have any control over the golfers paired with cardholder and whether the request will be 
fulfilled in every instance.  
• Other than individual golf game requests, it will be the responsibility of the cardholder to fulfil the minimum flight 
conditions in respect of each booking request, and Axis Bank/ Apexlynx will not be responsible to help the 
cardholder make up the minimum flight condition numbers.

• Cardholders will not be permitted to enter into any direct correspondence or communication with any golf

• Cardholders and guests may have to pay directly at the golf club for buggy, caddie, turfmate and golf insurance 
(as applicable as per local club rules) at normal published rates of the golf club.

6. Cancellations and Amendments 
 \n 
• Cardholders can cancel their domestic golf bookings for games/lessons only within the cancellation window which 
is 1 calendar day prior to the date of game or lesson. Full refunds will be initiated in case of in-time cancellations 
for any charges paid at the time of placing the booking.   
• Cardholders can cancel their international golf bookings for games only within the cancellation window which is 
4 calendar days prior to the date of game. Full refunds will be initiated in case of in -time cancellations for any 
charges paid at the time of placing the booking.   
• Cardholders will not be allowed to cancel their booking outside of the cancellation window and the golf round or 
golf lesson will be considered as a “used” golf round or “used” golf lesson and no refund will be initiated for any 
charges paid.    
• Short notice bookings will only be processed on best effort basis and will be subject to availability.  
• In the event that cardholders do not turn up to play their confirmed golf round or golf lesson then, it will be 
treated as a “no show” and the golf round or golf lesson would be taken as a “used” golf round or “used” golf 
lesson.  
• Any refunds as applicable will be initiated by Apexlynx within 24-48 hours of receiving the refund request. It may 
further take 7-10 working days for the refund to be processed, depending on the bank.
