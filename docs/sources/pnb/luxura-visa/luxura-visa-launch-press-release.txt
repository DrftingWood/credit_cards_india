      PNB Launches LUXURA Metal Credit Card on VISA Infinite Platform
~ A super-premium metal card offering global privileges across travel, dining, lifestyle
                             and everyday spends ~




New Delhi, 02 February 2026: Punjab National Bank (PNB), one of India’s leading
public sector bank, announced the launch of the PNB Metal Credit Card – LUXURA
– on the VISA Infinite platform, further strengthening its premium card portfolio. The
launch took place at PNB’s Corporate Office in Dwarka, New Delhi, in the presence
of Shri Ashok Chandra (MD&CEO, PNB) and Shri Anand Vijay Jha (Vice President,
Visa - India & South Asia), along with the Bank’s Executive Directors, and senior
leadership.

Designed for customers with evolving lifestyle aspirations, the LUXURA Metal Credit
Card on VISA Infinite brings together high credit limits, accelerated rewards,
international travel privileges and exclusive lifestyle benefits. With this addition,
LUXURA is now available on both RuPay and Visa platforms, expanding its global
acceptance and enhancing its lifestyle-led value proposition for customers.

Commenting on the launch, Shri Ashok Chandra, MD&CEO, PNB, said, “As
customer expectations continue to evolve, there is a growing demand for financial
solutions that seamlessly combine global acceptance, premium experiences and
everyday convenience. The LUXURA Metal Credit Card has been thoughtfully
designed to address these aspirations, offering a refined blend of exclusivity,
flexibility and lifestyle privileges. With the launch of the credit card on VISA Infinite
platform, PNB strengthens its premium banking proposition and reaffirms its
commitment to delivering globally competitive, customer-centric solutions that align
with the evolving lifestyle needs of today’s customers, both in India and
internationally.”

Cardholders can enjoy a robust rewards benefit, beginning with a welcome benefit of
20,000 reward points, equivalent to ₹5,000, on spending ₹50,000 within the first 90
days. The credit card also offers 4 reward points for every ₹100 spent, along with 3X
reward points on travel and dining, and 10,000 bonus reward points on annual
spends of ₹5 lakh. Additional premium features include zero forex mark-up on
international transactions and unlimited complimentary access to domestic and
international airport lounges.

The LUXURA Metal Credit Card on VISA Infinite unlocks a range of Visa Luxury
Hotel Collection benefits, including daily breakfast for two, food and beverage credits,
complimentary Wi-Fi, VIP guest status, room upgrades and late check-out, subject to
availability. Dining privileges extend to savings of up to 20% under the Visa Dine &
Save programme, valid across select restaurants in India and international markets.

Travel benefits include attractive discounts on domestic and international flights and
hotel bookings through platforms such as Ixigo, Agoda, Expedia and Trip.com, along
with exclusive offers at leading hospitality brands including IHG Hotels, ITC Hotels
and Elivaas Villas. Customers can also enjoy lifestyle and entertainment offers such
as buy-one-get-one movie tickets on BookMyShow, curated discounts on events,
dining and shopping via District, savings on groceries through BigBasket, and a fuel
surcharge waiver of up to ₹350 per billing cycle.

In addition, the card provides access to 24x7 Visa Concierge Services, offering
personalised assistance for travel planning, dining reservations, healthcare support,
entertainment bookings, gifting, shopping and lifestyle needs across major global
destinations.
The joining fee for the metal card is ₹4,999 plus GST, with an annual fee of ₹1,999
plus GST.

For more information, visit www.pnb.bank.in
