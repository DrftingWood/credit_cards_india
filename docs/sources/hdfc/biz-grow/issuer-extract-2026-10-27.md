# HDFC BizGrow issuer source comparison - 2026-10-27

## Current HDFC BizGrow product page (crawled 27 October 2026)

Current page: [BizGrow card page](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/business-credit-cards/biz-grow-credit-card), including the dated update notice for changes effective 15 May 2026.

- Base earn changed from multiples of INR 150 to 2 CashPoints per INR 200 effective 15 May 2026. This supersedes the older FAQ's 2 per INR 150.
- Selected business spend earns 10X after INR 10,000 eligible spend per statement cycle, with a maximum of 1,500 CashPoints per statement cycle.
- Current redemption table: statement credit INR 0.20 per point (minimum 2,500 points); SmartBuy flights/hotels up to INR 0.25; product catalogue up to INR 0.25; business catalogue up to INR 0.30.
- Current page lists the quarterly 2,000 point milestone, fuel surcharge waiver, activation voucher, INR 3 lakh credit liability cover, zero lost-card liability if promptly reported, paid optional business insurance, and current dining/SmartPay offers.
- Eligibility: self-employed Indian citizens age 21-65 with annual ITR above INR 6 lakh.
- Launch announcement: [HDFC press release dated 6 February 2024](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/content/dam/hdfcbankpws/in/en/pdf/press-release/2024/q1-jan-mar/february/hdfcbankexpandssmepaymentsolutionswithlaunchofbusinesscreditcardseriesforselfemployedcommunity.pdf).

## Card-specific FAQ (historical details)

Issuer PDF: [Biz Grow FAQ](https://www.hdfcbank.com/content/bbp/repositories/723fb80a-2dde-42a3-9793-7ae1be57c87f/?path=%2FPersonal%2FPay%2FCards%2FBusiness+Credit+Card%2FBiz+Grow%2FFAQ-Biz-Grow.pdf) (document says published 19 March 2024; search index exposed the text on 27 October 2026).

The indexed issuer text (document dated 19 March 2024) states:

- Historical base earn: 2 CashPoints per INR 150, excluding petrol, wallet, rent, education and EMI. The current 2026 product page supersedes this base rate.
- 10X CashPoints on listed business spends after spend exceeds INR 10,000 in a statement cycle; the listed categories include SmartPay/PayZapp bill payments, income/advance tax, GST, Dmart, ClearTax, MMT MyBiz flights/hotels, and Nuclei business software via SmartBuy Biz Deals.
- The older FAQ summary describes the cap as 1,500 CashPoints per month. The card-specific T&C below specifies 1,500 per statement cycle; the record uses statement-cycle language.
- Quarterly milestone: 2,000 CashPoints for INR 1 lakh spend. Fuel waiver: 1%, INR 400-5,000 transaction size, INR 250 maximum per statement cycle.
- Joining and renewal fees INR 500 + taxes; renewal fee waiver wording differs at exactly INR 1 lakh. FAQ says more than INR 1 lakh; current fee page states spend INR 1 lakh. Older FAQ says joining fee is charged after 120 days, but current page does not confirm that timing.

## Card-specific CashPoints T&C

Issuer PDF: [BizGrow Credit Card Cash Points Program T&C](https://v.hdfcbank.com/content/dam/hdfc-aem-microsites/biz-cards/bizgrow-credit-card-tnc.pdf) (document indexed as published 28 August 2024).

The indexed terms specify 10X (20 CashPoints per INR 150) business-essential earn after INR 10,000 per statement cycle and a cap of 1,500 CashPoints per statement cycle. They identify SmartPay/PayZapp bills, income/advance tax, GST, Dmart, ClearTax, MMT MyBiz flights/hotels and Nuclei business software; the terms say only transactions eligible under the bank's rules earn points. The record's former 1,000-point cap was corrected to 1,500. Education was added to base exclusions.

## HDFC Business MITC

Issuer PDF: [Business Card MITC](https://v.hdfcbank.com/content/dam/hdfc-aem-microsites/biz-cards/mitc.pdf), updated 14 July 2025.

Indexed issuer text places BizGrow among cards with a 3.75% monthly / 45% annual finance charge. It states cash advance charges of 2.5% or INR 500, whichever is higher, and 3.5% cross-currency markup. These support the existing card fee values.
