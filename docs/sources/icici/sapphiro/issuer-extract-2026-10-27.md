## Verified changes and unresolved detail
- The product is active and the apply path is live. Sapphiro is offered in paired dual-network choices (Mastercard + Amex or Visa + RuPay). Cleared the aggregate YAML's unsupported single Visa/Infinite assignment and exact 2014 launch date.
- Fees: joining INR6,500 + GST, annual INR3,500 + GST, next-year annual fee waiver on spend over INR600,000; forex markup 3.5%; monthly finance charge 3.75%. Current readable MITC did not establish the card-specific cash-advance transaction fee; omitted.
- Rewards: ICICI Reward Points, not PAYBACK. Current product page lists 2 RP/INR100 domestic, 4 international and 1 utility/insurance; fuel earns no points. Generic card T&Cs exclude cash withdrawal, fees/charges, balance transfer and EMI. Removed fixed INR0.25 valuation and unsupported MCC 6513/wallet/rent exclusions. Points have no cash value under issuer T&Cs; INR99 + GST redemption handling fee.
- iShop: current FAQ describes up to 12X base points on hotels, 6X on flights/buses/shopping vouchers. With Sapphiro's 2 RP/INR100 base, the respective rates are up to 24 and 12 points/INR100. They are portal-only and vary by active offer. Current cap sheet lists 15,000/month and 8,000/day for other credit cards; the shared monthly cap is modelled and daily cap noted because schema does not support a daily cap.
- Lounge: 4 domestic visits/quarter after INR75,000+ eligible spend in the previous calendar quarter; primary cardholder. FAQ splits entitlement for paired Mastercard/Amex card variants as two per quarter on each. Two international visits/year via Priority Pass, primary cardholder.
- Golf: one round/lesson per INR50,000 retail spend in prior calendar month, maximum four/month. Credit by 10th of the following month; use within next two months.
- BMS: buy-one-get-one, up to INR500 off second ticket, twice/month; from Apr-2026 requires INR25,000+ previous-quarter spend to access next quarter's offer. Add-on card must individually meet threshold; paired dual variants get one offer per month per variant.
- Welcome: guaranteed fixed vouchers are CLiQ INR3,000, EaseMyTrip domestic flight vouchers INR4,000 and Croma INR1,500 (INR8,500 total). Da Milano offers INR5,000 off when purchase is at least INR19,999, so this is conditional discount value, not an unconditional voucher; 3-month EazyDiner Prime also offered. Claimed within 45 days after joining fee payment.
- Insurance: air accident INR30,000,000 for primary cardholder on international travel when ticket is purchased with card. Product FAQ requires card activity within 60 days; policy updated Dec-2025 defines active card as a transaction in prior 90 days. Discrepancy retained for recheck. RuPay variant also has INR200,000 personal accident cover.
- Fuel waiver: 1% on fuel transactions up to INR4,000 at outlets in India; primary and supplementary cardholders. Current Sapphiro page does not specify the lower transaction bound or a monthly cap; left lower bound/cap unknown.
- Eligibility: Indian citizen aged 21?65; salaried monthly income INR75,000 (annualized to INR900,000) or self-employed annual income INR900,000; income proof required. No numeric credit-score floor published.
