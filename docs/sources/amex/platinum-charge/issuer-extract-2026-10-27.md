## Reconciled record

- Current card page lists the card as a charge card with an annual fee of ₹66,000 plus taxes, and currently says new applications in India are temporarily paused. The 31-Aug-2025 issuer schedule lists ₹66,000 for both first and renewal years, 3.5% forex, 3.5% cash advance fee (₹250 minimum), and no charge-card interest. Cash withdrawal service in India is suspended; this fee schedule does not establish domestic ATM availability.
- Current page confirms 1 MR point per ₹40 on regular spend, 3X on eligible overseas spend, and points do not expire. ShopWise 5X e-vouchers and Rewards Xcelerator up to 20X depend on merchant/offer terms and should not be flattened into universal earn rates. The existing fuel floor/bonus cap and exclusion treatment remain as previously modeled; source details are listed in the card record.
- Current page continues to list a welcome gift after paying the annual fee and spending ₹50,000 net within the first two months; stated issuance window is 6–8 weeks.
- The page continues to list a renewal voucher up to ₹35,000 after ₹20 lakh membership-year spend and payment of the renewal fee. Amex's October update says milestone eligibility will use transaction posting date from 15-Nov-2026 and vouchers/bonus points will be awarded by month-end.
- Current published benefits include a 1,550+ lounge collection, Fine Hotels & Resorts stated value of ₹44,300, air accident cover up to ₹5 crore (subject to eligible ticket purchase), overseas medical cover of USD 50,000 for up to seven days, purchase protection, EazyDiner Prime dining, and concierge. Current page lists Taj, Marriott, Hilton, Radisson, Hertz, Postcard and Accor elite memberships. Access mechanics and policy terms vary; see structured field notes.
- Current eligibility page lists resident Indian, age over 18, serviceable-city residence, Indian bank account, good credit history/no payment defaults, annual income at least ₹25 lakh salaried or ₹15 lakh self-employed, and more than 12 months of business trading if self-employed. It publishes no numeric credit-score threshold.
- Current Amex update says new hotel/flight bookings will be unavailable until 15-Nov-2026, with lifestyle services unavailable 9–14 Nov and support for existing bookings subject to the stated transition window. This is a temporary booking-service disruption, not a published cancellation of the card benefit.
