## Verified map and limits

- Fees: INR 75,000 joining, INR 10,000 renewal with INR 10 lakh annual waiver, 1.8% forex, 1.79% monthly finance; cash advance fee waived.
- Rewards: 1.5 points/INR 100 base; 0.7/INR 100 on utility, insurance, government and education; no fuel earn. Cash credit is INR 1/point, min 500 and max 10,000/month; MITC cash-redemption fee INR 149. Catalog and airline conversions have no fixed value/verified ratios.
- Lounges: 6 domestic per quarter after INR 5 lakh eligible settled spend in previous quarter; 4 international per quarter outside India, primary cardholder only. Golf: four games and four lessons per month effective 10-Mar-2026, primary cardholder only.
- Fuel waiver: 1% on INR 500–10,000; current schedule publishes no cap. GST on surcharge is not waived.
- BookMyShow offer and Total Protect are recorded as descriptive benefits; terms bound the actual value.
- Eligibility: salaried INR 36 lakh, self-employed ITR INR 42 lakh, invite-only. Public card-specific minimum age and bureau cutoff were not found.
