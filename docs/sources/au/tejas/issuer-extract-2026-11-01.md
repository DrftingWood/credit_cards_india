# AU Tejas — issuer comparison (2026-11-01)

Compared current AU Tejas product page/FAQ, Tejas welcome and partner-offer PDFs, current AU Card Member Agreement (24-Mar-2026), Rewardz FAQ and 30-Sep-2026 KFS.

## Fees and reward points

- INR 500 joining/annual fee + GST; no joining waiver; annual renewal waiver at INR 150,000 eligible retail spend in card anniversary year.
- KFS: forex 3.49%, finance charge 3.75% monthly, cash advance 2.5% or INR 500 minimum, overlimit 2.5% or INR 500 minimum.
- Base: 1 point/INR 100 other eligible spend; 5 points/INR 100 through AU Rewardz portal. Utility/telecom: 1 point/INR 100, max 100 per transaction. Overall cap 25,000 points/statement cycle.
- Exclusions: rent, education/government, EMI, fuel, cash withdrawal, quasi-cash, BBPS and transportation; agreement specifies BBPS on AU 0101, while product FAQ lists BBPS without clarifying channel. Retain that scope uncertainty.
- General Rewardz catalog conversion 0.25 paise/point; e-vouchers 0.20 paise. Minimum 200 points, INR 99 + GST per redemption. Points expire after two years, with additional closure/inactivity/delinquency forfeiture conditions in agreement.

## Welcome, milestones and offers

- Welcome choice: 2,000 points or INR 500 brand voucher, for paid-fee primary cardholder with activation and first successful transaction within 30 days; claim within six months.
- Statement-cycle milestone: 500 points after five transactions of at least INR 500 each. Fuel waiver: 1% on INR 400–5,000 transactions, maximum INR 100 per statement cycle.
- Merchant offers: District movies, Amazon Fresh/Now and Zomato each 10% off, INR 150 minimum, INR 50 maximum, once per card per month. Cab booking and AU 0101 bill-pay offers each 10% cashback, INR 150 minimum and INR 50/card/statement cycle; payment-channel/MCC and settlement conditions apply. These are distinct from point earn.
- Xpress EMI: eligible transaction minimum INR 500 and total conversion minimum INR 2,000; up to 20 transactions may be clubbed. Current KFS processing charge 1%, minimum INR 99 + GST.
- Card Liability Cover, Credit Shield and Device Protection are advertised but limits and full policy terms are not published in the card FAQ.

## Eligibility and modeling limits

Resident Indian; salaried 21–60, self-employed 25–65, add-on over 18. Card-specific income/score cutoffs are unpublished. Rewardz portal merchant/channel eligibility remains transaction-specific; the schema cannot represent every offer's MCC, channel, timing or stacking condition. No lounge benefit is advertised on the current card page.
