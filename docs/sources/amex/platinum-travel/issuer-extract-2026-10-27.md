## Reconciled record

- The current card page lists INR 5,000 first-year and renewal fees; the fee schedule updated 31-Aug-2025 lists 3.5% monthly finance charge, 3.5% foreign-currency markup, 3.5% cash advance fee with INR 250 minimum, and late fee 30% of minimum amount due (INR 500 minimum, INR 1,000 maximum). Domestic cash withdrawals remain paused in India.
- Current base earn is 1 Membership Rewards point per INR 50. Fuel, insurance, utilities, cash transactions, and merchant/POS EMI are excluded. SafeKey and post-purchase EMI continue earning. The previous wallet-load and blanket EMI exclusions were removed. Points do not expire while the card remains active and in good standing; cancellation forfeits points within 30 days. Reward Multiplier is merchant-specific.
- Current card page confirms 10,000-point welcome gift after annual fee payment and INR 15,000 spend in 90 days; 10,000 points on INR 4 lakh annual spend; and Taj Experiences voucher worth INR 20,000 on INR 7 lakh membership-year spend. Voucher examples vary by catalogue; the stored examples are not a single fixed point value.
- Lounge gate changed effective 1-Oct-2026: INR 70,000 eligible spend in Q3 2026 qualifies for Q4 2026 visits; INR 1 lakh in each quarter from Q4 2026 qualifies for the following quarter. Card has two complimentary domestic lounge visits each calendar quarter when qualified. Priority Pass membership is complimentary but international lounge usage is charged at the prevailing rate; the model records zero complimentary international visits.
- Effective 15-Nov-2026, Amex will use transaction posting dates for milestone eligibility and award points/vouchers by month-end. EMI services have a temporary 11-16 Nov processing disruption. New card applications in India are temporarily paused.
- Eligibility page lists resident Indian, over 18, annual income INR 6 lakh or more for salaried and self-employed applicants, Indian bank account, good credit history/no defaults, serviceable-city residence, and more than 12 months trading if self-employed. No numeric credit-score floor is published.
