What are Tata NeuCoins?
Tata NeuCoins are an exclusive Rewards Points metric system created by Tata Neu.
‘NeuCoins’ and ‘Tata NeuCoins’ may be interchangeably used in this document

What are the Exclusion Categories for Accrual of NeuCoins?
Following are the Exclusion Categories for Any type of NeuCoins, NeuCoins will not be accrued for the following
spends/transactions on the card:-

•     Fuel Spends | Wallet loads / Gift or Prepaid Card load/ Voucher purchase
•     Cash Advances | Payment of Outstanding Balances
•     Payment of card fees and other charges | Smart EMI / Dial an EMI transaction
•     Rental and Government related transactions
•     With effect from 1st Sep 2024, NeuCoins will not be accrued for Education payments made through third-party apps
      like (but not limited to) CRED, Cheq, MobiKwik, and others. However, education payments made directly through
      college/school websites, or their POS machines will earn NeuCoins.
•     With effect from 1st July 2025, NeuCoins will not be earned on Online Skill-Based Gaming transactions (MCC-5816)

How will a cardholder get NeuCoins on EMI spends on Tata Neu Plus HDFC Bank Credit Card?
• Cardholder will be getting 1% back as NeuCoins on the below type of EMI’s
   o Merchant/EasyEMI
   o Aggregator EMI
   o Brand EMI
• SmartEMI/Dial an EMI (DAE) is not considered under this feature.
• Only EMI principal amount will be rewarded with NeuCoins.
• Interest & processing fees amounts will not be eligible for NeuCoins.

What is the welcome benefit on the Tata Neu Plus HDFC Bank Credit Card?

Welcome Benefit of 499 NeuCoins (as reversal of 1st Year Membership Fees) will be given to the cardholder by Tata
Digital Private Limited inside Tata Neu App. Welcome Benefit is applicable on 1st Transaction done within 30 days of card
issuance and will be available to claim inside Tata Neu App within 60 days of such transaction.

Note: For eligible customers, option to claim the Welcome Benefit will be available inside Tata Neu App for 180 days
from statement date of the eligible transaction. The validity of the NeuCoins given against Welcome Benefit will be 365
days from the claim date. Lifetime Free or First Year Free Cards will not be eligible for the Welcome Benefit.

b. By using Net Banking

How do I activate my Credit Card for online, contactless and/or international transactions?
   1. Using HDFC Bank MyCards:
      1. Log in to https://mycards.hdfc.bank.in/using your registered mobile number
      2. Enter the OTP configured to your registered mobile number
      3. Click on “Card Control” service under Credit Cards.
      4. You can set and customise your credit card usage/limits
      5. Enter OTP configured to your registered mobile number to successfully make these changes.

   2. Or HDFC Bank Mobile Banking – Credit Card Section

   3. Or HDFC Bank Net Banking – Credit Card Section

How do I get Credit card statement?
Once the credit card statement is generated, it will be sent to your registered email address. You can also view and
download the credit card statement by logging into HDFC Bank NetBanking or HDFC Bank MyCard App or HDFC Bank
Mobile App online.

My Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card is lost/stolen. What should I do?
In case your card is lost or stolen, please block your card immediately to avoid it from being misused. To block your card
either:

    •   Call HDFC Bank PhoneBanking Number (1800 1600 / 1800 2600) or
    •   Log onto HDFC Bank NetBanking and block your card using the 'Hotlisting' option
