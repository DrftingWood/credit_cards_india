## Record comparison and actions

- **Network and product tier:** Horizon terms explicitly provide different lounge rules for Visa Signature and Mastercard World. The product page describes both variants. Cleared the record's single `visa` / `signature` values to unknown because one card record represents both versions. The product's `premium` tier remains a product classification, not a network tier.
- **Fees:** Confirmed standard joining and annual fee INR 3,000 plus GST, billed after the card anniversary; cash advance fee 2.5%, minimum INR 500; foreign currency markup 3.5%; monthly finance charge 3.75%; DCC 3.5% from 21 Aug 2026. Axis also advertises NIL joining and annual fees to eligible customers with a Burgundy Savings Account opened on/after 1 July 2026, subject to conditions and bank discretion. The schema's spend-based waiver field cannot encode this relationship-based offer, so it remains null with the condition in source notes.
- **Other conditional fees:** The current product page publishes category / threshold fees for rent, wallet, fuel, third-party-app education, utilities and online skill gaming. These conditional charges are documented in the fee notes because the current fee schema has no structured fields for them.
- **Rewards:** Confirmed 2 EDGE Miles per INR 100 on other eligible spend and 5 per INR 100 on Travel EDGE and direct airline channels. Travel agents and OTAs earn the base rate. Updated exclusions to include telecom alongside transport/tolls, utilities, insurance, education, government, wallet/gift cards, rent and fuel. The product-specific terms state partner-transfer ratios are dynamic; removed the inaccurate 1:1 ratio. Current Travel EDGE transfer terms show a 500,000-mile annual Horizon cap (100,000 Group A / 400,000 Group B) and 500-mile minimum; partner roster and ratios remain dynamic and should be checked at redemption.
- **Point value:** The product value chart illustrates INR 1 per mile for Travel EDGE booking examples but labels the chart illustrative. Removed the claim that INR 1 is a guaranteed floor; the record's value remains an illustrative estimate, not an assured realized value.
- **Airport lounges:** International quota is 2 visits per calendar quarter and a USD 1 authorization is reversed. Domestic quota is 8 per calendar quarter for Visa Signature and 6 for Mastercard World. Set the domestic visit count to 6, the lower guaranteed quota across both variants, and document the Visa Signature quota of 8 in notes. Set guest counts to unknown and removed DreamFolks / Priority Pass operator claims because the current card-specific terms do not establish them.
- **Other benefits:** Confirmed 5,000 welcome miles on first transaction of INR 1,000 or more within 30 days for paid cards; first transaction below the threshold disqualifies a later qualifying transaction. T&C lines contain an initial “greater than INR 1,000” phrase followed by a specific “INR 1,000 or above” clause, while current product page also says greater than or equal to INR 1,000; record follows the specific clause and product page. Cards issued from 1 Sep 2026 may take up to 60 days for posting. Renewal is 1,500 miles from second anniversary year onward for primary paid-card members, within 30 days. Fuel waiver: 1% for INR 400–5,000 transactions, capped INR 400 per statement month; fuel-surcharge GST is not refunded. Lost-card liability cover is up to INR 10 lakh, subject to policy terms and exclusions.
- **Eligibility:** Current product page/eligibility copy lists resident Indian applicants aged 18–70; it does not provide a card-specific income or score cutoff. Those numeric cutoffs remain null.
- **Launch date:** No exact launch date established from reviewed issuer sources; remains null.

## Schema and evidence limitations

The record cannot express two network variants with separate domestic lounge quotas or Burgundy relationship-based lifetime-free eligibility in structured fields; it stores the lower 6-visit quota and records the Visa-specific 8-visit quota in notes. Guests per visit and lounge operators are not stated by the reviewed current card-specific terms. The illustrative EDGE Mile value should not be treated as a guaranteed redemption return. Offers (Dining Delights and Wednesday promotions) are changeable and omitted from permanent benefits.
