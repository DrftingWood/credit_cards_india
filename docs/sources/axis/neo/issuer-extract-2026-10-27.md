## Record reconciliation

- **Fees and charges:** The current NEO page and archived 28-Aug schedule support INR 250 joining and second-year annual fees, first-year annual fee nil, and lifetime-free issuance only through select channels. The record correctly does not represent the selective offer as universal. Finance is 3.75% monthly, cash advance is 2.5%/INR 500 minimum, and forex/DCC is 3.5%; the fee notes also preserve transaction-specific charges and the Axis late-fee schedule.
- **Rewards:** The current page confirms 1 EDGE point per INR 200 and does not establish a universal rupee redemption value. The archived card-specific and general issuer terms support the exclusions and forfeiture conditions recorded in YAML. No accelerated everyday earn is modeled.
- **Activation and partner offers:** The page confirms 100% cashback up to INR 300 on the first utility bill payment within 30 days. Zomato is INR 120 off twice monthly at INR 499 minimum; Paytm utility discount is 5%, INR 299 minimum, INR 150 monthly maximum; Blinkit is 10%, INR 750 minimum, INR 250 monthly maximum. Cleartrip lists 7% flights up to INR 2,000 and 18% hotels up to INR 4,000, each once per use per month. These are conditional partner discounts, not general earn.
- **BookMyShow:** Current page lists 10% monthly movie discount, maximum INR 100, against INR 25,000 settled spend in the preceding three calendar months. The card terms state that the new gate starts 1-Nov-2026 and that new-card waivers apply during issuance month plus the next three calendar months. The YAML records this date boundary and waiver.
- **Eligibility and identity:** Current page states age 18–70 and resident Indian or NRI. Product materials do not establish a numeric income or bureau-score floor, network variant, network tier, or exact launch date. Lounge access remains unsubstantiated and null.

## Unresolved conditions

The issuer does not state which application channels receive lifetime-free issuance. The page's fee table leaves exactly INR 500 total due outside its displayed late-fee bands. Reward catalog values and fee amount are not published on the card page. Dynamic Wednesday offers are not valued as guaranteed benefits. These limitations remain explicit in the record.

The detailed source review and source archiving were completed 27-Oct-2026. The current product page was reopened 1-Nov-2026; no repeat review of the October terms was needed under the one-month rule.
