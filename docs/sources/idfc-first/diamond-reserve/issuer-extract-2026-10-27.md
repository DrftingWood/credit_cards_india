# IDFC FIRST Diamond Reserve: issuer comparison (2026-10-27)

Reviewed the current [Diamond Reserve product page](https://www.idfcfirst.bank.in/credit-card/diamond-reserve-credit-card), the 6-Jan-2026 launch release, 18-Jun-2026 card privileges, May-2026 cardmember communication, current customer MITC, 2025 product guide, 31-Aug-2026 shared insurance schedule, initial and revised international-rewards notices, 17-Sep-2026 zero-forex notice, and IDFC's current lounge-card eligibility page. Dated HTML captures and issuer PDFs are stored in this directory.

## Decisions in the record

- Launch date is 6-Jan-2026. The card page and launch announcement support INR3,000 joining/annual fees, INR6 lakh annual-fee waiver, zero forex, and the flat INR199 + GST cash-advance fee. Zero forex applied from launch, so the fee record now begins 6-Jan-2026.
- Current privileges support a 3-point/INR150 ordinary rate up to INR20,000 eligible monthly spend, 10 points/INR150 on eligible incremental spend above it, and separate 3-point and 1-point categories. Points are valued at INR0.25, do not expire, and redemption carries an INR99 + GST fee. Current card-specific FAQ and June terms exclude international spends; IDFC's revised 13-Sep bank-wide notice says rewards continue as applicable. The record follows the card-specific Diamond terms and preserves the broad-versus-specific conflict.
- Product-page headlines and detailed travel terms disagree on app booking bonuses. The current page promotes 20% hotel / 10% flight bonuses, while the detailed table uses 30 / 15 bonus points per INR150 and an 8,000-point monthly cap; an example also conflicts with the flight rate. No bonus rate is modeled.
- Current lounge terms support two domestic and two international visits per quarter, subject to INR20,000 eligible spend in the current month for next-month access. Primary and add-on cards share the quota. Movie, fuel-waiver, golf, railway-lounge, roadside and welcome-offer conditions are recorded in the YAML with their term limits.
- The Aug-2026 shared insurer schedule lists INR5 lakh personal accident cover, superseding the INR10 lakh amount in the Jan-2026 launch release. It also supports INR50,000 credit shield, card liability and purchase protection, INR1 crore air accident, and the listed USD travel covers. The record now includes credit shield.
- The issuer still advertises CFAR up to INR25,000, but the linked Diamond policy ended 7-May-2026 and a replacement policy period was not found. Current active CFAR amount remains unresolved and is not modeled as confirmed coverage.
- Current general lounge-card eligibility guidance states age 21+ and stable income, supporting minimum age 21. No Diamond-specific numeric income or bureau-score threshold was found. General guidance that says Indian citizenship does not establish a residence requirement, so residency remains null.

## Unresolved or schema-limited items

- Bank-wide revised international reward notice versus the Diamond-specific international exclusion.
- App travel bonus rates and the detailed flight example.
- Active CFAR policy period after 7-May-2026.
- Launch release versus Aug-2026 schedule personal-accident limit; latest schedule used.
- Numeric card-specific income and credit-score thresholds are unpublished.
- Airport meet-and-greet annual spend qualifier and current partner-level hotel offer terms need additional card-specific terms.
- Flat cash advance and ancillary add-on/UPI/replacement fees are retained in notes because the core fee schema cannot represent every fee type.
