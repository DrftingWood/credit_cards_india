## Confirmed and corrected

- Joining fee is nil; annual fee is INR 499, waived after INR 120,000 anniversary-year retail spend (not applicable to lifetime-free offers). Finance charge is 3.99% monthly. The current MITC lists 3.40% foreign-currency markup for both standard PaisaSave and its RuPay virtual companion; corrected the standard card from 3.5% to 3.4%.
- Fuel waiver is 1% for INR 500–3,000 transactions, capped at INR 250 per statement cycle. Corrected the prior INR 400–5,000/no-cap monthly model.
- YES BANK eligibility page lists age 21–60, salaried minimum net salary INR 25,000/month or self-employed ITR INR 750,000 and above. YAML annualizes the salaried monthly amount to INR 300,000 because the schema has no monthly salary field.
- The standard PaisaSave card is the physical Mastercard; the separate complimentary RuPay card is virtual. This network distinction is published by Paisabazaar, the co-brand partner. Mastercard tier is not established by the issuer evidence, so network tier is null.
- The reward terms were already substantively checked against the issuer page on 2026-10-25, less than one month before this review. They were left unchanged under the freshness instruction. That review maps 6%/1% cashback as reward points, 24 eligible MCCs, INR 3,000 cashback cap, INR 1 point value, exclusions and UPI limitations.

## Open points retained

- The reward structure's effective start date is not published. The conservative calculator mapping does not generalize the 6% MCC allowlist to all dining/travel spends; dining category matches require MCC-level treatment.
- No minimum credit score is published. The network is confirmed as Mastercard from the co-brand partner page, but a Mastercard tier is not established.
- Card-specific travel/dining MCC eligibility remains tied to the issuer's allowlist and UPI exception; avoid reading the marketing label as a blanket 6% rate.
