## Current card status, fees and charges

- The issuer states that new Prosperity Rewards issuance to new customers is discontinued; existing cardholders will receive a new card on expiry. The card is represented as `on-hold`, not discontinued for current holders.
- The card page identifies Mastercard or Visa as its network partner, so a single current network variant/tier cannot be set. No exact launch date is published.
- Joining fee INR 499 plus applicable taxes, waived after INR 10,000 spend within one month of card setup. Renewal fee INR 499 plus taxes, waived after INR 100,000 retail spend in the 12 months before renewal.
- Card page finance charge: 3.99% monthly (47.88% annualized). Current MITC v18 sets cash-advance charge to 2.5% or INR 650, whichever is higher, effective 15-Jun-2026; the older indexed card-specific MITC lists INR 500 minimum. The current v18 schedule is represented.
- Current MITC v18 sets foreign-currency markup at 3.50% from 15-Jun-2026.

## Rewards

- 2 YES Rewardz points per INR 200 on all eligible categories except the card's select MCCs; those select MCCs earn 1 point per INR 200.
- Current select MCCs from 15-Jun-2026: 4121, 4131, 4900, 5411 and 5499. Utility MCC 4900 earns at most 75 points per statement cycle.
- Current exclusions: rent, wallet loads, fuel, cash withdrawals, post-purchase and instant EMI, government, marketing/advertising, insurance, education, railways, toll, ferry and jewellery. The current issuer page provides MCC mappings; the YAML carries them.
- Current value chart says value is up to INR 0.25 per point. Realized redemption value is left unknown. MITC v18 charges INR 100 plus GST per reward redemption request and INR 99 plus GST for flight bookings. General MITC expiry is three years after earning.

## Benefits and remaining conflicts

- Fuel surcharge waiver: 1% on INR 500-INR 3,000 fuel transactions, capped at INR 100 per statement cycle. GST on surcharge is not reversed. General MITC says UPI fuel transactions do not receive the waiver.
- Current product page carousel advertises purchase protection up to INR 50,000 against accidental damage to online-purchased mobile/electronic items for six months. A purchase-protection FAQ dated 16-Apr-2024 says only specified cards are eligible and does not name YES Prosperity Rewards (it lists ACE/Prosperity Rewards Plus); applicability to this product is unresolved. The page statement is retained with medium confidence and the discrepancy is explicit.
- Current page terms reference lounge and golf conditions but do not expose a specific current quota on the rendered view; no quota is asserted.
- Age, income, credit-score and residency cutoffs are not displayed for this card on the current page. Existing-cardholder reissue is documented above.
