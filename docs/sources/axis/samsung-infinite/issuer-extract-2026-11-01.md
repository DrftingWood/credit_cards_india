# Samsung Axis Bank Infinite — issuer source review (2026-11-01)

## Sources compared

- [Axis Samsung Infinite product page](https://www.axis.bank.in/cards/credit-card/samsung-axis-bank-infinite-credit-card)
- [Axis card-specific Samsung campaign terms](https://www.axisbank.com/docs/default-source/default-document-library/credit-cards/tnc-for-samsung-infinite.pdf)
- [Axis current MITC](https://www.axisbank.com/docs/default-source/default-document-library/mitc-credit-cards.pdf)

## Verified

- The current product page indicates the card is not accepting applications; `on-hold` is retained.
- Axis campaign terms state 10% cashback on Samsung purchases through listed channels, capped INR 5,000 monthly and INR 20,000 per year from issuance; three activation transactions within 30 days earn 30,000 EDGE Reward Points. Flipkart was excluded effective 20-Dec-2024.
- The published campaign terms include Amazon as an eligible channel. The record's assertion that Amazon was excluded effective 1-Jan-2026 was unsupported by a current primary source and has been removed from the displayed channel note.

## Limit

The card-specific campaign PDF does not show a later revision date for Amazon eligibility. Treat the latest published Axis terms as what Axis documents publicly; actual eligibility remains subject to any account-specific or subsequently communicated terms.
