## Terms transcribed

- Visa Signature; product launch event 3-Jun-2024.
- Joining and annual fees INR5,000 + GST; annual waiver at INR600,000 spend; forex 3.5%; finance charge 3.75% monthly. Current general MITC gives cash advance 2.5%, minimum INR500.
- Annual fee reversal from year 2 requires at least INR600,000 spend during the prior anniversary year, excluding EMI. The current page calls this a calendar year; card-specific T&Cs say anniversary year, so data follows the more specific terms and records the conflict.
- Earn 7 Adani OneApp Rewards per INR100 at participating merchants, 1.5 domestic, 2 international, and 0.5 utility/insurance. Points floor to whole units. Exclusions: cash advance, fuel, rent, charges/interest, card fees, EMI/BT/PLCC, gambling and business spend. No cap stated in current card terms; issuer launch statement says rewards are uncapped.
- Points post to the Adani OneApp. Adani Rewards terms state points cannot be converted to cash, have no monetary value and expire two years after credit. The prior INR1-per-point and bank-statement credit assertions were unsupported and removed.
- Welcome offers for primary cardholder: INR5,000 flight voucher after paying the joining fee and spending INR25,000 within 60 days; INR2,000 hotel voucher on activation.
- Lounges: 4 domestic quarterly visits after INR75,000 spend in the preceding quarter (primary cardholder only); 2 international visits yearly (primary cardholder only). Premium lounge upgrades 4 per quarter (16 yearly), 4 valet services and 4 premium car parking services on activation each year.
- Porter: 2 services for each INR100,000 annual spend up to 8 at INR400,000 spend. Pranaam: 1 service on activation, second after INR300,000 spend.
- BookMyShow: BOGO, second ticket discount up to INR500, twice monthly, minimum two tickets; from 1-Apr-2026 requires INR25,000 spend in preceding quarter. Add-on holders must individually meet the spend gate.
- Fuel waiver: 1% on fuel transactions up to INR4,000 transaction value; no monthly rupee cap published.
- Eligibility: Indian citizen over age 21; salaried monthly income INR75,000; self-employed annual income INR900,000. Schema cannot model citizenship separate from residency or the precise age bound, so those structured fields remain null.

## Conflicts and limits

Product page says annual-waiver spend is measured in a calendar year, but specific card terms say preceding anniversary year. Product page says points are credited at billing-cycle end; card terms allow up to 45 business days after the statement. The participating-merchant list linked in card terms is not archived. Exact interaction between 7-point ecosystem rate and 0.5-point utility/insurance rate for Adani electricity is not stated. Card PDF has no visible revision date; YAML effective date is a verification snapshot, not an issuer effective date.
