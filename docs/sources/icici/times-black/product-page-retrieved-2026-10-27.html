    Times Black Credit Card FAQs

    What is Times Black ICICI Bank credit card?

    The Times Black ICICI Bank Credit Card is an exclusive Credit Card issued by ICICI Bank in association with Times Internet Ltd. and Visa. Designed for Ultra-High Net Worth Individuals (UHNIs), this card offers premium benefits across various categories, including luxury stays, travel experiences, lifestyle and mobility. 

    How long are Milestone Benefits available to eligible cardholders?

    Milestone Benefits can be accessed through the Times Black app for a period of six months, starting from the date the eligible cardholder is notified about the respective milestone by ICICI Bank.

    How to avail Complimentary International Airport lounge access?

    Enjoy unlimited complimentary accesses to international airport lounges with Times Black ICICI Bank Credit Card.

    Priority Pass Membership

    • Complimentary Priority Pass Membership for both the primary cardholder and Plus cardholder with Times Black ICICI Bank Credit Card
    • Access 1,900+ lounges in 145+ countries worldwide
    • Present your Priority Pass Membership Card at the participating international lounges.
    • For complete list of international airport lounges visit https://www.prioritypass.com/airport-lounges

    What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?

    For lounge visits within India, Primary and Add-on Cardholders can avail unlimited complimentary lounge access through Times Black ICICI Bank Credit Card. Please swipe the Times Black ICICI Bank Credit Card to avail the complimentary lounge access. To view the complete list of lounges, click here.

    How are ICICI Bank reward points earned? Are there any limits or exclusions?

    You can earn 2% ICICI Bank reward points on all eligible domestic transactions. However, there is a cap of 5,000 reward points per statement cycle for the following categories:

    • Utility payments

    • Insurance premium payments

    • Education payments

    • Government payments (such as property tax, advance tax, fees, court payments, postal payments, etc.)

    Additionally, ICICI Bank reward points will not be earned on fuel transactions, rent payments or transactions converted into EMIs. The points earned on such transactions will be reversed.

    What is the value of 1 ICICI Bank reward point, and how can it be redeemed?

    The value of 1 ICICI Bank reward point can be up to ₹ 1. However, when redeeming ICICI Bank reward points against your statement, the redemption rate is 1 Reward Point (RP) = ₹0.40.

    Does the Times Black Credit Card offer any luxury Gift Card benefits?

    Yes, as a Times Black Credit Card holder, you receive a TATA CLiQ Luxury Gift Card worth ₹10,000, which can be used to shop for premium luxury items and experiences.

    What are the Times Black Credit Card benefits related to international experiences?

    With the Times Black Credit Card, you can enjoy international experiences and benefits worth ₹10,000 on Klook, allowing you to explore exciting activities and attractions worldwide.

    What luxury stay benefits does the Times Black Credit Card provide?

    The Times Black Credit Card offers a one-night luxury stay at  Ayatana or Lohono worth ₹20,000, providing an unforgettable getaway experience in a luxurious setting.

    What are the charges applicable for the Times Black ICICI Bank Credit Card?

    Joining Fee: ₹ 20,000 + GST

    Annual Fee: ₹ 20,000 + GST

    How can I get the annual fee waiver?

    You can get your annual fee waived from the 2nd year onwards on minimum spends of ₹25,00,000 made in the previous year.

    When will I get my joining benefits for the Times Black ICICI Bank Credit Card?

    You will receive an e-mail regarding the same within 45 business days after the payment of the joining fee.

    How do I complete my video KYC verification?

    You are required to provide your PAN Card and Aadhaar Card details followed by a video-based verification with an ICICI Bank employee. You will be required to accept a call request from the ICICI Bank employee and complete your video KYC by answering a few questions asked by them and showing the original PAN Card. Video KYC is available 24x7 on all days.

    How will I get the PIN for my Credit Card?

    You can generate the PIN in any of the following ways:

    • Using the iMobile app: Go to Services > Card PIN Services > Generate Credit Card PIN

    • Using Net Banking: Log into Net Banking > Generate Card PIN > Credit Card PIN > Select Card and enter CVV > Submit > Enter OTP > Enter PIN > Generate Now

    • Using the Interactive Voice Response (IVR) system: Call Customer Care on 1800 1080 and follow the instructions.
