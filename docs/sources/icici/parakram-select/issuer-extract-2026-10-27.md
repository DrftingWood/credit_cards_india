## Supported record values

- Current card page states no joining or annual fee, 3.5% forex markup and monthly finance charge 3.75%; MITC supports cash advance and related general charges. Up to three add-on cards are free in card T&C.
- Card-specific rewards table: 2 RP/INR100 on other categories, 10 RP/INR100 on grocery and department stores (1,000 point monthly cap), zero on insurance, utility, international, cash advance, ATM, EMI, balance transfer, PLCC, fees and charges. Live card FAQ also explicitly includes CSD in accelerated categories.
- Redemption fee is INR99 plus GST. Current issuer FAQ says reward points have no equivalent currency value and value varies by product/service/catalogue; removed fixed INR values.
- Domestic lounge: two quarterly visits after INR75,000 spend in a calendar quarter, available next quarter, primary cardholder only (live card page).
- Current live card summary and FAQ state two international visits/year; detailed card-specific T&C says one/year. Record follows the current page/FAQ and the conflict remains open.
- Golf: live detailed FAQ says one round/lesson for each INR50,000 eligible retail spend in previous calendar month and maximum one round/month; card T&C says maximum four rounds/month. Record follows the live FAQ pending clarification.
- Card T&C insurance: personal accident INR200,000 and air accident INR2,000,000; insurance not applicable during service, only personal/non-service events. Fuel waiver 1%, INR400-INR4,000, INR250 monthly cap, all petrol pumps.
- Eligibility: current card page says Indian citizen over 21 and Indian Defence or Paramilitary personnel; numeric income/score cutoffs not published. No current evidence establishes an issued network/tier or exact launch date, so those fields are unknown.

## Known source conflicts / limits

The current card page is internally inconsistent: top summary and FAQ give two international lounge visits/year, while the linked card T&C gives one. Golf maximum is one/month in live detailed FAQ versus four/month in card T&C. Structured values follow current detailed FAQ/page statements and both conflicts are preserved above. The current page advertises two international visits and is primary issuer material, but the older or linked T&C may govern contractual entitlement; this remains unresolved. Network, tier and launch date were cleared rather than inferred from old third-party descriptions.
