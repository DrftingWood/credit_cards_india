SBI Card and lndiGo Form Strategic Partnership; Launch lndiGo SBI Card
                  -Customers can earn upto 29,000 bonus lndiGo 8/uChips per annum-
   -Enjoy upto 7% lndiGo 8/uChips on spends made across all services available on /ndiGo's ecosystem-


New Delhi, September 25, 2025: SBI Card, India's largest pure-play credit card issuer and lndiGo,
India's preferred airline, today launched the 'lndiGo SBI Card', as part of the lndiGo BluChip loyalty
program. This premium co-branded credit card has been launched in two variants, lndiGo SBI Card
and lndiGo SBI Card ELITE, to offer highly rewarding experience for avid travellers. Customers will
earn rewards on all spends made on offerings within lndiGo's ecosystem, and on hotel and travel
bookings, among other categories. Customers can enroll for the card digitally via SBI Card SPRINT,
by visiting the SBI Card website SBI Card.com, and can also opt to enroll offline by visiting SBI Card
retail kiosks.

lndiGo SBI Card unlocks a superior travel experience with an array of thoughtful benefits designed
to make every journey more rewarding and seamless. Cardholders can enjoy 7% rewards on spends
made using the lndiGo SBI Card ELITE and 3% rewards on spends made using the lndiGo SBI Card,
in lndiGo's ecosystem. They can also avail 3% rewards on spends made using the lndiGo SBI Card
ELITE and 2% on the lndiGo SBI Card, for hotel and travel bookings on other platforms. lndiGo SBI
Card ELITE and lndiGo SBI Card customers also enjoy 2%, and 1 % rewards, respectively, for spends
made on all other categories.

Rewards earned on lndiGo SBI Card ELITE and lndiGo SBI Card are more than just points; they serve
as a complete travel currency. The rewards are in the form of lndiGo BluChips, credited to the
cardholder's lndiGo BluChip account in every statement cycle. Cardholders can redeem them across
lndiGo's extensive services including flight bookings, hotel bookings, seat upgrades, meals, and
vouchers for services like Fast Forward, among many more. By combining spends with travel
privileges, lndiGo SBI Card enables customers to earn up to 29,000 bonus lndiGo BluChips every
year.

Speaking on the launch Ms. Salila Pande, MD & CEO, SBI Card said, "At SB! Card, we have
observed that travel constitutes an important spend category for our cardholders. With increased
exposure, and growing disposable incomes, tech-savvy customers in India are increasingly seeking
holistic travel experiences. As a customer-centric organisation, we truly understand the evolving
requirements and hence have formed this strategic partnership with /ndiGo to launch the /ndiGo
SB/ Card and address such needs. /ndiGo SB/ Card is a powerful product offering unparalleled

benefits, ensuring every journey becomes more rewarding and seamless. We are confident that this
premium credit card will be a key part of our strong co-brand card portfolio."

Mr. Pieter Elbers, Chief Executive Officer, lndiGo said: "With JndiGo BluChip, we are constantly
exploring ways to enhance the value proposition for our loyal customers. Our partnership with SB/
Card is an important step in that journey. This co-branded credit card is designed to bring significant
value addition, convenience, and benefits to our frequent flyers, transforming everyday spending

With an aim to redefine travel experiences, the lndiGo SBI Card offers significant welcome and
milestone benefits. Customers will receive up to 5,000 lndiGo BluChips on first year annual fee
payment and first transaction. Packed with exclusive milestone benefits, customers can earn 2500
lndiGo BluChips on annual spends starting from t2 lakh on the lndiGo SBI Card and 6000 lndiGo
BluChips on annual spends starting from t3 lakh on the lndiGo SBI Card ELITE.

Additionally, the co-branded credit card enables customers to avail renewal benefit of 2500 lndiGo
BluChips on annual fee payment of lndiGo SBI Card and 5000 lndiGo BluChips and 1 lndiGo 6E Eats
voucher on annual fee payment of lndiGo SBI Card ELITE. The credit card offers domestic airport
lounge access on both variants and complimentary international airport lounge access on the lndiGo
SBI Card ELITE, adding more to the premium experience. The lndiGo SBI Card ELITE also provides
travel insurance benefits for flight cancellations, lost credit cards, and lost baggage.

The joining and annual renewal fee of lndiGo SBI Card is t1 ,499 plus applicable taxes and that of
lndiGo SBI Card ELITE is t4,999 plus applicable taxes. Both variants of the contactless card are
available on Mastercard as well as RuPay payment platforms.

all major cardholder' segments in terms of income profile and lifestyle. The brand has a wide base
of over 21 MM+ cards in force. SBI Card has a diversified customer acquisition network that enables
the organisation to engage prospective customers across multiple channels. SBI Card is a
technology driven company.
