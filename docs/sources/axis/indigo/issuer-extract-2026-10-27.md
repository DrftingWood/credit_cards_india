## Record comparison and changes

- **Variant/network:** Axis launched the card on both RuPay and Visa. Cleared `network: rupay` and `network_tier: select` to unknown because this record combines both variants and current issuer pages do not state a single tier. Retained RuPay/UPI tags as supported card features. Launch date 18 February 2026 is confirmed by the launch release.
- **Fees:** Standard joining and annual fees are INR 799 plus GST; current product page advertises a limited-period NIL offer. The current card terms specify the lifetime-free offer for applications from 1 September to 31 October 2026, with more than INR 1,500 eligible spend in the first 30 days for the separate 1,200-BluChip activation voucher. Standard fee-paid welcome, renewal, milestone and regular earn benefits do not apply to lifetime-free/first-year-free cards. The typed fee model cannot encode application-window LTF eligibility, so standard fees remain structured and offer details stay in notes. The page also lists a 3.5% DCC markup and additional threshold fees for rent (1%, uncapped), third-party-app education (1%), wallet (1% from INR 10,000 cumulative per statement), fuel (1% from INR 50,000 cumulative per statement), and utilities (1% from INR 25,000 cumulative per statement); these conditional fees are captured in notes. The published late-fee table leaves exactly INR 500 unassigned (nil below 500; INR 500 from 501).
- **Base and accelerated rewards:** Confirmed 1 BluChip/INR 100 base, 2/INR 100 on listed grocery/dining MCCs, and 3/INR 100 for IndiGo channel transactions at MCC 4511 with merchant-name keyword IndiGo or Interglobe. Grocery MCCs: 5399, 5422, 5441, 5411, 5451, 5462, 5499, 5999. Dining MCCs: 5812, 5814, 5813. IndiGo-channel accelerated earning is uncapped. BluChip earns are credited within 30 working days from statement generation; refunds/reversals and later EMI conversions can reverse/adjust prior earns. EMI transactions, cash withdrawals, fees/charges/GST and temporary dispute credits are ineligible.
- **UPI cap:** UPI earns across base and grocery/dining accelerated categories share a 750-BluChip statement-month cap; IndiGo-channel spend is excluded. UPI earns over the cap do not count toward milestone spending. Encoded this as a reward capping rule, rather than a false universal cap on grocery/dining or a separate UPI earn rate.
- **Exclusions:** Confirmed fuel, wallet, rent, government, insurance, utilities including telecom, jewellery, gift cards, bridge/road/tolls and all EMI types. Removed unsupported fixed rent MCC 6513 because current card terms identify excluded categories without a fixed MCC list.
- **BluChip value/redemption:** Axis/IndiGo terms state BluChips have no monetary value; IndiGo controls redemption pricing. Removed INR 1,200 equivalent values for benefits and the fixed INR 0.60 redemption rate; voucher quantities remain `reward_units: 1200`, while `value_inr` is null. The card's `unit_value_inr` / `unit_value_inr_realized` retain the separately documented INR 0.60 / 0.45 estimates for calculator comparison, not guaranteed issuer values. The official IndiGo FAQ sets a 500-BluChip starting minimum and limits redemption to eligible one-way IndiGo marketed-and-operated flights; taxes, fees, surcharges, add-ons and applicable redemption/convenience charges require cash.
- **Welcome and milestones:** Standard 1,200-BluChip welcome/renewal voucher requires fee payment within 120 days of charge, is issued within 30 days, is not available for LTF/FYF cards, and expires after six months. The LTF activation voucher is 1,200 BluChips for qualifying applications 1 Sep–31 Oct 2026 with more than INR 1,500 eligible first-30-day spend; qualifying exclusions include EMIs, ATM cash, fees/charges/GST, and temporary disputed-transaction credits/reversals; issued within 90 days after the offer period and valid six months. Annual milestones: 1,200 BluChips at INR 1.5 lakh and INR 3 lakh eligible anniversary-year spends, with fee paid for that year; checked at statement generation, issued within 30 days after the next statement, valid six months. Capped UPI earns above 750/month do not count toward milestone spend.
- **Lounge:** One domestic visit per quarter after INR 50,000 eligible spend in the previous three calendar months. The current lounge program waives the spend gate for the card-issuance month and the following three calendar months. Accompanying guests pay lounge rates; no complimentary guest quota or operator is established by the reviewed sources. No card-specific fuel-surcharge waiver is advertised in the current product page or card T&Cs, so it remains null.
- **Eligibility:** Current product page says primary cardholder age 18–70 and resident India; bank approval remains discretionary. Card-specific income and score cutoffs are unpublished and remain null.

## Schema and remaining evidence limits

The card schema stores a single network, so the card's dual RuPay/Visa issuance is recorded as unknown in structured network fields. It cannot structurally encode the dated lifetime-free campaign, the preceding-three-calendar-month lounge gate with its new-card exception, or the shared channel-specific UPI cap; these conditions are described in notes/capping rules. BluChip redemption value varies and must not be presented as guaranteed INR cash value. The IndiGo FAQ adds a 500-chip starting minimum and excludes 6E add-ons from points redemption. The project's numerical card calculator uses separate third-party-supported INR 0.60/0.45 unit estimates from the IndiGo loyalty-program record.
