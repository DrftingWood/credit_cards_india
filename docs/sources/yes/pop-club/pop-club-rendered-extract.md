## Current product page

- Current issuer listing offers POP-CLUB; application is through the POP Club partner app/site. Network is RuPay; issuer does not state a RuPay tier or exact launch date.
- Joining fee is NIL; renewal fee is INR 399 plus taxes for standard-fee cards. The page also advertises a limited-time lifetime-free offer. The 2026 MITC schedule gives the INR 399 annual fee and INR 150,000 annual spend condition; current card page says customers who cross that threshold are not charged the fee. Lifetime-free cohorts still qualify for the 1,500 POPcoin milestone.
- Card page states 3.99% monthly finance charge. Current consolidated MITC v18 lists 3.5% forex for POP-CLUB after 15-Jun-2026 and 2.5%/INR 650-min cash advance fee. A prior card-specific MITC lists INR 500 minimum; v18 is the later common schedule.
- Eligible net salary: INR 25,000 monthly for salaried applicants; ITR INR 750,000 or more for self-employed applicants. Age 21-60. Existing YES BANK credit cardholders are ineligible. Minimum bureau score and residency requirement are not stated.

## Rewards and benefits

- 10 POPcoins per INR 100 on online/e-commerce spend; 2 POPcoins per INR 100 on other eligible spend, including UPI. Minimum transaction to earn POPcoins is INR 100. Points apply only to personal spends; ordinary earning cap is not stated.
- No POPcoins for fuel, rent, wallet loads, government/marketing-advertising transactions, ATM withdrawals, utilities, insurance premiums, POI funding, EMI and, effective 01-Jun-2026, education. Issuer-published MCCs are preserved in the card YAML. Issuer FAQ says POPcoins expire 365 days after credit.
- POP's [current site](https://mypop.in/) states 1 POPcoin = INR 1, redeemable across the POP ecosystem and partner brands. POPcoins are not cash-convertible; redemption options vary. YES Bank MITC v18 charges INR 100 plus GST per reward redemption request.
- The card-specific issuer FAQ says POPcoins expire 365 days after credit. This conflicts with the general MITC v18 three-year term for reward points; the card-specific POPcoin term is followed in the record.
- Annual anniversary-year eligible spend of INR 150,000 earns 1,500 POPcoins and waives the INR 399 renewal fee for standard-fee accounts. Lifetime-free accounts remain eligible for the milestone.
- Current page advertises a joining-benefit bundle worth approximately INR 2,000, including partner benefits. Offer eligibility, fulfillment and expiry terms may vary and were not visible in the rendered page.

## Fuel-waiver conflict

- The current POP-CLUB card-page terms explicitly state that from 01-Jun-2026 the 1% surcharge waiver applies to INR 1,000-INR 3,500 fuel transactions and is capped at INR 100 per statement cycle. GST on surcharge is not waived.
- Consolidated MITC v18 also includes the new transaction range, but its common waiver-cap table continues to show INR 300 for POP-CLUB. The card-specific current page is represented in the YAML and the INR 100 vs INR 300 conflict is retained here. The page does not specify UPI fuel-waiver handling.

## Remaining unknowns
