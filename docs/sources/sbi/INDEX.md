### Corrections and status

- SBI's current customer notice says new Club Vistara SBI Card PRIME issuance stopped 28-Sep-2024. Set discontinued date to that date; the previous 12-Nov-2024 was the Vistara/Air India merger date, not the card issuance stop date. Launch date retained as 26-Nov-2019 from SBI's official media timeline.
- Air India says the existing co-brand card program's benefits continued through 31-Mar-2026, and cards/points/vouchers moved to Maharaja Club. CV point balances transferred 1:1. Card program benefit snapshots now end 31-Mar-2026.
- Corrected fees to INR2,999 joining, annual and renewal. SBI waived renewal fees for renewals after 31-Mar-2025 when the renewal ticket and milestone-ticket benefits were discontinued. The previous `renewal_fee_inr: 0` was incorrect as the base renewal price.
- SBI card T&C states 4 CV points per INR200 eligible spend, explicitly says points have no cash/monetary value, and lists excluded cash advances, fees/charges, disputed transactions, Flexipay, merchant EMI, e-wallet MCCs 6540/6541, rent MCC 6513 (from 15-Apr-2024), government MCCs 9399/9311 (from 15-Jul-2024). Removed speculative fixed INR0.55/INR0.50 point values. Air India documents 1:1 migration; no direct fixed cash value is asserted.
- Recorded the 8 domestic and 4 international lounge visit allotments with quarterly caps. Removed INR72,000 and INR7,500 baggage-cover amounts because they were not supported by the currently linked card T&C. Retained the INR100,000 lost-card liability from T&C.
- SBI FAQ lists minimum age 18; income and credit-score cutoffs remain unknown.
- SBI and Air India merger pages do not publish a post-merger standalone SBI reward schedule; continuation of the 4/INR200 earning rate after 12-Nov-2024 through 31-Mar-2026 is treated as an inference from their statement that existing card benefits/terms continued, and is explicitly noted in the YAML.
