# AU Laksya — issuer comparison (2026-11-01)

Compared the current AU Laksya product page and FAQ, Laksya lounge terms, current 24-Mar-2026 Card Member Agreement, AU Rewardz FAQ, and AU KFS/Schedule of Charges v1.9 (30-Sep-2026).

## Fees and eligibility

- INR 1,000 joining and annual fees plus GST. No joining waiver. Renewal waiver at INR 200,000 retail spend in the card anniversary year.
- Current AU KFS general charges: 3.49% forex markup, 3.75% monthly finance rate, cash advance 2.5% or INR 500 minimum. Card page gives salaried age 21–60, self-employed 25–65, add-on over 18, Indian resident; income and score floors are not published.
- Exact network/tier is not established per issued card. AU lounge program admits Visa/RuPay AU cards; no single network/tier inferred.

## Rewards

- Base: 2 points/INR 100 on other eligible retail; 1 point/INR 100 on insurance, utility and telecom, capped at 100 points per transaction.
- Grocery/departmental: 5 points/INR 100, shared grocery/departmental statement-cycle cap 1,000 points. Contactless: 5 points/INR 100, no maximum stated.
- Overall card cap: 25,000 points per statement cycle under Member Agreement. Exclusions: cash, rent, education/government, EMI, fuel, BBPS, transportation and quasi-cash. Reward calculations on INR 100 increments follow general program terms.
- General AU Rewardz catalog value: INR 0.0025 per point, e-vouchers INR 0.002. Minimum redemption 200 points; INR 99 + GST per successful redemption.
- The calculator schema cannot isolate contactless channel and transaction-level 100-point cap; current file documents these as non-modeled rules to avoid overestimating mixed-category spend.

## Benefits

- Welcome choice: 4,000 points or INR 1,000 voucher on activation and first transaction within 30 days.
- Current product page says two domestic lounge visits per quarter after INR 50,000 spend in the previous quarter (8/year). The lounge PDF dated 28-Nov-2025 instead says spend in the same calendar quarter, plus first-30-day onboarding waiver. This wording conflicts; the dataset uses the current page and records the PDF discrepancy.
- Fuel surcharge waiver 1%, on INR 400–5,000, maximum INR 150 per statement cycle.
- Merchant offers: monthly 15% discounts at Amazon Fresh/Amazon Now and Zomato (INR 250 cap each, INR 500 minimum), plus District BOGO (INR 250 cap, INR 250 minimum); once per month, add-ons included.
- Issuer advertises insurance, card liability, credit shield and device protection but product page does not publish policy limits/conditions. Values remain null.
