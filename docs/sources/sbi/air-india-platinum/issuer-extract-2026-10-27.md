# Air India SBI Platinum: issuer comparison (27 Oct 2026)

## Sources reviewed

- [SBI customer notices](https://www.sbicard.com/en/customer-notices.page.page): issuance of the Air India SBI Platinum Card stopped on 10-Oct-2024; accelerated Air India earn changed effective 31-Mar-2025.
- [SBI card-specific Air India reward terms](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/personal/credit-cards/travel/air-india-sbi-platinum-tc.pdf): 5 points per INR100 on Air India tickets via website, app, booking offices or call centres using primary or add-on cards; applies to base fare and self/others. 2 points post initially and 3 after merchant-ID mapping, within 60 working days, subject to Maharaja Club number, KYC and exact name matching.
- [SBI Air India card terms](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/ekit-tncs/Air-india-tnc.pdf): ordinary earn 2 points per INR100; reward exclusions include cash advances, balance transfers, Flexipay, fuel, merchant EMI, e-wallet MCC 6540/6541, rent MCC 6513 from 15-Apr-2024, and government MCC 9399/9311 from 15-Jul-2024. Air Miles redemption requires 5,000 to 10,000 points under Air India terms; transferred points lapse after 12 months. Air-mile redemption must happen before voluntary closure while the SBI card is active.
- [SBI lounge terms](https://www.sbicard.com/lounge-benefits): Air India SBI Platinum is in the eligible set; from 10-Jan-2026 the quota is 4 domestic visits per calendar year, max 1 per quarter, replacing 8/year, max 2/quarter.
- [SBI MITC](https://www.sbicard.com/en/most-important-terms-and-conditions.page): current joining/annual fee of INR1,499; 3.5% DCC; 1% fuel surcharge is offered only on select cards and card-specific waiver details apply. Finance charges are 3.75% monthly on unsecured cards from 01-Nov-2024.
- [SBI legacy card-upgrade page](https://www.sbicard.com/en/personal/benefits/card-upgrade.page): advertises 5,000 welcome points, 2,000 anniversary points, 2 points per INR100 and 15,000 bonus points at annual spend of INR3 lakh. Its 10-point Air India earn claim conflicts with the later notice and card-specific addendum; used only for welcome/anniversary and to flag milestone ambiguity.
- [SBI launch release](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/who-we-are/news/2013/SBI_Cards_launches_new_co-branded_credit_card_with_Air_India.pdf): launch date 16-Sep-2013; original milestone schedule 5,000 at INR2 lakh and 10,000 at INR3 lakh.

## Record changes and unresolved details

- Marked the card discontinued from new issuance on 10-Oct-2024. This status describes product availability; issuer terms and benefits remain relevant to existing accounts.
- Set the verified launch date, retained the current MITC fees, and updated monthly finance charges to the post-01-Nov-2024 rate.
- Removed unsupported fixed INR values for Reward Points. Kept the existing shared loyalty-program reference because its dataset ID remains `air-india-flying-returns`; the program's current name/currency is Maharaja Club/Maharaja Points.
- Updated Air India earn from legacy 15-to-5 terms to the current 5/INR100 addendum and recorded its delayed 3-point posting and eligibility constraints. Added explicit reward exclusions and relevant MCC codes.
- Updated lounge quantity and calendar/quota conditions effective 10-Jan-2026. Added the issuer-advertised 2,000-point anniversary gift.
- Kept the 2013 milestone thresholds (5,000 at INR2 lakh; 10,000 at INR3 lakh), but marked their current interpretation unresolved: SBI's legacy upgrade page now advertises 15,000 at INR3 lakh without saying whether this is cumulative or incremental.
- The current sources do not establish card-specific income or credit-score cutoffs. Fuel-waiver transaction range/cap is retained from the card's legacy public material but lacks a current card-specific issuer schedule; treat these numeric limits as lower-confidence legacy terms.
