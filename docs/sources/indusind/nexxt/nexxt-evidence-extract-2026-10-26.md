## Field evidence and conflicts

- The product page states 1 Reward Point per INR150; the launch release is dated 14-Nov-2018 and identifies Mastercard partnership. The Dec-2025 benefit guide image shows the Mastercard Platinum face, supporting the modeled network and network tier.
- The current Pay by Rewards terms state INR0.75 per point, 500 points minimum, POS use only, and exclude e-commerce, IVR and MOTO. The Dec-2025 benefit guide states INR1 per point for Pay with Rewards and INR0.75 per point for cash credit. Since the POS values conflict, the YAML leaves the general unit value and POS redemption value null. Cash-credit value remains INR0.75 per point.
- The cash-credit terms state a 500-point minimum and a 2,500-point maximum per calendar month. A separate bank update lists a 2,500 monthly capping figure for Nexxt but does not say whether it caps points earned or redeemed. The record therefore does not assert that earning is uncapped.
- The product page and benefit guide publish travel cover amounts for lost baggage (INR100,000), delayed baggage (INR25,000), lost passport (INR50,000), lost ticket (INR25,000) and missed connection (INR25,000). Full policy conditions and exclusions were not verified.
- The product page still advertises personal air-accident cover of INR25 lakh. The bank-wide card update says that cover was discontinued effective 21-Dec-2025, so it is not modeled as current.
- The card page has no lounge access entry in its current visible benefits and no card-specific age, income, credit-score or residency criteria were established. These remain unknown.
- The current MITC lists INR10,000 joining fee and NIL annual fee; actual joining fee may vary by the fee plan offered at application.
- The current fuel schedule supports a 1% waiver on eligible INR500-INR3,000 transactions, capped at INR250 per billing cycle.
