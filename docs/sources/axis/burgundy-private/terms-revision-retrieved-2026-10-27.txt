 
 
 
Revision of Burgundy Private -The One Card  Terms and Conditions 
 
 
While you continue to enjoy the benefits on your  Burgundy Private - The One  Card, the 
following revisions will be applicable on your card basis the effective dates below: 
 
Effective 28-08-2026 
 
• When you make a payment towards your credit card outstanding balance, the payment 
will be adjusted across different components of your outstanding balance in a defined 
order. Payments will be adjusted first towards applicable fees and charges (including 
taxes), followed by EMIs, interest charges, purchase transactions, and cash advances. 
 
Effective 01-09-2026 
 
• The Burgundy Private - The One Card remains complimentary for Burgundy Private 
clients who maintain a Total Relationship Value* (TRV) of INR 5 Crore and above with 
us (excluding demat). 
 
A revised annual fee of INR 1 Lakh + GST will be applicable to your Burgundy Private - 
The One Card in case your TRV falls below INR 5 Crore**. 
 
For complete details on this fee update, please refer to the Most Important Terms and 
Conditions available here. 
 
* To understand how TRV is calculated, click here.  
 
** TRV maintenance will be checked for the month prior to your card anniversary 
month, with the applicable fee, charged in the card anniversary month. The annual fee 
has been revised from INR 50,000 to INR 1 lakh.  
 
 
Effective 15-11-2026 
 
• Complimentary memberships linked to your card will be renewed only with your explicit 
consent. Renewal details, including consent options, will be shared closer to the 
renewal date. Please click here to view the list of applicable memberships.
