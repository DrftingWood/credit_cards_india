## Remaining conflicts and schema limits

- The minimum FD amount (INR 50,000 vs INR 1 lakh), forex markup (0% vs stale 1.49%), golf rounds (4 vs 12) and trip-cancellation policy end date conflict within currently linked issuer materials.
- Dual network issuance, UPI payment-rail overrides, all merchant-category-code boundaries, and loyalty-tier BluChip bonuses do not fit the current single-network/category schema completely.
- A separate future October campaign was verified on the current page; the expired 1-Jun–30-Sep offer is not represented as active.

This comparison records the best-supported current values and visible issuer conflicts; it is not an assurance that terms will remain unchanged after the review date.
