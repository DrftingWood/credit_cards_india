No points for wallet load, fuel, rent, cash withdrawal, insurance, repayments, jewellery, education, government services, EMI, and RuPay credit-card UPI transactions below INR 2,000. Points post within 7 working days of the following week. No card-specific MCC list or universal INR point value is published; value and MCCs remain unknown. Generic terms may include additional categories, but exact Kwik applicability is not sufficiently card-specific to encode as certain.

Card is virtual, UPI-linkable through RuPay. No lounge access or other quantified evergreen benefits listed.
