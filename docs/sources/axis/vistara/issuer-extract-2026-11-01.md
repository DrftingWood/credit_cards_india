# Axis Bank Vistara cards — issuer source review (2026-11-01)

Applies to Vistara, Vistara Signature and Vistara Infinite records.

## Sources compared

- [Axis revised Vistara card terms after the Air India merger](https://www.axisbank.com/docs/default-source/default-document-library/credit-cards/terms-and-conditions-axis-bank-vistara-cards.pdf)
- Current Axis card pages and current Axis MITC for archived product and fee references.

## Verified status and changes

- Axis states new sourcing is discontinued, while existing cardholders may continue using their cards for domestic and international transactions and earn Maharaja Points on eligible spends until further notice. This is an interim arrangement; the dataset's `discontinued` status describes issuance, not immediate account closure.
- At the next renewal cycle, annual fees are waived. Complimentary Maharaja Club tier memberships, renewal ticket vouchers and spend-milestone ticket vouchers are discontinued. Other listed lounge, golf and dining benefits continue under the interim terms.
- Eligible-spend exclusions published in the revised terms include rent, wallet loads, utility bills, government services, insurance, jewellery, gold, silver, precious stones/metals and watches. Maharaja Club governs point validity and redemption.
- Corrected all three records' merger-transition notes to distinguish discontinued sourcing from continued existing-card use; refreshed source retrieval and verification dates.

## Limits

The interim terms remain “until further notice.” Variant-specific historic earn rates and legacy benefits are retained as card terms, with the post-merger exclusions and discontinuations described above. No new-card application should be inferred from the archived card pages.
