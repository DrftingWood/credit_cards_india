# AU Xcite Ace insurance evidence review (2026-11-01)

## Issuer sources checked

- [Current Xcite Ace product page](https://www.au.bank.in/personal-banking/credit-cards/swipeup-program/xcite-ace-credit-card), accessed 2026-11-01.
- [Insurance terms linked from that page](https://www.au.bank.in/pdf/xcite-ultra-credit-card/card-liability-insurance-tnc.pdf), which identifies itself as last updated 16-Apr-2021.
- [Device Protection Plan link](https://sr.au.bank.in/device-protection), which redirects to a registered-mobile-number login.

## Findings

- The current product page advertises INR 50 lakh air-accident cover, credit shield up to INR 2 lakh, purchase protection up to INR 25,000, lost-card/counterfeit/skimming/online-fraud cover, baggage loss/delay, flight delay, passport loss and plane-hijack cover. It does not publish current policy wording or most limits.
- The linked 2021 policy is stale. Its general provisions restrict cover to the primary cardholder and active cards, require claim documents within 60 days of intimation, and state general exclusions. Air accident cover is accidental death only and requires the ticket to be purchased using the AU card. Purchase protection is limited to specified household goods and fire/allied perils or burglary at the registered residence for 90 days; mobiles, laptops and tablets are excluded.
- The 2021 policy's global cover provisions require international airline travel booked with the AU card. Baggage delay requires more than 12 hours and reimburses essential emergency purchases; passport/visa replacement expenses are described; hijack cover is USD 250 per hour up to an unspecified sum insured. The accessible text does not establish current separate flight-delay conditions or current limits.
- The device-plan link is gated behind mobile-number login; its terms and coverage values could not be checked publicly.

## Data treatment

Retained the benefit categories and amounts currently advertised on the issuer page, while labeling the detailed policy conditions unconfirmed because the only linked wording dates from 2021. Did not promote the stale wording into a current guarantee, infer unpublished limits or assign values to gated device protection. Current verification of the in-force policy and insurer schedule remains open.
