## Reconciliation

- Fees: INR 2,499 annual fee, waived after INR 250,000 eligible anniversary-year spend. The limited-period zero joining/first-year offer began 1-Sep-2026; its end date is not published. Standard joining fee waiver and cash/finance/forex charges are captured in the fee source notes.
- Rewards: 3 points/INR 100 base; 5X on eligible e-commerce, international, dining and travel transactions, with 5,000 accelerated points per statement cap. Cashback/statement-credit value is INR 0.25/point. The shared issuer T&C specify transaction minimum, MCC/channel eligibility, exclusions, point forfeiture/expiry events, 1,000-point redemption minimum and separate logged-in SmartDeal travel earn. These channel-specific extras are not treated as general purchase earn.
- Benefits: 10,000 welcome points after INR 50,000 eligible spend within 60 days; 20,000 anniversary-year points after INR 500,000 annual eligible spend; unlimited domestic lounge access after INR 75,000 preceding-quarter spend; District movie BOGO capped at INR 250/month; and 1% fuel waiver capped at INR 250 per statement. The 15-Jul-2026 revision and Eterna milestone PDF support the additional lounge/FITPASS conditions recorded in YAML.
- Insurance: accident cover ceased for newly issued Eterna cards from 25-Aug-2026 per the current product terms; the record does not carry forward historical insurance as a current benefit.
- Eligibility: the reviewed issuer page lists age 25–55 and net annual income above INR 1,200,000 for salaried or self-employed applicants. No numeric credit-score minimum is published. The universal application form lists Visa, Mastercard and RuPay variants; the YAML row represents Visa and leaves tier unset.

## Limits retained

The live first-year fee offer has no published end date. Lounge guest entitlement and concierge status are not established in reviewed issuer material. Product/schema treatment of multiple networks is limited to a single represented variant. Reward eligibility remains dependent on issuer-assigned MCC and channel flags.
