# Federal Bank — MCC map (2026-07-03)

MCC handling applied across the Federal Bank India catalogue during the 2026-07 audit.

## Universal rent exclusion
Every Federal card carries `mcc_exclusions: ['6513']` (residential rent, MCC 6513).

## Category-MCC accelerators (FedRewards; 1 RP ≈ ₹0.25)
- **Celesta** — 3x on Dining + 3x on international spends; 5 RP/₹150 base.
- **Imperio** — 3x on Health-Care & Grocery + 2x on Dining; 4 RP/₹150 base;
  utility bill-payments (electricity/gas) also rewarded.
- **Signet** — 3x on select category + 2x + 1x base (3 RP/₹150).
- **RuPay Signet** — 3x on Electronics & Apparel; UPI-enabled; 3 RP/₹150 base.
- **Scapia** — rewards ("Scapia coins") on all travel bookings via the Scapia app;
  zero forex.

## Spend-gated lounge & milestones
Federal ties lounge access and voucher milestones to quarterly spend thresholds:
- Celesta: intl lounge boosted at ₹60k/quarter.
- Imperio: 2 domestic lounge visits next quarter at ₹40k/quarter; Big Basket vouchers
  at ₹50k/quarter.
- Signet: vouchers at ₹20k/quarter.
- RuPay Signet: 1 domestic lounge visit next quarter at ₹20k/quarter.

## Common exclusions
Fuel, wallet-loads, insurance-premiums and EMI are excluded from base reward accrual
on Federal cards (in addition to rent).
