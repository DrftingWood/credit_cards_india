Rules Of Access Under The Program

  1. The Program is available till 31 st August, 2025 for Domestic lounge access, and till 14th April, 2025 for International lounge access.
  2. To access the Participating Airport Lounge, customer’s Card must be validated at point of entry by swiping the Card and the customer will be denied the complimentary entry if the Card authentication fails upon this swipe. Eligible Card along with boarding pass must be presented at the participating lounge.
  3. Lounge service will be available on a first come first serve basis. In case of full occupancy, customers may be requested to wait in queue.
  4. Participating Lounges reserve the right to enforce a maximum stay policy (usually 2 or 3 hours). It is at the discretion of the individual lounge operator who may impose a charge for extended stays.
  5. Every lounge follows its food offerings and child policy. Eligible customers should make prior inquiries at the lounge reception before entry.
  6. The entitled benefits for the card members (DBS Bank/DreamFolks Membership) are non-transferable.
  7. For domestic lounge access, Lounge staff will be checking customer’s eligibility to extend the lounge access and will record the usage via DreamFolks provided System.
  8. For domestic lounge access, if customer has done a pre-booking and generated QR code which is unused, the complimentary service will get retained post completion of QR code validity.
  9. Once the complimentary visits are exhausted, the visit gets charged as per the walk-in price of the lounge or the charges applicable on web-access portal/EDC machine (whichever medium customer is trying to purchase it from).
  10. The provision of free alcoholic drinks (wherever local law permits) is at the discretion of each Participating Lounge operator and in some cases may be limited. Eligible customers must make prior inquiries before ordering any separate alcoholic drinks apart from the free alcoholic drinks offered and will be responsible for paying any charges for additional consumption directly to the participating lounge.
  11. Lounge has the right to terminate the lounge visit early or to refuse entry to any Customer who is intoxicated or behaving in a disorderly fashion or is otherwise in violation of the lounge conditions and for any statutory, regulatory, or airport policy reasons including but not limited to health and safety policies or fire safety regulations.
  12. By availing the Lounge Service / Access, it is deemed that the Service Receiver and/ or Customer has before availing the Service read, understood, and agreed to be bound by, without limitation and/ or qualification, all terms and conditions associated with the Lounge Service/ Access.
  13. Eligible Cardholders will be allowed access to all Participating Airport Lounges under the Program, based on one of the following entry types:

    Entry Type 1

    1. For Domestic lounges: Complimentary entry for Eligible Cardholder only, (subject to a nominal swipe fee of INR 2). All Eligible Cards must be validated at point of entry by swiping a transaction of a nominal fee of INR 2
    2. For International Lounges: Please present your Eligible Card and boarding pass at the participating lounge and mention ‘LoungeKey’ at the reception. Card will be validated at point of entry. Amount equivalent to US$35 will be on hold and the hold amount will be released in your account within 30 working days post authentication regarding complimentary lounge access. Usage of the lounges outside India is subject to complimentary lounge access quotas mentioned above and any usage beyond such access quotas will be subject to full guest fee of US$35 per visit.

    Entry Type 2:

      Paid entry (after complimentary lounge access quota has been exhausted) for the Eligible Cardholder according to the respective prices of usage displayed at the Participating Airport Lounges, for itself or for its accompanying guests (payment will be made directly at the Participating Airport Lounge by the Eligible Cardholder).

      Notwithstanding anything herein, complimentary entry may be restricted or unavailable if DBS Bank or the card network (VISA/MasterCard/RuPay) withdraws the Program or any Participating Airport Lounge or changes eligibility criteria in relation to any Eligible Cardholder, for any reason at its sole and absolute discretion; or the allotted limit/quota of complimentary entry is exhausted on the Eligible Card as mentioned herein above or as determined by DBS Bank in its sole discretion and any attempted usage beyond such access limit/quota will be rejected by the Participating Airport Lounge; or If the maximum capacity for Eligible Cardholders or an Eligible Card has been reached at the relevant Participating Airport Lounge. In this regard it is to be noted that capacity limits at a Participating Airport Lounge may be different for different Eligible Card types. DBS Bank shall not be held responsible under any circumstances for any such unavailability.
