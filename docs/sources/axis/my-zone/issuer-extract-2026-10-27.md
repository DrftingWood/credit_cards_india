## Field-by-field reconciliation

- **Identity and network:** Active, unsecured MY ZONE card. Launch date not established in reviewed current product sources. Axis network-choice announcement lists Mastercard, Visa and RuPay as options for new applications/renewals; set `network: null` because an individual issued variant cannot be inferred. Network tier remains unknown.
- **Fees:** Current product page lists INR 500 joining and annual fees; no fee waiver in current KFS. Finance rate 3.75%/month (55.55% p.a.), cash advance 2.5%/minimum INR 500, forex 3.5%, and add-on joining/annual fees nil. It also lists INR 175 cash payment; replacement nil; duplicate statement, charge slip and outstation cheque waived; alerts free; hotlisting nil; balance enquiry waived; overlimit 2.5% minimum INR 500; cheque/auto-debit return 2% minimum INR 500; railway surcharge as prescribed; reward redemption fee without amount; and current late-fee slabs. INR 500 outstanding is not assigned to a late-fee slab. Current DCC is 3.5% plus GST. Other card-page charges: rent 1% per transaction; third-party education 1% per transaction; wallet 1% at cumulative INR 10,000/statement; fuel 1% at INR 50,000/statement; utilities 1% at INR 25,000/statement; online gaming 1% at INR 10,000/statement.
- **Rewards:** 4 EDGE Reward Points per INR 200. Current page explicitly excludes movies, fuel, insurance, wallet, rent, utilities, education, government, EMI, jewellery, gift cards, bridge fees and road fees/tolls. My Zone-specific 2025 revision additionally names utility/telecom, cash advances and repayments. The schema enum can express cash advance but has no movie or repayment category; those remain in explanatory notes. No MCCs are established for the movie benefit and no fixed INR point value is published. The 2025 terms say points may be forfeited 30 days after card closure or if MAD is overdue for more than 90 days.
- **Lounge:** One complimentary visit per calendar quarter to select domestic lounges. Access requires INR 50,000 eligible spend during the previous 3 calendar months. Newly issued cards waive the spend gate for issuance month and the next 3 calendar months. Guest limit is unpublished.
- **Milestone:** 1,000 EDGE points at INR 150,000 eligible spends in the card anniversary year. Spend is assessed 60 days after anniversary to remove reversals/cancellations; points credited within 90 days. Movie, fuel, insurance, utility, rent, wallet, jewellery, education and government spend excluded. SonyLIV annual renewal tied to INR 150,000 spend was discontinued 1 Apr 2026.
- **Welcome and other benefits:** SonyLIV Premium annual subscription (issuer value INR 1,499) with first purchase in first 30 days, subject to promotional eligibility; current page says voucher SMS generally within 45 days of issuance. District BOGO: 100% off second ticket, code AXIS200, capped at INR 200/month; no rewards on movie transactions. Swiggy INR 120 off on minimum INR 500 order, up to twice monthly per user ID, code AXIS120, not Haldiram's. Cleartrip: flights 7% up to INR 2,000 and hotels 18% up to INR 4,000, each once per use/month, code CTAXISMYZONE. Fuel waiver 1% on INR 400-4,000, max INR 400 per statement cycle; GST not refunded; some stations charge INR 10+GST instead. Dining Delights and Wednesday offers are variable; no fixed savings encoded. Purchases over INR 1,500 may be converted to EMI.
- **Eligibility:** Primary applicant 18-70; residents and NRIs eligible. Income documents requested but no minimum-income threshold published; no score cutoff stated. Bank reserves approval discretion.

## Modeling notes and limits

The current product page gives a useful card-specific schedule and updates its common fees; the linked older revision PDF does not contain the Aug-2026 common fee changes. The latest page/KFS/MITC were used for the fees above. The record schema cannot encode all late-fee boundaries, generic surcharge thresholds, rewards-forfeiture triggers, calendar-quarter lounge spend gate or milestone sub-timing. BOGO attempt count and guest access are not published. Dynamic merchant offers are documented without treating them as guaranteed fixed savings.
