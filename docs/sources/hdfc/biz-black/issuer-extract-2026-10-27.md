## Reconciliation
- Removed the unsupported launch date. Current fee page confirms ₹10,000 plus tax joining/renewal, ₹1.5 lakh joining-fee waiver spend within 90 days, ₹7.5 lakh renewal waiver in 12 anniversary-year billing cycles, 2% FX markup, 1.99% monthly finance charge, and 2.5% or ₹500 minimum cash-advance fee.
- Current product page sets the welcome offer to All Accor+ Explorer for cards set up from 15 Sep 2026, with ₹1.5 lakh spend within 90 days; offer applies only to first-time cardholders, excluding conversions/migrations/upgrades. The previous Club Marriott + ₹5,000 Taj voucher is limited to cards set up 25 Oct 2025–14 Sep 2026.
- Current eligibility page requires self-employed Indian citizen age 21–65, annual business ITR above ₹45 lakh, and a mandatory liability relationship. Older issuer FAQ says ITR above ₹30 lakh and does not mention the liability relationship; current product page was used, and discrepancy recorded in the data.
- Verified 5 RP/₹150 base earn (excluding petrol, wallet, rent, education and EMI); defined business categories earn 5X when total statement-cycle spend reaches ₹50,000; accelerated points capped at 7,500 per statement cycle. Removed an unsupported fixed point rupee value; redemption value varies by route. 1:1 transfers do not establish a fixed realized INR value.
- Issuer page says ₹5,000 voucher each ₹5 lakh milestone, capped ₹20,000 on ₹20 lakh spend in a calendar year (excluding EMI, rent and petrol). Schema lacks a calendar-year cycle, so exact calendar cycle remains in the benefit text.
- Removed fuel waiver; current fee schedule says not applicable. Removed complimentary insurance claims; issuer describes a business-insurance package at a separate ₹3,785 annual premium including GST. Lounge access is unlimited but active-card gated; issuer defines active as at least one online/in-store transaction in the previous month. Golf: six games per quarter; concierge 24/7.

## Remaining uncertainties
Current product page supersedes older FAQs on welcome and eligibility. Reward value varies by redemption. Calendar-year milestone cycle is text-only due to schema limits. Recheck these terms at the next review.
