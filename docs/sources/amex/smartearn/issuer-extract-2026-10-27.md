## Reconciled record

- Current product page lists ₹495 annual fee and full renewal waiver at ₹40,000 qualifying spend in the preceding membership year. The official fee schedule supports ₹495 yearly fee, 3.5% monthly finance, 3.5% forex and 3.5% cash advance charge with ₹250 minimum. The current late-payment formula is 30% of Minimum Amount Due (₹500 minimum, ₹1,000 maximum); Amex says statements generated after 15-Nov-2026 will instead use late-fee slabs based on Total Amount Due, ranging ₹500–₹1,250. Schema cannot encode the current fee formula or upcoming revised slabs.
- Current card-specific SmartEarn page states 1 Membership Rewards point per ₹50 and merchant-specific total rates of 10X/5X. Current total-point monthly caps are 500 jointly for Zomato/EaseMyTrip; 500 jointly for Flipkart, Uber, BookMyShow and other named 10X merchants; and 250 for Amazon. The current merchant list can change. These rates are not applied to broad online/dining/travel categories.
- The generic Reward Multiplier select-brand shopping program ended effective 30-Sep-2026. The SmartEarn product page still lists its card-specific 10X/5X merchant schedule. The two benefits are distinguished in the data; the generic program's stale 2X promotional reference on the SmartEarn benefits page is not treated as a currently valid reward.
- Current product page confirms ₹500 welcome statement credit after paying first-year annual fee and ₹10,000 eligible spend within 90 days, and ₹500 vouchers at ₹1.20 lakh, ₹1.80 lakh and ₹2.40 lakh membership-year milestones. The issuer lists voucher terms separately; the ₹31-Jul-2026 expiry found on the same benefits page applies to a separate EaseMyTrip booking offer, not these voucher milestones.
- Reward exclusions: fuel, insurance, utilities, cash transactions and merchant/POS EMI. Amex confirms SafeKey EMI and post-purchase EMI continue earning. Unsupported wallet-load and blanket EMI exclusions were removed.
- Current eligibility page lists resident Indian, age over 18, salaried personal annual income ₹4.5 lakh or more / self-employed ₹6 lakh or more, Indian bank account, good credit history/no defaults, serviceable-city address, and more than 12 months trading if self-employed. No score floor is published.
- New India applications are temporarily paused. Amex also lists an EMI-request processing disruption during 11–16 Nov 2026. This is not a product discontinuation.

## Corrections and limitations
