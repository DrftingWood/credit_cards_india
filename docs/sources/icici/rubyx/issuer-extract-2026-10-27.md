## Verified changes and source conflicts
- Product is active and application link remains live. Current generic Rubyx offers paired network choices (Mastercard + Amex or Visa + RuPay). Cleared unsupported single Visa/Signature fields and exact 2014 launch date; those details are unestablished for the aggregate record.
- Fees: joining INR3,000 + GST, annual INR2,000 + GST, next-year annual fee waiver for spend over INR300,000; forex 3.5%, monthly finance 3.75%. Cash-advance transaction fee not found in the current accessible MITC tariff; left unknown.
- Rewards: 2 points/INR100 domestic, 4 international, 1 utilities and insurance. Fuel earns no points. Removed unsupported wallet-load/rent exclusions and MCC 6513, and removed fixed INR0.25 point valuation because issuer terms state points have no cash value and redemption depends on offers. INR99 + GST redemption handling fee.
- iShop: current issuer FAQ describes up to 12X hotels and up to 6X flights, buses and shopping vouchers; rates depend on current offers. Current cap sheet lists 15,000 points/month and 8,000/day for iShop on other credit cards. The shared monthly cap is modelled; daily cap retained in notes because schema has no daily cap field.
- Lounge: 2 domestic visits/quarter after INR75,000 or more spend in preceding quarter, primary cardholder. Paired network card variants split entitlement as one visit per quarter per variant. No international lounge visits for ordinary Rubyx; issuer FAQ separately describes international lounge access only for Emirates Skywards Rubyx. Railway lounge: product page says 8/year; FAQ says 2/quarter (equivalent).
- Golf: one round or lesson per INR50,000 eligible retail spend in previous calendar month, max two/month; credited next month and usable for two months.
- Movies: 25% off up to INR150 twice monthly at BookMyShow and INOX. BookMyShow requires INR25,000+ preceding-quarter spend from Apr-2026; add-on cardholder must meet it individually. FAQ says one BMS offer/month/variant on dual-card product. No INOX spend gate found on current page.
- Welcome: core INR5,000 vouchers (Tata CLiQ 2,000; EaseMyTrip domestic 2,000; Croma 1,000). Separately, the current page lists EaseMyTrip holiday gift card 3,000, Elivaas coupon 10,000 subject to INR50,000 transaction, EazyDiner Prime and Avis offers. The extra offers have separate restrictions and are not treated as fungible guaranteed value.
- Insurance: up to INR10,000,000 air accident (primary cardholder, international travel, ticket purchased on card). Product FAQ says prior card use within 60 days; linked policy says an active card needs a transaction in prior 90 days. This conflict is retained. RuPay variant separately has INR200,000 personal accident cover.
- Fuel waiver 1% for INR400?INR4,000 fuel purchases; applies to primary and supplementary cardholders; no cap is specified.
- Eligibility: Indian citizen aged 21?65; salaried monthly income INR75,000 or self-employed annual income INR900,000; income proof required. No numeric credit score floor published.
