# IDBI Bank Winnings — issuer evidence extract

Verified on 2026-10-26 from the [current Winnings product page](https://www.idbi.bank.in/winnings-credit-card.aspx), the [IDBI credit-card FAQ](https://www.idbi.bank.in/credit-card-faq.aspx), and locally archived issuer KFS, MITC and Usage Guide PDFs. The official search-indexed page was crawled within the prior month; direct page open timed out during capture. The PDFs in this directory were previously downloaded from IDBI on 2026-07-04; they are retained as corroboration, but the current product page was used for current advertised terms.

## Current product page

- Joining fee NIL; primary annual fee INR899 from year 2; waiver on annual spend over INR90,000. Current KFS says the annual fee is waived on minimum INR90,000 preceding-year spend, so the threshold wording is ambiguous.
- Finance charge 3.4% monthly; cross-currency markup 3.5%; cash advance fee 2.5% or INR500, whichever is higher.
- 2 Delight Points per INR100 spend. 500 points on activation; 500 on first INR1,000+ transaction within 30 days; milestone 1,000 points at INR100,000 and 1,500 at INR200,000 net annual spend. Whether two milestone thresholds stack is not specified.
- Fuel waiver 1% on INR400-4,000 transactions, maximum INR400 monthly.
- Lounge: 2 domestic visits per calendar quarter and 2 international per calendar half-year. The issuer's domestic quarter is defined as 15 Jan-15 Apr, 15 Apr-15 Jul, 15 Jul-15 Oct and 15 Oct-15 Jan.
- Eligibility: primary age 21-60, up to age 65 for self-employed; resident in India. Criteria are indicative and approval is discretionary. No income threshold or minimum credit score is stated.

## Supporting issuer documents

- Local KFS confirms INR899 annual fee/INR90,000 preceding-year waiver, 3.5% cross-currency markup, 3.4% monthly interest and cash advance 2.5% or INR500 minimum.
- Local MITC excludes MCCs 6010, 6011, 6211, 6300, 6381, 9211, 9222, 9311, 9399, 9405, 4112, 5960, 5541, 5542, 5172 and 5983 from Delight point accrual. Reversed/refunded transactions debit previously issued points. The archived MITC also lists three-year point expiry and nil redemption fee.
- The current FAQ states one Delight Point converts to 0.25 paisa (INR0.0025) and gives minimum 4,000 points for cashback. The stated unit value is exceptionally low and may be a typo; the product page does not clarify it. Record leaves point value and rupee redemption rate unknown pending an issuer correction.
- The archived usage guide repeats the 2/INR100 base rate, birthday-month double points and older usage instructions. Birthday-month double points are not listed on the current product page and were not carried into the current record.
