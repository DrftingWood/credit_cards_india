# Google Pay Flex Axis Bank Credit Card launch release

- Issuer: Axis Bank
- Page: https://www.axis.bank.in/about-us/press-releases/google-pay-and-axis-bank-launch-flex
- Published: 2025-12-17
- Checked: 2026-10-26

Axis's issuer announcement says the Google Pay Flex Axis Bank Credit Card is the first product under Flex by Google Pay and is built on the RuPay network. It states each Star is worth INR 1 and can be redeemed on Flex transactions. The announcement does not specify a RuPay network tier.
