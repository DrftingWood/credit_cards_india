How to avoid extra charges

Small habits that go a long way in keeping unnecessary charges off your statement.

Pay in full, every month

You are charged interest only if you miss your due date and you pay less than your billed amount.

Avoid cash transfers

Cash withdrawals and bank transfers on your credit card attract high fees and interest. Pay directly with your card instead.

Spend within your limit

Spending within your credit limit keeps your credit profile healthy and helps you avoid extra charges.

Big spend? Pay it in parts

Convert large purchases to EMI and manage repayments easily.

Review your spends often

Regular checks help you spot unusual expenses before they add up.
