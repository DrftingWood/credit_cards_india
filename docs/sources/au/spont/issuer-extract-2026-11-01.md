# AU SPONT — issuer comparison (2026-11-01)

Compared the current AU SPONT product page/FAQ with the SPONT-specific lounge terms effective 10-Apr-2026 and current AU KFS/Schedule of Charges v1.9 (30-Sep-2026).

## Fees and rewards

- Joining/annual fees INR 299 + GST. KFS: forex 3.49%, finance 3.75% monthly, cash advance 2.5% or INR 500 minimum.
- AU advertises 1% cashback on e-commerce, POS, contactless and UPI transactions through any UPI channel; cashback posts on statement date. Current public card page does not give a numeric cap or complete category exclusions, so none are invented.
- Separate, non-expiring Spont Coins accrue on UPI through AU 0101 only. Earn and redemption values are unpublished; not modeled as monetary value.
- Fuel waiver 1% on INR 400–5,000, maximum INR 100 per statement cycle. Current public material advertises 2 railway-lounge visits per calendar quarter; guest/add-on conditions are not fully clear from the product page.

## Airport lounge access

- Product page advertises two domestic airport lounge visits per quarter after INR 50,000 spend in a calendar quarter. Linked SPONT lounge terms effective 10-Apr-2026 clarify the rule: qualifying net retail spend in a calendar quarter unlocks two visits in the next calendar quarter.
- Primary cardholder only; add-on cardholders are excluded. Guest charges apply. A non-reversible INR 2 card-validation transaction is charged at entry. AU's linked general lounge program does not publish more card-specific entitlements than the two visits advertised on the SPONT page.

## Eligibility

Resident Indian; salaried age 21–60, self-employed 25–65, add-on over 18. Card-specific income and bureau thresholds are not published.
