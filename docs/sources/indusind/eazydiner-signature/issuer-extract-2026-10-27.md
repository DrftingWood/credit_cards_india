## Record corrections

- Fee remains ₹2,999 joining/annual, with no spend waiver. Current MITC gives 3.83% monthly finance charge, cash advance fee 2.5%/₹300 minimum, and 3.5% FX markup.
- Current Reward Point value is ₹0.10 (updated 15-Jul-2025). Base earn is 4/₹100; accelerated earn is 10/₹100 on eligible hotel/travel, shopping and entertainment, with 7,500 monthly cap. Dining and fuel do not earn Reward Points. Points expire at anniversary-year end; redeem only in EazyDiner app, not as cash credit.
- Extra dining discount: 25% up to ₹1,000/transaction, ₹2,000 monthly allowance; ₹30,000 non-dining spend in previous calendar month unlocks another ₹3,000 discount, for ₹5,000 monthly maximum. Must pay through PayEazy in app; same-restaurant payments within 120 minutes excluded.
- Issuer notice discontinues BookMyShow/movie tickets and complimentary domestic lounge access effective 15-Jul-2025. These are not current benefits.
- Issuer product page also lists annual EazyDiner Prime, 2,000 bonus points and ₹7,500 Postcard Hotel voucher with the fee, and a fuel-surcharge waiver. The latter's exact transaction limits are not stated. Historical launch date not established.
