# IndusInd InterMiles co-brand cards — issuer source review (2026-11-01)

Applies to Odyssey Amex, Odyssey Visa, Voyage Amex and Voyage Visa records.

## Sources compared

- [IndusInd InterMiles Voyage Visa reference page](https://www.indusind.bank.in/in/en/personal/cards/credit-card/intermiles-voyage-visa-credit-card.html), which says the product is discontinued for new sourcing and existing cards and displayed only for reference.
- [IndusInd current card updates](https://www.indusind.bank.in/in/en/personal/credit-card-related/important-updates.html), which states InterMiles redemption for all IndusInd credit cards ended effective 21-Jan-2025.
- [IndusInd Celesta rewards page](https://www.indusind.bank.in/in/en/microsites/celesta/celesta-rewards.html), which confirms the redemption cutoff.

## Correction

The four records had legacy earning and benefit periods left open-ended despite the issuer's discontinuation of both new and existing InterMiles cards. Bounded their legacy terms through 20-Jan-2025, the day before the published redemption cutoff, and refreshed source/review dates. The records remain historical discontinued products; they should not be presented as cards still earning or redeeming InterMiles today. Variant-specific historic terms remain subject to their archived terms.

## Limits

The current issuer pages establish discontinuation and the redemption cutoff but do not expose full migration FAQ text for each variant in searchable page content. The exact replacement-card mapping is not used to infer additional benefits.
