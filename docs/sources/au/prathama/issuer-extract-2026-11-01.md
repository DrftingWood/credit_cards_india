# AU Prathama — issuer comparison (2026-11-01)

Compared the current AU Prathama product page/FAQ with the 16-May-2026 Card Member Agreement, AU Rewardz FAQ and 30-Sep-2026 KFS/Schedule of Charges.

## Fees and core rewards

- Joining and annual fees INR 100 + GST; no joining waiver; annual fee waived from year two at INR 75,000 anniversary-year eligible retail spend.
- KFS/general charges: 3.49% forex, 3.75% monthly finance, cash advance fee 2.5% or INR 500 minimum; Prathama fuel-waiver cap INR 100/month.
- 1 point/INR 100 on other eligible transactions, 2 points/INR 100 on contactless. After crossing INR 5,000 cumulative spend in a statement cycle, an additional 2 points/INR 100 is stated on incremental spend. AU Card Member Agreement caps all points at 1,000 per statement cycle.
- Card FAQ and agreement both support that threshold/cap structure. Whether the post-threshold rate stacks with contactless earn is not stated; the record does not model a second accelerator that could double-count.
- No points on fuel, EMI, cash/quasi-cash, rent, transportation, education or government payments; agreement also states BBPS exclusions and 2-year expiry, 12-month inactivity/closure/delinquency forfeiture.
- Standing Instruction: 500 bonus points for each successful settled setup, once per merchant lifetime; no welcome bonus. Fuel surcharge waiver 1% for INR 400–5,000 transactions, capped INR 100/statement cycle.
- General AU Rewardz conversion: 0.25 paise/point for catalogue and 0.20 paise for e-vouchers; minimum redemption 200 points and INR 99 + GST fee. The record's realized estimate is only a proxy for e-voucher redemption, not a universal value.

## Eligibility and limitations

AU lists resident Indian status and age ranges (salaried 21–60; self-employed 25–65; add-on over 18). Card-specific income and credit-score requirements are not published. Card Liability Cover, Credit Shield and Device Protection are advertised, but public card page does not publish their policy limits/conditions.
