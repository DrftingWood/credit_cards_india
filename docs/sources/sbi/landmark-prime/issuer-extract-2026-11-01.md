## Verified details

- Fees: INR 2,999 joining and renewal, plus taxes. The current product page lists the INR 2,999 annual and renewal fee; current SBI MITC supports the variant fee. No fee-waiver threshold is published for this card.
- Earn: 25 points/INR 100 at online and retail Landmark stores (Lifestyle, Home Centre, Max, Spar, Baby Shop and EasyBuy); 10/INR 100 on dining, movies and international spends; 2/INR 100 on other retail. Points convert at 4 points = INR 1 and are redeemed through the closed-loop Landmark Rewards route. The booklet says INR 99 plus GST is charged for redemption below 1,000 points.
- Cashback/reward source value: the product page equates 12,000 points with INR 3,000 and repeats 4 points = INR 1. Welcome offer is 12,000 points on payment of annual fees; annual milestone is 20,000 points on INR 5 lakh overall annual retail spend.
- Reward cap: current SBI Cardholder Agreement specifies a 7,500-point monthly cap on cumulative 5X earn for SBI Card PRIME dining, movies, departmental-store and grocery categories, and states the cap applies to corresponding co-brand and AmEx variants. Modeled this cap for the dining/movie 10-point tier. The Landmark-specific 25-point store tier is 12.5X relative to the 2-point base and is not expressly described by the generic 5X cap language; a conservative 7,500/month cap is modeled because it is a departmental-store co-brand category. SBI does not publish a Landmark-specific interpretation, so this cap mapping remains an inference and is flagged in the record.
- Lounge: 8 domestic visits/year, max 2/quarter; complimentary Priority Pass membership and 4 international visits/year, max 2/quarter. Post-quota international visit charge is USD 27 plus taxes.
- Fuel waiver: 1% on INR 500–3,000 transactions, capped INR 250/statement cycle.
- Other retail exclusions on the current product page include fuel, rent, government, gaming, wallet and EMI. SBI Reward Program terms separately exclude cash advances, Flexipay/Balance Transfer, financial charges and e-wallet MCCs 6540/6541. No unsupported insurance-premium exclusion is asserted.
- Eligibility: SBI policy publishes salaried ages 21–65 and self-employed ages 25–70; no fixed card-specific income or credit-score threshold was found. Removed the previous aggregator-derived income estimate from the note.

## Open source limits

The 7,500-point monthly cap's application to the Landmark-specific 25-point store earn is not expressly explained by SBI; the dataset flags that interpretation. Current product-page text and card booklet do not publish exact merchant MCC coverage or a fixed income/credit-score minimum. Historical point expiry follows SBI's current general 24-month policy.
