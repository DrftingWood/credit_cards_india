- Retained legacy earn rate 6 Maharaja Points per INR 200 as the card's last-known rate; the reviewed transition terms confirm eligible points continue, but do not restate this rate. Exclusions were reconciled to the new shared terms. Removed MCC 6513 as a reward exclusion because the cited evidence categorizes rent but does not establish that MCC as a specific reward-blocking code.
