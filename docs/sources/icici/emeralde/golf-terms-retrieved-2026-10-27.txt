➢ How many days prior to the tee time do I have to make bookings?

   The bookings for a specific tee - off date open exactly 15 days prior to the tee      - off date for all
   golf courses under this programme and close 4 to 6 days prior to the actual teeoff date,
   depending on the golf club.

➢ How many open booking requests can I hold at a time?

   ICICI Bank Credit Card members can hold only one open booking request (pending/
   confirmed) at any point in time. Successful booking is on ‘first come first serve’ basis and is
   subject to weekly stock availability.
   An open booking request will be considered closed once the customer has played the
   booked round on the tee - off date.

➢ What is the validity of the golf session credited to the customer?

   The golf session is valid for 60  days from the credited date. Post completion of 60 days the
   rounds will auto expire. Customers will    not be able to access expired rounds for booking
   purpose

➢ Can the Validity period of complimentary Golf Rounds/Lessons be extended?

   As per the Programme Policy, Rounds/Lessons once expired cannot be extended.

➢ Can I continue to play golf at the courses after I have utilised the complimentary rounds?

   You are welcome to tee - off at your preferred golf courses even after you have utilised the
   complimentary rounds of golf. The green fees vary from course to course depending on the
   time of the week (weekend/weekdays) and the season.
   To know more about green fees applicable at various courses, please call i           - Assist, our
   concierge service on 1800 - 26 - 70731 (toll - free from MTNL/BSNL lines) or 022 6787 2016
   (standard call charges apply). This service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a we             ek.

   ICICI Bank Diamant Credit Card members may call ICICI Bank Diamant Personal Concierge
   on 1800 11 6979 (toll free) or 011 4060 8637 or contact their Relationship Manager.

➢ Are the golf privileges available on Supplementary/Add               - on cards?

   The golf programme is available only for primary card members.


➢ Do I need to carry my own golf set?

   No, it is not required. Golf sets are available for hire at the golf course. Charges for the same
   have to be borne by the card member.

   Yes, you can invite friends or guests to play with you. Please remember, in such cases, the
   charges of the guest accompanying you will have to be borne by you. Golf courses reserve

➢ How can I make payment for accompanying guests, if any?

   Payment for guests accompanying a card member for the game can be made online on
   www.teetimes.golftrip z .com

➢ Is it mandatory for me to bring a guest along?

   Some golf courses do not allow a single player on weekends and sometimes even on
   weekdays. They may also pair a single golfer with other players if deemed suitable.


➢ In cases where a caddy and buggy is compulsory, is it complimentary as well?

   No, the green fee is complimentary, courtesy ICICI Bank. Charges for caddy and buggy have
   to be borne by the card member.

➢ What are the conditions regarding use of a caddy and buggy on golf courses?

   The use of a caddy and/or buggy is mandated by certain golf courses. If usage of a caddy
   or buggy is mandatory, the card member will be required to hire the same at the golf club
   and pay directly to the golf course. The card member will be responsible for             his goods and
   not the caddy. Giving a tip to the caddy is in the spirit of the game. At some courses, the
   card member might not be allowed to drive the buggy himself.

➢ What if I cancel a confirmed booking?

   Cancellations need to be made at least 6 days prior to tee time. Some golf courses do not
   allow cancellation. The same will be communicated to you at the time of booking. In case of
   a cancellation after the stipulated time, one slot will be deducted from t         he complimentary
   slots allotted to you. In case of payments made for guest/s or self, the same will not be
   reversed.


➢ Is a Handicap Certificate compulsory to play golf?

   Most golf courses mandate requirement of a Handicap Certificate.
