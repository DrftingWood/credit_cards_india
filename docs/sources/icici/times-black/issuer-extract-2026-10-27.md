## Corrections supported by current issuer evidence

- Visa network is confirmed; no current card page or launch release establishes Visa Infinite tier. The old 2024-03-01 launch date was unsupported and has been cleared; the archived issuer launch page does not expose a reliable publication date.
- Current issuer page: joining/annual fee INR20,000 + GST, annual fee waived from year two after INR2,500,000 spend in the prior year; 1.49% forex markup; 3.75% monthly interest when only MAD is paid. Fee table says ATM withdrawal ?Nil?; the fee field now records a zero transaction fee, while preserving that finance charges may apply.
- Card-specific T&Cs: 2% domestic and 2.5% eligible international reward points. Up to INR1 per point depending on redemption; statement credit is INR0.40 per point. Utility, insurance, education and government spend share a 5,000-point per-statement cap. Fuel, rent and EMI transactions earn no points. Current ICICI update notice defines rental MCCs 6513/7349 and excludes e-wallet loading since 05-Feb-2024. General issuer FAQ gives 3-year reward validity and INR99 + GST handling fee per redemption request.
- Unlimited domestic lounge access for primary and add-on cardholders; unlimited international Priority Pass access for primary and add-on cardholders. International guests are billed USD29 + GST per guest. The previous YAML incorrectly stated unlimited free guests and included Dreamfolks/DragonPass.
- Joining benefit sources support the EaseMyTrip INR10,000 or Lohono INR12,000 stay voucher, Toni & Guy INR3,000 and Interflora INR1,000 vouchers, up to INR10,000 Visa wallet for Atlys/OneVasco eligible services, and a Zomato Gold annual membership. Joining benefits require joining fee + GST within the first two statement cycles; email notice is expected within 45 business days. Zomato market price is not treated as card-provided cash value.
- Current anniversary-year milestones: INR200,000 (Klook INR10,000), INR500,000 (mobility wallet/eligible transfer option INR10,000), INR1,000,000 (Tata CLiQ Luxury INR10,000), INR2,000,000 (Ayatana/Lohono stay INR20,000). Benefits available six months from issuer notification. Fee waiver threshold is separately INR2.5 million.
- December-2025 insurance policy: air accident is death-only INR30,000,000 (not permanent disability); purchase protection INR140,000/90 days; plane hijacking INR100,000; credit shield INR100,000; lost-card/fraud INR50,000; checked baggage INR84,000; rail/road injury medical INR50,000; passport/travel documents INR35,000; baggage INR25,000; baggage delay and missed connection INR21,000 each; flight delay INR17,500. Primary cardholder only; active-card clause defines activity within prior 90 days. Removed former unsupported INR5,000,000 travel-medical assertion.
- Current CFAR policy: up to INR12,000 per card for the first two eligible domestic flight, hotel and movie cancellations; cardholder must pay with Times Black. Fuel waiver is 1%, maximum INR1,000 per statement cycle. Removed unsupported unlimited golf.
- Eligibility: Indian citizen over 21; salaried monthly income INR200,000 or self-employed annual income INR2,400,000; income proof required. Card remains invitation/curated issuance. No maximum age or numeric credit-score floor is published.
