## Reconciliation with the card record

- The current issuer page still identifies the CA Metal card, calls it lifetime free, and advertises 1.99% forex markup. The current KFS remains the source for 2.99% monthly finance charge, 2.5% cash-advance fee (INR 500 minimum), and related charges. No fee or markup discrepancy was found against the record.
- The page confirms 2 Reward Points/INR 100 on retail, 8/INR 100 on dining, travel, tax and software, and 1/INR 100 on utility, telecom and insurance. It confirms the 5,000-point statement-cycle cap for the accelerated MCC group and 100 points per utility/telecom transaction. Fuel, rent, education/government, EMI, BBPS, cash and quasi-cash are excluded. The card-specific March terms and general agreement provide detailed MCC and overall cap rules; MCC 9311 remains ambiguous where it appears in both eligible tax and excluded MCC groupings.
- AU Rewardz confirms 0.25 paisa per point for catalogue redemption and 0.20 paisa for e-vouchers. The record keeps catalogue value as the primary value and separately notes the lower voucher value and redemption fee.
- The product page confirms 2 domestic lounge visits per calendar quarter and the INR 50,000 preceding-quarter spend gate effective 1-Oct-2026. It also confirms the INR 5,000/30-day welcome threshold and 2,000 bonus points, 25% ICAI annual-membership renewal cashback, fuel-waiver transaction range INR 400–5,000 with INR 250 cap, and no-cost EMI processing for eligible conversions.
- The product page lists up to INR 50 lakh air-accident cover, credit shield up to INR 5 lakh, purchase protection up to INR 25,000, and card-liability/travel covers. Detailed policy limits and conditions for several covers are not stated on the page; the YAML keeps those limits unfilled rather than inferring them.
- Eligibility remains restricted to resident Indian applicants in the CA program: salaried age 21–60, self-employed age 25–75, add-on age 18+. The page gives no numeric income or credit-score floor. The current application navigation includes an apply link.

## Open points

The issuer does not publish a clear interpretation of MCC 9311's conflicting reward treatment, exact insurance terms for every listed cover, or a numeric income/credit-score floor. Card-network branding is not established by the reviewed product page. Point-expiry timing differs between AU's generic FAQ and the member agreement; the schema has no expiry field.

The current product page was opened and checked on 1-Nov-2026. Supporting card-specific and shared issuer documents are dated as listed above; no broad recheck of the fresh 27-Oct fee/reward review was needed.
