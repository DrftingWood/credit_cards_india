# AU Bank Vetta Credit Card — issuer source review (2026-11-01)

- [Current Vetta product page](https://www.au.bank.in/personal-banking/credit-cards/vetta-credit-card): benefits, annual fee and waiver rules, age/residency criteria, domestic and international airport lounge headline, railway lounge, birthday points, fuel waiver, foreign markup, contactless, Xpress EMI, add-on cards and liability headline.
- [AU revised airport lounge terms, effective 10 April 2026](https://www.au.bank.in/pdf/zenith-credit-card/revision-of-usage-terms-and-conditions_jan_25-4.pdf): Vetta-specific domestic and Priority Pass visit counts, spend eligibility timing, primary-cardholder rule, net retail spend definition, charges.
- [AU Priority Pass usage terms](https://www.au.bank.in/content/dam/aubank/in/en/pdf/abc-pro-credit-card/priority-pass-lounges.pdf): Vetta membership issuance on request, one complimentary international visit per calendar quarter outside India, domestic India charges and excess-visit charges.
- [AU Schedule of Charges / KFS](https://www.au.bank.in/pdf/credit-card/Schedule-of-Charges-CC.pdf) and [Card Member Agreement](https://www.au.bank.in/credit-cards/credit-card-member-agreement.pdf): fee schedule and point cap/exclusion rules. The existing record cites these for fee and reward terms; point earning detail was not resolved by the product page.

## Verified and updated

- Annual membership fee INR 2,999 plus taxes. First-year waiver: INR 40,000 retail spend within 90 days of card setup. Renewal waiver: INR 150,000 retail spend in the preceding card anniversary year.
- Product page advertises up to 4 Reward Points per INR 100 but publishes no base/category breakdown in accessible text. Keep rate fields unknown. The existing record uses AU Rewardz FAQ values (0.25 paise per point; 0.20 paise per e-voucher) and the Member Agreement's 25,000-point statement-cycle cap; point exclusions and utility/telecom limits remain governed by the agreement.
- Domestic airport access: one visit per calendar quarter. From 10 April 2026, INR 50,000 net retail spend in a calendar quarter unlocks access in the next quarter. Primary cardholder only; add-on cardholders are ineligible. INR 2 validation charge is non-reversible.
- International airport access: one Priority Pass visit per calendar quarter outside India, four annually. Vetta Priority Pass is issued on request. Priority Pass use within India costs USD 35 plus GST; excess international visits cost USD 35 plus applicable taxes. The product page's broad wording that India use is chargeable is consistent.
- Railway lounge access: two visits per calendar quarter; add-on cardholders share the stated complimentary visits.
- Birthday benefit: 1,000 points when any retail transaction is made on the birthday. Quarterly/yearly milestone headlines remain unquantified and are not modeled.
- Product page confirms air accident cover up to INR 50 lakh, reported-lost-card zero liability, 1% fuel surcharge waiver (INR 400–5,000 transactions, INR 100 cap), 3.49% cross-currency markup, free add-on cards, contactless PIN-free payments up to INR 5,000 and Xpress EMI on eligible transactions of INR 2,000 or more. Detailed policy terms and eligibility are not fully public on the product page.
- Public eligibility: resident Indian; salaried age 21–60 and self-employed age 25–65. These are indicative criteria.

## Record correction and limits

The previous record's lounge note could be read as same-quarter spend qualification. The current rules explicitly grant the entitlement in the *next* calendar quarter; the YAML is corrected. No issuer-published text breakdown for the advertised “up to 4” reward rate was found, so no base or accelerated rate has been inferred. Milestone award details and full insurance terms remain unavailable in the reviewed public sources.
