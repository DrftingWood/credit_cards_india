                            Spend Based Airport Lounge Access

                                      Terms & Conditions
                                              June 2026


    •   Customer would get 3 complimentary lounge access vouchers on spends of Rs. 60,000 or more
        in preceding calendar quarter- 2 Domestic lounge access (Within India) + 1 International
        lounge access (Outside India). Please refer this document further for further details and steps
        to avail the vouchers.

    •   Starting 01st July 2026, lounge access will be provided basis the spends of Rs. 60,000 done in
        previous calendar quarter. For Example- Once a cardholder achieves spends of Rs. 60,000
        spends in calendar quarter July-Aug- Sep, customer would be able to avail lounge access
        vouchers.

    •    Complimentary Lounge access is provided on a select list of participating lounges.
             o For Domestic list (Within India lounges) of the participating lounges please click here.
             o For International list (Outside India) of participating lounges, please refer click here.
                 Check in “Find Lounge” Section in top left of page.
List of participating lounges may change basis various factors such as closures, change in service
provider or at the discretion of bank.

    •   Voucher can be generated by Primary Card holder only.
    •   Card holder should present their voucher at the eligible lounge while availing the benefit
        (customer may be asked to present the card/ boarding pass as well and should keep it handy
        to enable hassle free admission).
    •   Complimentary access is allowed to Primary card holder only.
    •   Spends aggregation would be considered basis transaction posting or settlement date and not
        basis the transaction date.
    •   Validity of voucher would be 180 days from the date of generating the voucher.
