# AU CheQ insurance scope clarification (2026-11-01)

## Issuer material reviewed

- [AU CheQ card page](https://www.au.bank.in/personal-banking/credit-cards/cheq-au-credit-card), including its generic insurance summary and linked policy.
- [AU Card Member Agreement](https://www.au.bank.in/credit-cards/credit-card-member-agreement.pdf), dated 28-Nov-2025.
- [AU insurance terms](https://www.au.bank.in/content/dam/aubank/in/en/pdf/credit-card/insurance-terms-and-conditions.pdf), last updated 12-Jan-2024; and the card-liability terms linked from the CheQ page, last updated 16-Apr-2021.

## Finding

The CheQ page exposes a generic footer summary of AU card insurance and links to general policy documents. The current Member Agreement says insurance coverage varies by card and that personal/air-accident cover is available only on selected variants. The linked policy documents do not identify CheQ as an insured variant or provide CheQ-specific benefit limits. The generic list therefore does not substantiate card-specific coverage amounts for CheQ.

No insurance amounts have been added to `data/cards/au/cheq-rupay.yaml`. Its benefit-source note now records this limitation. The existing CheQ issuer comparison covers the current card page, rewards terms and Member Agreement; the insurance scope remains unpublished for this card.
