# IDBI Royale Signature issuer-index follow-up (2026-11-01)

This follow-up addresses the two open items in the 27-Oct review: whether the unusually low point-to-cash conversion is really expressed in paisa, and the insurance details exposed by current IDBI FAQ/search-index text. Direct page opens still return HTTP 502; the material below is issuer-owned indexed page text, not a full-document capture.

## Official issuer sources

- [Royale Signature card page](https://www.idbi.bank.in/royal-credit-card.aspx): 3 Delight Points per INR 100; 500 points on activation; 750 on the first INR 1,500+ transaction within 30 days; one Visa lounge visit per calendar quarter; fuel surcharge waiver for INR 400-INR 5,000 transactions; Visa Signature privileges and age/residency criteria.
- [Credit-card FAQ](https://www.idbi.bank.in/credit-card-faq.aspx): confirms 3 points/INR 100, three-year validity, INR 0.25 **paisa** per point (INR 0.0025), minimum 4,000 points for cashback and 1,000 for services. It lists Royale air-accident cover INR 25 lakh, personal accidental-death cover INR 5 lakh, credit shield for dues at death, and counterfeit-card cover up to INR 10 lakh.
- [Credit-card overview and FAQ](https://www.idbi.bank.in/credit-card.aspx): confirms the Royale Signature variant, zero lost-card liability after report, and a separate fraudulent-use cover up to INR 5 lakh when reported within 48 hours.
- [Key Fact Statement](https://www.idbi.bank.in/pdf/key-fact-statement.pdf): nil joining, annual and renewal fees for Royale; 3.5% cross-currency markup; cash-advance fee 2.5% or INR 500, whichever is higher.

## Record changes

- Confirmed the INR 0.0025 conversion rather than interpreting the FAQ's 0.25-paisa wording as INR 0.25. Existing reward valuation is correctly stored at INR 0.0025 per point.
- Separated zero lost-card liability from fixed-limit fraud cover. Added the FAQ's INR 5 lakh personal-accident cover and outstanding-dues credit shield. Recorded the INR 5 lakh vs INR 10 lakh fraudulent-use/counterfeit coverage conflict instead of merging them into one limit.
- Preserved the issuer FAQ's 180-day air-accident claim deadline against the general overview's one-year wording. Both sources require at least two card transactions/activities in the preceding three months for the air-accident cover.
