What is the BOBCARD Uni GoldX Credit Card?

It’s a lifetime-free co-branded credit card that rewards you with 1% value back in the form of Uni coins on every eligible transaction. You get 1% value back across spends and 5% value back when shopping through the Uni Store. Plus, it comes with zero forex markup, making it ideal for international travel.

What are the features and benefits of the BOBCARD Uni GoldX Credit Card?

Here’s a quick look at the top features and benefits of this premium card:
1. 1% value back on every transaction: In the form of Uni coins. This is applicable on every transaction you make * (exclusions apply).
2. 5% Value back at Uni Store: In the form of Uni coins. This is applicable when you shop for vouchers at the Uni Store.
3. Zero Forex Fees: No additional charges when you use your card internationally. 
4. Lifetime Free: Enjoy a lifetime of benefits with no joining fees and no annual charges. 
5. Fuel Surcharge Waiver: Get a 1% waiver on fuel surcharges at petrol pumps across India. 
6. No Expiry on Rewards: Your rewards never expire, and they can appreciate in value over time.

What are the fees for the BOBCARD Uni GoldX Credit Card?

There are no joining fees and no annual fees. The card is completely free for a lifetime.
