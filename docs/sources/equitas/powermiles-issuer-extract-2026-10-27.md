## Current MITC v1.2

Issuer PDF: [MITC v1.2](https://equitas.bank.in/strapi-dev/uploads/MITC_V1_2_Track_change_8ed95eca57.pdf)

Search extract identifies “PowerMiles/PowerMiles Secured” and says luxury memberships are available on joining-fee/annual-fee payment or spend milestones. For PowerMiles, it specifies INR 120,000 in the first three months for the first year and INR 480,000 annually from the second year, effective 24 November 2025. It also describes 3X accelerated rewards for international POS transactions on a best-effort basis based on MCC. This evidence qualifies the previous unconditioned “complimentary memberships” description. It does not independently establish online international transactions earning 3X.

## PowerMiles insurance document

Issuer PDF: [PowerMiles insurance cover](https://equitas.bank.in/strapi-dev/uploads/Insurance_Power_Miles_Website_1_5c2327d59b.pdf)

Search extract lists air-accident death cover up to INR 1 crore, credit shield to the credit limit or INR 8 lakh (whichever is higher), and lost-card liability up to INR 10 lakh for the primary cardholder. It says air-accident cover requires tickets purchased using an Equitas credit card, cover activates after card activation, and insurer policy exclusions/limitations apply. Full policy wording, definitions, and all claim conditions remain unreviewed; database wording must not imply guaranteed claim payment.
