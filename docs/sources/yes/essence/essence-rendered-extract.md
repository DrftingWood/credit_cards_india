## Fees, rewards, and eligibility

- Standard fees are INR 799 joining and renewal, exclusive of GST. Joining fee reversal: INR 20,000 spend within one month of setup. Renewal reversal: INR 100,000 spend in the preceding 12 months. The product page also advertises a limited-period lifetime-free offer; LTF customers are exempt from the standard membership charges. The current page does not state when the standard fee schedule first became effective.
- Current MITC v18: monthly finance 3.99%; foreign-currency markup 2.75%; cash advance fee 2.5% or INR 650, whichever is higher. DCC is 3% for Essence.
- Earn 12 YES Rewardz/INR 200 on online fashion, beauty, travel, dining and grocery; 8/INR 200 on other online shopping; 4/INR 200 on offline spend. Maximum total earning is 9,000 points per statement cycle. Issuer-listed exclusions include rent, wallet, fuel, government services, marketing/advertising, insurance, education, railways, toll, ferry, cash withdrawals, post-purchase/instant EMI and fees/charges. Product-page MCCs are preserved in the YAML.
- Issuer describes point value as up to INR 0.25; actual redemption value varies by channel. Current MITC gives 3-year expiry, INR 100 + GST redemption fee per request and INR 99 + GST additional convenience fee for flight bookings.
- Eligibility page: age 21–60, salaried, minimum net salary INR 30,000/month, and existing YES Bank credit-card holders are ineligible for the regular card. It separately says existing Mastercard/Visa holders may apply for a virtual RuPay variant; this wording is retained as an eligibility ambiguity. No bureau-score cutoff is published.

## Benefits and conditions

- Welcome: INR 1,000 in vouchers after the newly issued cardholder completes a first successful transaction within 45 days of issuance; issuer says the voucher link follows validation of that transaction.
- Bonus points: 5,000 after INR 50,000 spend within the first 90 days; 10,000 on INR 100,000 annual spend (the page does not define the annual measurement window more precisely).
- Domestic lounge: one primary-cardmember visit per calendar quarter, subject to INR 25,000 spend in the previous quarter.
- District movie BOGO: maximum INR 250 discount on the second ticket, once per user per calendar month.
- Birthday-month Myntra voucher worth INR 1,000 and annual health checkup; Economics Times Prime subscription; coffee BOGO twice per calendar quarter at eligible outlets in select malls; and green-fee waivers at select golf courses.
- Purchase protection covers accidental damage for six months on mobile/electronic devices purchased online; exact insured amount, exclusions and claims conditions were not established from the linked FAQ.
- Fuel surcharge reversal: 1% on INR 500–4,000 transactions, capped at INR 125 per statement cycle; GST is not reversed. Current MITC excludes UPI fuel transactions from surcharge reversal.

## Unknowns retained

The exact card launch date and a single network/tier are not established. The page references Mastercard/Visa and a virtual RuPay variant. The paid standard fee schedule's original effective date, complete purchase-protection policy limits, and annual-milestone measurement window remain unpublished or unresolved in the reviewed material.
