## Corrections and verified fields

- Corrected launch date from 1-Dec-2017 to 20-Dec-2017 using BPCL's official launch coverage.
- Cleared the single Visa/Platinum assertion. SBI's product page says the card is accepted at Visa or Mastercard merchants, while its combined card booklet uses Visa/RuPay branding. These sources do not establish one issued network and tier, so the structured network and tier are null and the card is tagged multi-network.
- Current joining, annual and renewal fees are INR499; the add-on fee is nil. From 1-May-2026, SBI reverses the annual fee at INR1 lakh annual spend, increased from INR50,000. Updated the fee effective date and threshold.
- Welcome offer: 2,000 points with stated value INR500 after joining-fee payment; SBI says points post within 20 days.
- Rewards: 13 points/INR100 on eligible BPCL fuel, maximum 1,300 points per statement cycle; card terms separately state a maximum INR10,000/month of BPCL fuel spend for earning. Fuel recognition depends on SBI's updated merchant-ID list. Dining, departmental stores, groceries and movies earn 5/INR100, capped together at 5,000 points/month; base 1/INR100 continues after the cap. Eligible MCCs are grocery 5411, department stores 5311, movies 7832/7922 and dining 5812/5813/5814. Metro Cash & Carry, Reliance Market and Walmart are excluded from accelerated department/grocery earning.
- Other retail earns 1 point/INR100. Terms exclude balance transfers, wallet loading MCCs 6540/6541, financial charges, disputed transactions, cash advances, non-BPCL fuel/service stations, Flexipay and merchant EMI. Four points equal INR1 for fuel redemption; points have no general cash value. The current page lists BPCL fuel and Shop n Smile catalogue redemption, not statement credit. Removed the unsupported statement-credit option and 0.22 realized-value claim.
- Fuel surcharge waiver: 1% on BPCL fuel, no minimum, transaction maximum INR4,000, statement-cycle/account cap INR100; POS swipes only and GST/other charges excluded.
- Cleared the unsupported rent MCC exclusion because the card-specific booklet does not list it; retained wallet MCCs 6540 and 6541.
- SBI's general policy supplies age ranges (21–65 salaried, 25–70 self-employed); no BPCL-specific income or credit-score threshold is published. The product page currently lists no lounge or insurance benefit for this card.

## Remaining source limitations

The booklet includes legacy network language and older general card terms; the current product page is used for current offers and fees. The product page's Visa/Mastercard acceptance wording and the booklet's Visa/RuPay logo wording do not resolve the network of every issued card. The fuel point spend ceiling is separately stated in the booklet and represented in the reward note; the 1,300-point statement cap remains in the structured rule.
