# ICICI Emeralde Credit Card issuer review (27 Oct 2026)

Product page: https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/cards/credit-card/emeralde-credit-card

Compared the current product page and FAQ, live linked 2022 membership guide, current linked MITC and general card terms, current golf programme terms, current group insurance policy, and cancellation claim process. Local PDF copies are listed in `docs/sources/icici/_manifest.json` and stored in this folder. The page remains marked available to apply.

## Encoded from current issuer evidence

- Joining and annual fees: INR12,000 plus GST. Current page says spend over INR1,000,000 waives the next year's annual fee. The 2022 membership guide contains a different INR1,500,000 annual reversal threshold and even an alternate monthly plan; it is retained as historical product evidence, not used over the current page.
- Foreign currency markup: 2%; monthly finance charge: 3.75%.
- Rewards: 4 ICICI Reward Points per INR100 domestic and international spend; 1 point per INR100 utility and insurance spend. The current product page gives earn rates but not a stable point-to-rupee value or exact exclusions schedule. Older linked membership material says retail transactions except fuel. No fixed point value, MCC list, or iShop multiplier is asserted. General current terms say rewards accrue on retail spend, exclude fees/charges/EMI and are not earned on reversed transactions.
- Lounge: unlimited domestic and international airport access for primary cardholder. No guest allowance is claimed. The page describes Priority Pass in the international lounge FAQ.
- Golf: one round or lesson per INR50,000 retail spend in the previous calendar month, up to four rounds/lessons per month. The issuer FAQ says benefits are credited by the 10th of the next month and usable in the following two months.
- BookMyShow: buy one, get up to INR750 off the second ticket, advertised up to four times per month. From 1-Apr-2026, eligibility requires INR25,000 spend in the preceding spends quarter, with add-on members meeting the spend threshold individually. For dual-network holders, one offer per month per variant.
- Birthday vouchers: the product benefit panel lists INR5,000 Tata CLiQ for American Express and INR5,000 PostCard Hotels for Mastercard. FAQ text elsewhere only names American Express, so Mastercard eligibility is flagged as internally inconsistent. Visa/RuPay variant birthday eligibility is not published.
- Cancellation: current product page states no cancellation charges on domestic flight and hotel bookings up to INR12,000, twice yearly. Claim process PDF supplies procedure and reporting requirements; it does not broaden the eligible booking types.
- Insurance: primary-cardholder air-accident cover INR30,000,000; international travel only and ticket must be purchased with the card. Product FAQ says card used once during the 60 days before incident; linked group policy defines active card using a 90-day window. Conflict remains noted. Removed stale INR5,000,000 travel-medical assertion because this coverage is not shown for Emeralde in the current card FAQ.
- Fuel: 1% surcharge waiver on fuel transactions up to INR4,000; primary and Plus cardholders. Current page states no minimum band or cycle-wide cap.
- Eligibility: Indian citizen over 21; FAQ states age 21-65. Salaried monthly income INR150,000 (annualized to INR1,800,000 in the schema); self-employed annual income INR1,800,000. No credit-score cutoff published.

## Cleared or left unresolved

- Cleared unsupported Mastercard World Elite scalar and 2017 launch date; product currently has multiple network variants and current issuance is not stated consistently enough to select one network or tier.
- Removed fixed point valuations, iShop 12X/6X claims, unverified MCC exclusions, unlimited lounge guest access, unsupported travel-medical cover and stale Amex-only birthday scalar.
- Cash advance fee is omitted: current product page refers to the MITC, but readable current source text did not establish a card-specific transaction fee/minimum. The old 2.5%/INR500 value is not treated as verified.
- `effective_from: 2026-10-27` in the card YAML records the review snapshot date only; the issuer does not publish effective dates for most current product-page terms.
