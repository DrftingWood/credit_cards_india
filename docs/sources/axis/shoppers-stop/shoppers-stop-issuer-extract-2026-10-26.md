# Axis Bank Shoppers Stop issuer evidence extract (2026-10-26)

Sources: [current product page](https://www.axis.bank.in/cards/credit-card/axis-bank-shoppers-stop-credit-card), [card member terms](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/credit-cards/terms-and-conditions-for-axis-bank-shoppers-stop-credit-card.pdf?sfvrsn=6573da5e_4), current MITC, and [issuer-wide terms update effective 2026-08-28](https://campaign.axis.bank.in/generic/terms-and-conditions-generic-all.pdf).

## Fees and eligibility

- Joining/annual fees INR500; annual fee waived at cumulative spend of INR100,000 or more in the anniversary year. Card-specific terms limit the waiver to eligible issuance/onboarding and exclude rent and wallet MCC categories from qualifying spend.
- Finance charge 3.75% monthly; cash advance 2.5%, minimum INR500; forex/DCC 3.5% (DCC current from 2026-08-28).
- Other current product-page charges include rent, education-app, utility, wallet, fuel and gaming transaction fees; some are not represented as schema fields.
- Primary applicant age 18–70 and resident in India. No card-specific income or bureau-score cutoff is published.
- The T&C references Visa and Mastercard MCC rules; no one current network/tier is asserted.

## Rewards

- First Citizen points earn 2 per INR200 on other spend; 20 per INR200 on Shoppers Stop Exclusive Brands; 12 per INR200 on other brands at Shoppers Stop and listed standalone partner stores. Accelerated totals are split between Shoppers Stop and Axis, and Axis attribution depends on merchant IDs supplied to the bank.
- Product value chart states INR0.50 per First Citizen point. Points redeem at Shoppers Stop and listed partner stores, online or in-store.
- Categories excluded from points include transportation/bridge/road/toll, utilities, insurance, education, government, wallet, rent, fuel and gift cards. The terms say “MCCs” but the numeric table is not extractable from the copy; no specific MCC is claimed in structured data.
- Points remain valid while the card is active; after card closure, accumulated points transfer to First Citizen and expire 365 days after closure.

## Welcome and other benefits

- INR300 worth of First Citizen points on the first transaction within 30 days of issuance.
- Welcome voucher package nominally totals INR2,250: three INR500 store vouchers after INR5,000 target spend, an INR500 Exclusive Brand Apparel voucher after INR2,500 qualifying purchase, and an INR250 online voucher. Welcome terms apply to eligible newly issued/onboarded cards; complimentary Golden Glow membership is also listed.
- Fuel waiver: 1% on INR400–5,000 transactions, capped at INR400 per statement cycle; GST on surcharge is non-refundable. Fuel earns no First Citizen points.
- Current product page lists an EazyDiner discount (15%, max INR500, minimum INR2,500, once/month), but the currently linked detailed campaign PDF is dated through 2024-06-30. The live offer terms must be checked at redemption; the inconsistency is documented.
- Card T&C also lists two end-of-season sales and one festive sale annually, 45-day exchange, 15% birthday/anniversary-month discount on Exclusive Brand apparel, and free home delivery for eligible purchases.

## Effective-date conflict and archive

The card page says terms updated effective 2026-08-28 and links a bankwide change notice. The card-specific T&C itself is the June 2025 revision; values are a 2026-10-26 verification snapshot. The August 2026 update changes DCC to 3.5%, late fee at INR50,000+, and adds gift-card and bridge/road/toll reward exclusions.
