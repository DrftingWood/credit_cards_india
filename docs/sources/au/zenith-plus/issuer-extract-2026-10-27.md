## Field reconciliation

- **Network, tier, launch date:** current product page explicitly has Visa and RuPay variants. The dataset's single network field cannot represent both; network and network tier are now null. No product-specific launch date was found, so `launched_on` is null. Generic `tier` is also left null because the issuer does not publish a tier classification.
- **Fees:** current product page says INR 4,999 joining and annual membership fee, renewal waiver after INR 8 lakh net retail spend in the previous card-anniversary year, and 0.99% forex markup. KFS v1.9 confirms 1.99% monthly finance charge. Crucially, the current KFS says zero cash-withdrawal fee for Zenith+; the prior 2.5%/INR 500 minimum was wrong and is corrected to zero. The KFS cash-withdrawal limit is 20% of credit limit. Current effective dates for these card-page values are not published; YAML `effective_from` is this verification snapshot, not a claim that terms began on that date.
- **Rewards:** current product page states up to 2 points per INR 100 on dining, travel, and international spends. It does not expose a base rate or exact qualifying MCCs in page text. AU Rewardz FAQ says a Zenith+ point is worth INR 1 and redemption requires 1,000 points. Current member agreement says points expire two years after accrual and charges INR 99 + GST per successful redemption. The older PDF's 1-point-per-INR-100 base rate cannot be relied on while its issuer URL is unavailable; base rate stays unknown.
- **Caps and exclusions:** the 24 Mar 2026 member agreement table lists a 25,000-point statement-cycle cap for Zenith+ with applicability to Rent, Education & Government, Insurance, Utility and Telecom MCCs; it marks other named cards as applicable to “All.” A separate table lists 1,000-point statement-cycle caps for those special categories. The agreement also says rent, education/government and BBPS do not earn points, and utility/telecom earn 1 point per INR 100, capped at 100 per transaction; it excludes EMI, fuel, cash withdrawal and quasi-cash. Because the cap table and exclusions conflict in scope, no 25,000-point card-wide cap is modeled. Current agreement does not support wallet-load or blanket insurance-premium exclusions, nor a blanket utility exclusion; those were removed.
- **Milestones:** current AU card-services FAQ lists 1,000 bonus points per statement cycle for Zenith+, effective 1 Jan 2026, but the live card page does not publish its spend threshold. It is recorded as conditional and unquantified in spend inputs. The product page also advertises a complimentary Taj Epicure membership, while the FAQ calls it an anniversary-year milestone with a spend condition. No current qualifying threshold was found, so this is not counted as unconditional welcome value.
- **Welcome benefit:** the issuer offers a choice between an INR 5,000 brand voucher and 5,000 points. Taj Epicure is no longer combined into this welcome benefit.
- **Lounges:** current product page confirms 16 domestic and 16 international accesses annually (4 per quarter). The lounge terms exempt Zenith+ from the INR 50,000 quarterly spend gate applying to other eligible cards. Priority Pass use in India is chargeable. The product page supports Visa and RuPay, so network-specific rails are not assigned. Guest eligibility is not stated on the page; left unknown.
- **Fuel waiver:** 1% on transactions INR 400–5,000, capped at INR 1,000 per statement month; confirmed by current product page and KFS.
- **Eligibility:** resident Indian; salaried 21–60, self-employed 25–65, add-on over 18; monthly net income INR 2.5 lakh. Criteria are indicative and approval is at bank discretion. Annual-income fields remain null because the issuer threshold is stated monthly; bureau-score minimum is unpublished.
- **Other benefits:** current page advertises Meet & Assist (4/year, max 1/quarter), golf games/lessons, BookMyShow tickets, complimentary device protection, air-accident cover and purchase protection. Visit limits or detailed coverage terms are not available in the current page text; amounts remain unknown.

## Remaining evidence gaps

The current base rate, exact 2X MCC mapping, special-category reward cap interaction, monthly milestone threshold, Taj Epicure spend condition, guest lounge access, network-specific benefit differences and insurance/device-protection limits are not fully published in accessible current issuer text. Do not infer these values from the unavailable older PDF or third-party summaries. The card's generic rewards remain partly non-computable until AU clarifies them.
