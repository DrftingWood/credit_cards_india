   
Terms & Conditions for Club Marriott Membership on Burgundy Private Credit Card 
  
The membership under this offer is made available to Axis Bank Burgundy Private Credit Card holders only.   
Only Primary card holders are eligible for this offer.   
To avail the membership, the card holder needs to sign the consent form and submit the same either via the  
Burgundy Private partner or by sending an e-mail to premium.experience@axisbank.com  
Club Marriott is a digital membership and will be received by the card holder within 7 working days from date 
of request.  
  
Features and Benefits of Club Marriott Membership  
  
Club Marriott membership entitles members to the following benefits:   
• Up to 25% off on Food & Beverage at Participating Marriott Hotels in India for a maximum of 20 
guests.  Select Marriott Luxury Hotels in India offer a 15% off up to a maximum of twenty (20) guests.  
• Up to 20% off on Food & Beverage at Participating Marriott Hotels in Asia Pacific (excluding India) for 
a maximum of 10 guests. Select Marriott Luxury Hotels in Asia Pacific (excluding India) offer a 15% off 
up to a maximum of ten (10) guests.  
• Up to a 20% discount on Best Available Rate for Rooms at Participating Marriott Hotels in India. 
Select Marriott Luxury Hotels in India offer a 10% discount.   
• Up to a 20% off on weekend Best Available Rate for Rooms at Participating Marriott Hotels in Asia 
Pacific. Select Luxury Marriott Hotels in Asia Pacific offer a 10% discount.  
• Up to 20% off on Spa services at select Participating Marriott Hotels in India.   
• Up to 15% off on MBOW orders at select Participating Marriott Hotels in India.  
 
Voucher Benefits (4 vouchers) valid at any participating Marriott hotel in India  
• One voucher offering a 50% discount on the food bill and a 20% discount on the beverage bill at any 
restaurant of a participating Marriott hotel in India (valid for a maximum of 10 guests)  
• Two Vouchers offering a 30% off Best Available Rate for a maximum of two nights stay each at any 
participating Marriott hotel in India subject to availability.   
• One voucher offering an upgrade to the next category of room (subject to availability and when 
booked on the Best Available Rates) for a maximum of two nights at any participating Marriott hotel 
in India
