## Field-by-field reconciliation

- **Identity / status:** Secured FD-backed Axis credit card, active. Launch date is not stated in the current issuer sources checked. Network is Visa: the live card fee schedule refers to overseas replacement through Visa Global Customer Assistance Services. Network tier is unconfirmed; ?Platinum Chip? is a chip/security description, not proof of a Visa tier.
- **Fees:** Product page and Oct KFS agree on INR 500 joining fee, INR 500 annual fee, 3.40% monthly finance charge (49.36% annual), 2.5% cash advance/minimum INR 500, and 3.5% foreign transaction fee. The Visa-assisted replacement, INR 175 cash payment, nil replacement, waived alerts/balance enquiry, nil hotlisting, 2.5% overlimit/minimum INR 500, 2% cheque/auto-debit reversal/min INR 500/max INR 1,522, railway surcharge, and unspecified reward-redemption fee are in current card page. Explicit fee schedule and KFS outweigh the unexplained comparison tile?s nil-fee claim; no qualifying offer terms were found.
- **DCC / common fees:** Card page still says DCC 1.5% + taxes; current MITC and KFS common schedule say 3.5% for Axis retail cards. Record notes this issuer conflict, with 3.5% modeled based on newer generic terms. Current MITC additionally lists rent 1% per transaction for MCC 6513, wallet 1% at cumulative INR 10,000 per statement (MCC 6540), fuel 1% at INR 50,000 (MCCs 5983/5541/5542/5172), third-party education 1% per transaction, utility 1% at INR 25,000 (MCCs 4814/4899/4900), and skill-gaming 1% at INR 10,000 (MCC 5816). Applicability to this secured variant is not fully clear where its detailed product schedule differs; amounts remain documented in the notes, not modeled as structured fees. Late-fee boundary INR 500 remains undefined; common current MITC slabs and the repeated-MAD INR 100 fee are documented.
- **Rewards:** 4 EDGE Reward Points per INR 200 on domestic transactions. Fuel and movie-offer transactions earn no points. The card page restricts movie cashback to MCC 7832/7922, so these MCCs are listed in `mcc_exclusions`. It does not establish international reward earn or other card-specific excluded categories. Avoid applying the separate unsecured My Zone card?s 2025 exclusion list to the My Zone Easy secured card. Reward redemption fee is charged, but fee amount and unit cash value are not stated.
- **Benefits:** Movie cashback is 25% all week for online and box-office transactions at MCC 7832/7922, capped at INR 1,000 per calendar year; no separate per-transaction cap is published. Fuel surcharge refund is 1% on INR 400-4,000 transactions, up to INR 400 per statement cycle; GST on surcharge is not refunded and some fuel transactions incur INR 10 + GST instead. Additional current page benefits recorded: contactless up to INR 5,000; cash access up to 100% of credit limit from day one; up to 50-day purchase grace period if prior bill paid in full; EMI conversion for transactions over INR 2,500; Dining Delights minimum 15% off at partner restaurants; and rotating Wednesday offers. Dynamic offers remain non-guaranteed/variable.
- **Eligibility:** Axis FD at a branch required, INR 15,000 minimum and INR 25 lakh maximum. Minors excluded (minimum age 18). Tax Saver, flexi/auto-sweep deposits and FDs in HUF, society, trust or company names are ineligible. The page advertises guaranteed approval conditional on product requirements. General Axis policy mentions resident Indians and NRIs, but this card page does not say whether NRI-owned FDs qualify; residency is therefore null. No card-specific max age, income or bureau score threshold is published.

## Remaining ambiguities / schema limits

Issuer materials conflict on standard fees (comparison tile vs detailed product page/KFS) and DCC (card page vs newer common schedule). We use the card page and KFS for joining/annual fee, and the newer MITC/KFS schedule for DCC while preserving both conflicts in the record notes. The product benefit schema cannot encode movie cashback percentage, MCC restriction inside the movie benefit, cashback calendar-year period distinctly, fee schedule slabs, conditional transaction fee thresholds or card-specific variation inside generic charges. The generic linked Aug-2026 PDF is not a substitute for a card-specific terms schedule.
