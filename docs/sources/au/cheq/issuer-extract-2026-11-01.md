# AU CheQ Visa and RuPay — issuer comparison (2026-11-01)

Reviewed the current AU product page, AU CheQ Rewards T&C, and the 24 March 2026 AU Card Member Agreement.

## Shared card terms

- AU currently issues both Visa and RuPay variants from one CheQ application; both variants must be closed together. Standard issuance is lifetime free; the optional LED Visa costs INR 999 plus GST once.
- Transactions must be at least INR 200. Base earns 2 CheQ points per INR 200; accelerated earn groups share a 12,000 point statement-cycle cap. Points expire two years from transaction date.
- CheQ points can be redeemed only in the CheQ app. The Member Agreement explicitly says there is no redemption fee for this program. Travel/gift card redemption is 2 points per INR 1; bill payment is 4 points per INR 1.
- Exclusions include wallet reload, jewellery/gold, rent, insurance, education, government services, fuel, EMI-converted, digital gaming, and cash transactions (issuer MCC lists in the source).
- AU card page publishes lifetime-free fees, LED surcharge, fuel waiver up to INR 150, eligibility age/residency, partner brands, and 12,000 point cap. General KFS charges apply to finance and cash advance; CheQ-specific foreign markup is 3.49%.

## Visa

12% (24 points/INR 200) on eligible partner merchant IDs and CheQ-app utility bills; 5% (10 points/INR 200) on issuer-listed MCCs; 1% base (2 points/INR 200) elsewhere eligible. Partner MID eligibility and lists can change. Visa POS/e-commerce purchases are included in accelerated groups.

## RuPay

The same accelerated rates apply to RuPay UPI transactions only when routed through CheQ App. RuPay POS/e-commerce at partner merchants and listed MCCs earns only 1% base. CheQ Travel and utility via CheQ app can earn 12% on RuPay POS/e-commerce as described in the Agreement. Other TPAP UPI transactions earn zero; other CheQ-app UPI earns 2.5% (5 points/INR 200), uncapped and not stacked with accelerated categories.

## Source limitations

Partner merchant eligibility is MID-based and may change in the CheQ app. Current pages do not publish a stable complete partner MID list. Reward point accrual and merchant classification can therefore differ from category-level estimates. Benefit coverage and insurance details need their linked policy terms for full validation.
