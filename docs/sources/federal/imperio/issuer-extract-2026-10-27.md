## Corrections and field decisions

- Filled the four APR bands: 3.75% monthly below INR 50,000; 2.69% at INR 50,000-INR 300,000; 1.99% at INR 300,001-INR 1,000,000; and 0.69% above INR 1,000,000. Prior record used a wrong 1-crore band ceiling and omitted two published rates. APR uses prior-quarter operative-account AMB and is updated quarterly.
- Changed network tier from Visa Signature to Visa Platinum based on the current issuer's Visa Imperio listing. Record describes the Visa variant; Federal Bank also offers Mastercard Imperio variants, which the one-network schema cannot represent simultaneously.
- Confirmed zero current joining/annual fees for current onboarding (limited-period offer); pre-3-Apr-2023 cohorts may retain the old INR 1,500 fee. Confirmed 3.5% forex, 2.5% cash advance (INR 500 minimum), up to four add-on cards at INR 100 plus GST each.
- Confirmed 1 point per INR 150 base, total 3x on healthcare/grocery and 2x on utilities; no accelerator cap is published. Shared exclusions include cash, fuel, wallet loads, government, financial institution/quasi-cash and rent. Insurance MCCs 6300/5960 cap at 250 points per card per calendar month. Catalog face value INR 0.25; statement credit INR 0.10. Redemption cost is INR 99/request in MITC, while FAQ calls INR 99 a minimum and says actual fee varies by redemption type.
- Confirmed two domestic lounge visits per quarter after INR 40,000 qualifying spend in the immediately preceding cycle; cycles run 21st-20th. Add-on spending counts for threshold, but access is primary-holder-only. INR 2 domestic validation charge is non-refundable; guests are chargeable.
- Refreshed welcome offer at INR 400 after INR 5,000 within 30 days. BigBasket threshold is INR 50,000 per quarter, but voucher is limited to once per financial year and selected customers only. Voucher value is not published and validity is up to 12 months. The milestone schema cannot represent a yearly award limit attached to quarterly qualification; value remains null and repeatability is left unknown.
- Updated residency to either resident or NRI based on Federal Bank FAQ. Celesta-specific age, income and bureau thresholds are not published; age remains null. No exact launch date was identified.
