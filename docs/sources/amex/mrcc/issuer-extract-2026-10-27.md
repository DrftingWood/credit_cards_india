## Reconciled fields

- Product page lists joining fee ₹1,000 and renewal fee ₹4,500. It currently lists full waiver at ₹1,50,000 preceding membership-year spend and 50% waiver at ₹90,000–₹1,49,999. The separate Amex consumer update says the 50% tier ends effective 15-Nov-2026. The card record preserves the currently displayed tier and its announced end date in the condition text.
- Official schedule lists 3.5% monthly finance charge, 3.5% foreign-currency markup, and cash advance charge 3.5% (minimum ₹250). Amex benefit page says cash withdrawals have been paused in India since 31-Jan-2022; the fee remains a contractual schedule item, not confirmation of current ATM availability.
- Product page lists 1 Membership Rewards point per ₹50, excluding fuel, insurance, utilities, cash transactions, and merchant/POS EMI. Amex says post-purchase and SafeKey EMI continue earning.
- Current Gold Collection catalogue lists 18,000-point options valued ₹6,000–₹9,000 and 24,000-point options valued ₹8,000–₹14,000; values vary by selected voucher and are not a cash redemption promise.
- Current benefits page lists 1,000 points for four monthly transactions of at least ₹1,500 each, another 1,000 points after enrollment and ₹20,000 calendar-month spend, a 4,000-point welcome bonus for paying the joining fee and spending ₹15,000 within 90 days, plus 5,000 points on first-year renewal after annual-fee payment. The renewal points are stated to be credited within 90 days.
- Effective 15-Nov-2026, Amex says monthly milestone eligibility will use transaction posting date and eligible milestone points will be credited by the end of the qualifying month. This future operational rule is documented in the benefits source notes.
- Eligibility page lists resident Indian status, age over 18, specified serviced cities, personal income at least ₹4.5 lakh salaried / ₹6 lakh self-employed, an Indian bank account, good credit history/no defaults, and more than 12 months of trading for self-employed applicants. No numeric score floor is published.
- The product page says new card applications in India are temporarily paused. Existing card usage continues. This does not establish that the product itself is discontinued.
