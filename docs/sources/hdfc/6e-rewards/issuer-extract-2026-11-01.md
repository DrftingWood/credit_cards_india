## Confirmed and corrected

- HDFC states that the card was discontinued and existing holders' card benefits ended by 31-Oct-2025 or earlier upon migration. New applications are closed. The 6E Rewards programme ended on 1-Nov-2025.
- The HDFC migration FAQ says unused 6E Rewards converted to IndiGo BluChips 1:1 on 1-Nov-2025. HDFC says BluChip value depends on route and booking timing; the former 6E point value of INR 1 is not the migrated BluChip's value. Updated the redemption description and point-value field to distinguish the historic scheme from the current airline balance.
- HDFC's card page lists historical earn at 2.5 points/INR 100 on IndiGo app/site flight bookings, 2/INR 100 on grocery, dining and entertainment capped at 100 points monthly, and 1/INR 100 on other spend.
- HDFC fees page confirms INR 500 joining and renewal fees, 3.5% foreign transaction markup and INR 99 plus tax redemption fee. The card-specific fees are historical because the card benefits ended in 2025.
- HDFC's page lists the INR 1,500 flight voucher, one 6E Prime add-on and INR 150 IndiGo convenience-fee discount. Exact welcome trigger conditions are not established by the currently accessible page and remain as an open detail.
- Eligibility page lists salaried age 21–60 and monthly gross income above INR 50,000; self-employed age 21–65 and annual ITR above INR 7.2 lakh. The YAML schema cannot store separate age bands by occupation and uses annual income, so the stored combined range and INR 600,000 annualized salaried threshold are documented approximations of issuer criteria. No issuer credit-score threshold was found.

## Open / conflicting evidence

- YAML retains the historical fuel surcharge waiver (1%, INR 400–5,000, capped INR 250) from earlier card material. HDFC's later shared MITC lists fuel surcharge waiver as not applicable for 6E Rewards Indigo, but that schedule is later than the card's 31-Oct-2025 benefit end. The historical benefit's effective period is not resolved; do not present it as a current benefit.
- The live product page continues to display legacy benefit details alongside a discontinued notice. Date-bounded fields end 31-Oct-2025; they are not current offers.
- The HDFC card-specific terms link currently redirects to unrelated Millennia terms, so card-specific detailed exclusions and welcome trigger terms could not be revalidated from that PDF.
- This record describes a discontinued card and its historical terms, not the replacement card issued to migrated customers.
