# AU Xcite insurance scope clarification (2026-11-01)

The current [Xcite product page](https://www.au.bank.in/personal-banking/credit-cards/swipeup-program/xcite-credit-card) describes card-liability cover in the card-specific benefits. It does not list air-accident cover there, although its shared footer displays an INR 50 lakh air-accident figure. AU's [28-Nov-2025 Cardmember Agreement](https://www.au.bank.in/content/dam/aubank/in/en/credit-cards/credit-card-member-agreement.pdf), sections 17.1-17.1.2, says accident cover applies only to selected card variants and requires an active card and an air ticket purchased with an AU card; it does not identify Xcite as eligible or state its insured amount. Therefore no Xcite air-accident benefit is added based on the generic footer alone.

The product page links the [Xcite insurance terms](https://www.au.bank.in/pdf/xcite-credit-card/card-liability-insurance-tnc.pdf), last updated 16-Apr-2021. Those terms describe historical policy conditions and are not treated as confirmation of the current policy. Current Xcite lost-card liability remains represented as a product-page claim with detailed limits/conditions unknown. All other card fields retain their 2026-10-26 issuer review.
