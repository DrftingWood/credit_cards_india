About the benefit: 
On purchase of one movie/non -movie ticket on BookMyShow App and website, customer can avail upto Rs.500 
off on the second ticket (movie bookings) and upto Rs. 1000 off on the second ticket (non -movie bookings). This 
benefit can be availed for a maximum of 5 times  each for movie and non -movie tickets in a month per Axis Bank 
Burgundy Private Credit Card. 
Offer validity: Offer is valid as long as card is active.  
How to avail: 
On website:  
1. Go through the regular ticketing flow for selecting the movie, cinema and show of your choice  
2. Proceed to payments page  
3. Confirm your details by providing your email id & mobile number as this information will be used for 
confirming your transaction  
4. To avail offer, check “Unlock Offers or Apply promo codes”.  
5. Click on the tab – Credit Card  
6. Click on 'Select Offer' and click on 'Axis Bank Credit Card offer' from the offers dro p down list  
7. Enter your 16 digit credit card number in the box and click on 'Check'  
8. Congratulations! You would have successfully availed your offer  
9. You will have to pay the remaining transaction amount using the same card on which you have availed 
the offer. Your card number will auto populate in the box. To make the payment, enter your name on 
the card, expiry date and CVV card details.
