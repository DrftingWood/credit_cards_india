## Field decisions

- Corrected launch date to 24-Aug-2022 based on the issuer release. HDFC says Tata Neu Infinity had Visa and RuPay variants. This record is the RuPay variant.
- Fees match current issuer materials: ?1,499 joining/renewal, waiver after ?3 lakh spend before renewal, 2.5% forex, monthly finance 3.75%, and cash advance 2.5% with ?500 minimum.
- NeuCoins are modeled as points, not cash: the terms say 1 NeuCoin = ?1 on eligible Tata Neu/Tata-brand redemption, with no cash conversion. The current MITC gives Infinity a 50,000-point statement-cycle earn ceiling.
- Non-EMI eligible partner Tata-brand transactions earn 5% total (1.5% base + 3.5% additional), subject to the brand-specific monthly/annual spend limits in Annex 1 and MID/TID recognition. Tata Pay's monthly cap increased from ?50,000 to ?100,000 effective 1-Aug-2024; annual cap remains ?600,000. Non-Tata and merchant EMI spends earn 1.5%.
- Non-UPI grocery, insurance, utility, and telecom/cable each earn 1.5%, capped separately at 2,000 NeuCoins/month. RuPay UPI earns 0.5% on eligible UPI IDs and an extra 1% for Tata Neu UPI ID, all UPI sharing 500/month. Existing capping note remains that UPI is not scored in calculator inputs.
- Additional 5% through NeuPass on selected Tata Neu categories is described separately from card rewards. It requires NeuPass enrollment and primary-cardholder mobile matching; current issuer page excludes Tata Pay, Tanishq, Cult.fit, Air India and Tata Play. Eligible categories vary and no universal cap/list is specified, so it is documented without assigning it to all Tata Neu purchases or scoring it.
- HDFC product page says eligible Indian salaried applicants age 21-60 and monthly income ?1,00,000; self-employed age 21-65 and ITR above ?12,00,000. HDFC eligibility FAQ explicitly requires Indian residency; no credit-score minimum is published.
- Welcome benefit is 1,499 NeuCoins (first-year fee reversal) for the first transaction within 30 days; lifetime-free/first-year-free cards excluded. Current product page describes a 60-day claim window and a separate eligible-customer 180-day-from-statement window; both are preserved in the record.
- Domestic lounge vouchers: ?50,000 spend in the quarter unlocks 2 per quarter, max 8/year; claim within 120 days and use within 180 days. RuPay international access is 4/year, 1/quarter; the temporary US$3.25 transaction is reversed.
- The current product page states insurance amounts of ?1 crore accidental air death, ?15 lakh overseas emergency hospitalization and ?9 lakh lost-card liability. Linked insurance PDF gives policy period 23-Jul-2026 to 22-Jul-2027 but also says the benefits are valid only through 22-Jul-2026. The record retains amounts and documents this internal validity-date conflict; policy covers primary cardholder and includes activation/trip/claim conditions.
- HDFC page announces a change from 25-Nov-2026: NeuCoins must be manually claimed within 12 months or expire; once transferred to Tata Neu, they remain valid another 12 months. As this effective date is in the future relative to this review, current behavior remains recorded with the upcoming change noted.
