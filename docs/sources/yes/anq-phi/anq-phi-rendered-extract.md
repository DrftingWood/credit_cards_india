## Official Anq Phi product-guide terms (issuer-indexed PDF text)

- 24 points per INR200 for groceries, supermarkets, travel, dining, hotels, apparel, online entertainment, digital goods and OTT; 4 points per INR200 for other eligible categories. The guide describes earning as uncapped.
- Reward exclusions include fuel, rent (up to three rental transactions in a rolling 30 days), wallet loads, government services, ATM withdrawals, utility/telecom bill payments, insurance, toll/bridge, education, jewellery/watches/precious metals, hospitals, cash advances, fees/charges and EMI conversions. June-2026 issuer-wide exclusions also apply.
- Three complimentary international lounge visits per calendar year; up to three add-on cards.
- Fuel waiver: 1% for INR400-5,000 in the older product guide, capped at INR300 per statement. The current MITC/change update supersedes the band with INR500-3,000 while retaining the INR300 cap.
