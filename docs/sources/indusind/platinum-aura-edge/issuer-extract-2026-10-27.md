# IndusInd Platinum Aura Edge issuer source review (27 Oct 2026)

## Sources checked

- [Current Premium Card MITC, dated 13 Oct 2026](https://www.indusind.bank.in/content/dam/indusind-corporate/schedule-of-charges/credit-cards/MITC_Premium.pdf): product fee table, interest, cash advance, foreign markup and DCC.
- [Platinum Aura Edge Benefit Guide, dated 14 Jan 2026](https://www.indusind.bank.in/content/dam/indusind-corporate/Other/welcomekit/credit-card-benefits/Platinum-Aura-Edge-Benefit-Guide-14-01-2026.pdf): plan rates, fuel waiver, travel cover, Total Protect, redemption and plan change details.
- [Current issuer product page](https://www.indusind.bank.in/in/en/personal/cards/credit-card/platinum-aura-edge-visa-and-mastercard-credit-card-easy-credit.html): lifetime-free positioning and current cash-redemption cap.
- [Issuer reward program update page](https://www.indusind.bank.in/in/en/personal/credit-card-related/important-updates-reward-points-program.html): monthly reward cap table. The product page specifies the limit as a cash-redemption cap; it is not modelled as an earn cap.

## Record changes

- Confirmed nil joining and annual fees, 3.95% monthly finance charge, 2.5% cash advance fee subject to ₹300 minimum and 3.5% foreign currency markup from current MITC. The same MITC lists a separate 2% DCC fee for Aura Edge; this is recorded here because the card schema has no DCC field.
- Replaced the previous single-plan reward claim with four mutually exclusive choices: Shop, Home, Travel and Party. Each selected plan earns 0.5 points per ₹100 on other spends, with category accelerators documented separately from the Jan-2026 issuer guide. The plan is chosen during application; change is available after six months for ₹250. Cash credit is ₹0.40 per point and capped at 2,500 points per calendar month.
- Corrected fuel surcharge waiver to 1% on transactions between ₹500 and ₹3,000. Issuer says a monthly cap exists but the reviewed source does not state its amount; GST on the surcharge is excluded.
- Added Travel Plus cover limits: lost baggage ₹1,00,000; delayed baggage ₹25,000; lost passport ₹50,000; lost ticket ₹25,000; missed connection ₹25,000. The guide conditions the cover on international travel and advance/return notification. Added Total Protect fraud cover up to assigned credit limit, subject to reporting and claim documentation conditions.
- Removed unsupported launch date and unverified specific age, income and credit-score thresholds.

## Unresolved or source limits

- The fuel-waiver monthly cap amount is unpublished in the reviewed product guide.
- The MITC lists a 2% DCC fee plus GST separately from the 3.5% foreign markup.
- Product page and benefit guide identify different insurers for Travel Plus coverage. The record preserves benefits/limits and refers to policy terms without asserting an insurer; insurer identity needs issuer confirmation.
- The reviewed current sources do not establish product-specific age, income or credit-score thresholds.
- The exact launch date remains unknown.
