2. Current Live+ guide/page states two domestic plus one international visit; HSBC generic lounge FAQ still lists four domestic. Current product-specific Live+ language is represented; no access provider or guest count is inferred.
3. The issuer does not explicitly say whether 10% category spend continues earning 1.5% after the INR1,200 monthly accelerator cap. The record does not state a verified post-cap rule; downstream consumers may apply the schema?s default base-after-cap behavior.
