# IDFC FIRST Private: issuer comparison (2026-10-27)

Reviewed the current [FIRST Private product page](https://www.idfcfirst.bank.in/credit-card/FIRSTPrivateCreditCard), 17-Jul-2026 consolidated terms, current general credit-card policy, the issuer's current [insurance overview](https://www.idfcfirst.bank.in/credit-card/benefits/insurance-and-protection), and the standalone CFAR PDF linked from the product page. Evidence copies and dated page captures are listed in the IDFC FIRST source manifest.

## Field decisions

- New cards issued from 17-Jul-2026 have a INR50,000 + GST commencement fee, INR25,000 + GST annual fee from year two, and 50,000 reward points after commencement-fee payment. Terms for cards issued earlier are not fully described on the current page; do not apply the new issue cohort's fee values to legacy accounts.
- Current reward terms are 10 points/INR150 on eligible domestic spends, 5/INR150 on eligible international spends, and 1/INR150 on listed categories. Points accrue only up to the credit limit per statement cycle; repayments and re-use do not reset that cap. Fuel, cash withdrawals, EMI-converted transactions, quasi-cash, fees/GST, loans-on-card and balance transfers do not earn standard points. Travel Gallery bonus terms grant 50/20 additional points per INR150 on eligible hotel/flight bookings.
- The issuer's card-specific July terms support unlimited airport lounges, four accompanied domestic guest visits per year, international ticketed travel insurance, and the listed lounge access conditions. The product page and consolidated terms show separate flight and hotel CFAR limits of INR25,000 each, shareable between primary/add-on cardholders across up to two transactions. Current policy runs from 8-May-2026 or the later card-issue date through 7-May-2027; booking and cancellation must fall within the policy period and cancellation must be at least 24 hours before travel.
- The one-page CFAR PDF linked by the issuer is stale: it ends 7-May-2025. The July-2026 consolidated terms and current product page provide the current policy period. The card record follows those newer card-specific terms and flags the stale linked PDF.
- The July terms support INR2.5 crore personal air-accident cover, INR5 lakh purchase protection, and USD500/300/100/300 travel covers. IDFC's insurance overview lists INR25 lakh personal-accident cover and INR5 lakh lost-card liability. The FIRST Private page's insurance subsection instead states INR50,000 zero card-liability cover. The record includes the overview's personal-accident amount and preserves the lost-card limit conflict, using the card-specific INR50,000 value.
- Current rewards and lifestyle claims were checked against detailed terms, including BookMyShow discounts (up to INR750 twice monthly), golf, railway lounge, meet-and-greet, Postcard Hotels, Taj Epicure and roadside assistance. Existing spending milestones ended for new card terms from 17-Jul-2026; YAML retains legacy milestone offers with their card cohort limits.
- Residency is supported only by IDFC's general credit-card application page; the current FIRST Private page does not publish numeric age, income or bureau-score thresholds.

## Open discrepancies and modeling limits

- Lost-card/zero-liability limit: INR50,000 in the current FIRST Private page versus INR5 lakh in IDFC's current insurance overview.
- The general insurance overview's INR25 lakh personal-accident limit is not restated in the card-specific consolidated terms, although those terms include personal-accident conditions.
- New issue fee terms began 17-Jul-2026; the live page does not fully explain fees for existing cardholders.
- The linked standalone CFAR PDF is stale, although the consolidated card terms contain the newer 2026-27 policy.
- Cash-advance fee, current account-specific APR after the 9% onboarding rate, and some partner campaign conditions are not fully represented by the schema.
