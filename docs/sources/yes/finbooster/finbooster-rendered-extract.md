## Current issuer terms represented

- Joining and renewal charges are NIL; the current issuer listing says the card carries a free Credit Strong subscription. Duration and activation details were not available in the reviewed content.
- Current rewards listing: 10 YES Rewardz per INR 200 on online dining; 6 per INR 200 on online grocery and apparel; 2 per INR 200 on other eligible retail spend except fuel. No total rewards cap is shown in the reviewed current page or MITC table.
- MITC v18 gives 3.99% monthly finance, 3.5% foreign-currency markup effective 15-Jun-2026, 1% DCC charge, and cash advance fee 2.5% or INR 650 minimum effective 15-Jun-2026. The FinBooster-specific fuel waiver is 1% for INR 500-3,000 transactions, capped at INR 100 per statement cycle; UPI fuel is excluded and GST on the surcharge is not waived.
- June 2026 issuer notice adds reward exclusions for insurance (MCC 6300, 6381), education (8211, 8220, 8249, 8299, 8241, 8244, 7911), railways (4112, 4011), tolls (4784), ferry (4111), and jewellery (5944). The issuer notes MCC lists are indicative and may change.
- Generic YES Rewardz information states point value up to INR 0.25; actual value is redemption-channel dependent. MITC gives INR 100 + GST redemption fee per request and three-year point expiry.
