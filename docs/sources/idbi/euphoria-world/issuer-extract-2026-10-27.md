## Current issuer extracts recorded

- Fees: nil joining; ₹1,499 annual from year two; 3.5% forex; 2.9% monthly finance; cash advance 2.5% or ₹500 minimum. Waiver source wording differs at the ₹1,50,000 boundary: the product page says “more than”; KFS states ₹1,50,000.
- Rewards: 3 Delight Points per ₹100 generally; 6 per ₹100 on airline, hotel, IRCTC and bus purchases. FAQ states 0.25 paisa per point (₹0.0025), with 4,000 point cashback and 1,000 point services minimums; points expire after three years. The unusually small stated conversion should be confirmed against a live redemption transaction/catalog before relying on estimated rupee value.
- Benefits: 500 points on activation; 750 points on first ₹5,000+ transaction within 30 days; 1,500 points at ₹2 lakh net annual spend and 2,000 at ₹4 lakh; 12 domestic lounge visits per calendar year; fuel waiver 1% on ₹400–₹5,000 transactions, capped at ₹500/month; air accident cover ₹25 lakh and lost-card fraud cover up to ₹5 lakh subject to issuer conditions.
- Eligibility: resident Indian; age 21–60 salaried and 21–65 self-employed. No specific income or credit-score minimum found. Historical launch date not established.
