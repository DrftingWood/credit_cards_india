## Corrections and verified fields

- Corrected launch date from 1-Mar-2023 to 1-Sep-2022, the publication/launch date shown in SBI's issuer media record. Kept Visa as the supported network from SBI's Cashback Visa brochure and card terms; cleared Visa Platinum because no card-specific Visa tier is stated in the issuer sources.
- Confirmed the current joining fee is INR999 plus tax from SBI's active application consent, annual fee INR999 and renewal fee waiver at INR2 lakh preceding-year spend from SBI's MITC. Historical start dates for these fee values remain unverified.
- Confirmed the 1-Apr-2026 reward structure: 5% online cashback capped at INR2,000/statement cycle and 1% offline POS cashback capped at INR2,000/statement cycle; combined cap INR4,000. Online over-cap spends do not earn the offline rate. SBI's FAQ uses posting/settlement date for accrual, reversals for returns, and credit within two working days of statement generation.
- Retained online as a payment-channel classifier: issuer terms say the network/acquirer online indicator determines online versus offline classification. This rule can apply across product categories; it is not an online-shopping merchant allowlist.
- Confirmed per-transaction cashback rounding down, the INR100 minimum spend condition, eligible MCCs and exclusion list from the terms and 25-Feb-2026 revision notice. The added 1-Apr-2026 exclusions are gaming MCCs 7993/7994/5816, toll MCC 4784, and government MCCs 9222/9311/9402.
- Confirmed 1% fuel surcharge waiver on eligible INR500–3,000 transactions, capped at INR100 per statement cycle, from the card terms. Fuel itself does not earn cashback.
- Replaced the unsupported aggregator-only INR6-lakh income note with unknown card-specific income/score cutoffs and SBI's general occupation-specific age bands. Current issuer sources do not publish a card-specific minimum income or fixed credit-score requirement.

## Remaining source limits
