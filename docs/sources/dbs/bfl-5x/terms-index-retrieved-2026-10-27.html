Important update:

Effective 1st August 2026, the Bajaj Finserv DBS Bank SuperCard will be discontinued, and the cardholders will be migrated to the DBS SuperX Credit Card or DBS SuperX Plus Credit Card by DBS Bank. Please refer to the communication sent on your registered email id and mobile number for additional details.

Effective 1st April 2026, a fee of 1% of total spends if cumulative spends exceed ₹10,000 in a statement cycle will be applicable on select travel transactions. (Applicable for Travel Merchant Category Codes (MCC): 4111 - Local and suburban commuter passenger transportation, including ferries, 4131 - Bus lines, 4784 - Tolls and bridge fees).

Effective 1st April 2026, a fee of 1% of total spends if cumulative spends exceed ₹20,000 in a statement cycle will be applicable on select travel transactions (Passenger Railways). (Applicable for Travel Merchant Category Codes (MCC): 4112).

Effective 1st April 2026, a fee of 1% of transaction on transactions of ₹5,000 or more will be applicable on wallet-load transactions. (Applicable for Merchant Category Code (MCC) – 6540).
