# IDFC FIRST WOW! issuer comparison (27 Oct 2026)

Compared the current [FIRST WOW! product page](https://www.idfcfirst.bank.in/credit-card/wow), [May-2026 FIRST WOW! update](https://www.idfcfirst.bank.in/content/dam/idfcfirstbank/pdf/credit-card/FIRST-WOW-Credit-Card-Communication-May-26.pdf), [earlier card update](https://www.idfcfirst.bank.in/content/dam/idfcfirstbank/pdf/credit-card/FIRST-WOW-Credit-Card-Important-Update.pdf), and current customer MITC. Retrieved source copies are archived alongside this extract.

## Current source-backed terms

- Product page identifies the Visa Infinite platform. The date 2021-06-01 previously used as the launch date was not established by a launch announcement; launch date cleared.
- Joining/annual fee INR0; forex markup 0%; ATM cash-withdrawal fee INR199 + GST per withdrawal; ATM cash withdrawals interest-free for up to 45 days. Issuer gives variable APR 8.5%-46.2% p.a.; no fixed monthly rate asserted.
- 1 Reward Point per INR200 is the base; 4X on eligible online, offline and international transactions, and 1X on insurance premiums, utility bills, FASTag and railway. Fuel, EMI and cash withdrawals earn no points. 1 point is valued at INR0.25. A redemption convenience fee INR99 + GST applies. Current page says points expire 24 months from credit.
- May-2026 notice says existing balance as of the July-2026 statement cycle remains valid through July 2028; points earned from July-2026 statement cycle onward are valid 24 months from credit. This supersedes the older Dec-2025 notice saying points never expire.
- May-2026 notice: 1% fuel surcharge waiver capped at INR200/month; transaction bounds were not stated. It also confirms roadside assistance valued at INR1,399.
- Standard WOW comparison column has no domestic lounge access, joining welcome package, or annual fee; those lounge/welcome claims in the old YAML were WOW Black terms.
- Indian resident eligibility: age 18-80, Indian citizen/permanent India address, singly-held IDFC FIRST FD at least INR20,000. Limit is 100% of FD; no income proof or credit history required. NRI route uses NRE account and NRE FD minimum INR25,000.

## Unresolved or excluded claims

- Current issuer page gives a variable APR range, not a fixed monthly finance rate.
- Earlier December-2025 update advertised personal accident cover INR200,000 and lost-card liability INR25,000; the newer May-2026 update and current product page do not reaffirm those covers, so neither is asserted in the structured record.
- Fuel surcharge waiver minimum/maximum transaction amounts are not published in the reviewed current card-specific sources.
- No reliable issuer launch date found.
