## Current fields supported by issuer evidence

- **Status and identity:** Current product page presents the Premium card and an application path. Current sources reviewed do not establish one network or network tier for this Premium product; Mastercard/Visa references in older terms are MCC examples and the lounge program supports several networks. RuPay/Select has therefore been cleared. The 2019 launch release is for the predecessor standard co-brand; no exact Premium launch date is asserted.
- **Fees:** Current page says INR 1,000 + GST joining/annual, with INR 30,000 anniversary spend waiver; 3.5% forex and DCC; 3.75% monthly finance; cash advance 2.5%/INR 500 minimum; late-fee slabs; and 2.5%/INR 500 minimum over-limit fee. Current MITC confirms add-on fee nil and the generic cash-advance fee. The page also publishes rent (1%), education-app, wallet, fuel, utility and gaming transaction fees, cash-payment fee, and repeated-MAD charge; these are summarized in YAML notes where the schema has no dedicated field.
- **Rewards:** 1 EDGE Mile per INR 150 base with no overall cap. At listed IndianOil outlets using authorized IOCL EDCs, the issuer advertises 6 total miles per INR 150 up to INR 15,000 monthly fuel spend, then 1 base mile per INR 150. The accelerated band total is 600 miles. Grocery/supermarket earns 2 total miles per INR 150 through INR 5,000 monthly, then base only; 33 total miles maximum in the 2X band. The prior record's 66-mile grocery cap double-counted base and bonus miles.
- **Transaction exclusions:** The 2025-06-20 card-specific update says rewards are excluded on Utility & Telecom, Education, Rent, Wallet load, Government services, Insurance, Gold & Jewellery, Financial Institutions, Cash advances and Repayments. It says these exclusions are identified by category rather than MCC. The bankwide 2026 update also excludes gift cards, bridge fees, road fees and tolls. Unsupported isolated MCC 6513 has been removed from `mcc_exclusions`.
- **Reward value/redemption:** Card terms assign INR 1 per EDGE Mile for eligible IOCL fuel redemption (minimum 500 miles). Current EDGE Rewards FAQ lists INR 1 per mile for Pay by Rewards against card outstanding, minimum 10,000 and maximum 50,000 miles per redemption, with expected credit within 7 working days. The card receives a fee waiver for redemption. Partner transfers, Travel EDGE bookings and catalogue items vary, so no single realized value or global floor/ceiling is asserted.
- **Welcome and benefits:** 500 miles on first transaction within 30 days, no minimum spend, credited T+12; not for existing-customer swaps or certain pre-31-Jul-2024 migrations. Zomato offer: 30% off, up to INR 150/order, minimum INR 200, twice per calendar month. Fuel surcharge waiver: 1% at listed authorized IOCL outlets, described as unlimited; GST is not refunded. The current lounge program lists two domestic visits per quarter and requires INR 50,000 settled eligible spend in the preceding three calendar months; newly issued cards are exempt for issue month plus the next three calendar months. Current page also lists Wednesday Delight and Dining Delights; these remain descriptive because merchant offers rotate.
- **Eligibility:** Primary cardholder age 18–70, resident of India; criteria are indicative. Numeric income and bureau thresholds are not published.

## Conflicts and limits

- **Annual fee waiver threshold:** The current product page and its page-linked card-terms PDF both say INR 30,000 per anniversary year. An older issuer-hosted alternate T&C indexed by search says INR 100,000 for customers issued from 2024-07-01, but that URL now redirects to a sitemap and the applicable version/population is not clear. YAML follows the currently linked page and terms (INR 30,000) and flags the conflict for recheck.
- **Fee-waiver exclusions:** The linked card-terms copy mentions Wallet, Rent and Insurance; the dated 2025-06-20 notice says only Rent and Wallet are excluded from fee-waiver spend. YAML follows the dated revision and records the linked-copy conflict.
- **Returned-payment fee:** Current product page still says a INR 450 minimum; the specific 20-Dec-2024 issuer notice revises the minimum to INR 500 and removes the maximum cap. YAML follows the dated notice.
- **Fuel waiver and rewards scope:** The product page says all IOCL fuel outlets, while the terms and current outlet list limit accelerated miles and reversal eligibility to authorized/listed EDC outlets. YAML preserves that operational qualification.
- **Lounge access:** The product page says two visits per quarter but omits the INR 50,000 gate stated in the linked general lounge program; the general program is followed.
