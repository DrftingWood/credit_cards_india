## Terms reflected in the record

- Current membership: INR 499 joining and renewal fee; renewal waiver at INR 120,000 qualifying spend in the preceding year. The issuer material reviewed did not state the start date for this fee schedule.
- Cashback: 1% on transactions not converted to EMI, with no minimum transaction threshold and a maximum INR 500 per calendar month. Cashback posts in the statement following the end of the month. Card-specific terms exclude fuel, cash withdrawals and other categories under issuer policy; no complete current category/MCC list was available, so unsupported rent, wallet, insurance and MCC 6513 exclusions were removed.
- Automatic EMI: minimum eligible purchase INR 5,000; INR 5,000–10,000 converts to 3 months, INR 10,001–20,000 to 6 months, and INR 20,001–500,000 to 12 months. Interest is 18% p.a. on a reducing monthly balance; processing fee is 1% plus GST. Cash withdrawals, fees/charges, merchant EMI and specified categories are excluded. The bank can pause auto-conversion for overdue dues and caps running EMIs at 60.
- General MITC terms effective 15-Jun-2026: 3.99% monthly finance charge; 3.5% foreign-currency markup; cash-advance fee 2.5% or INR 650, whichever is higher.
- Fuel surcharge reversal: 1% on INR 500–3,000 transactions, maximum INR 250 per statement cycle; GST on the surcharge is not reversed. The issuer did not show a YES EMI Card lounge entitlement in the reviewed product material.

## Unknowns retained
