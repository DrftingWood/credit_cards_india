## Current issuer statements captured

- YES Rewardz earning: 8 points per INR 200 for online shopping, 4 per INR 200 for offline shopping, and 2 per INR 200 on issuer-defined select categories. The displayed offers exclude select categories as described on the page.
- Overall reward cap: 5,000 points per statement cycle. Utility MCC 4900 earning is capped at 150 points per statement cycle.
- Effective 2026-06-15, the page excludes rent, wallet, fuel, UPI, government and marketing/advertising transactions, cash withdrawals, transactions converted to EMI, insurance, education, railways, toll, ferry and jewellery from reward earning.
- Eligibility: age 21–60; salaried or self-employed; minimum net salary INR 25,000/month or ITR INR 750,000 and above. Existing YES Bank cardholders are not eligible for regular ACE; a virtual RuPay variant is stated for existing Mastercard/Visa ACE holders.
- Joining fee INR 499, reversed after INR 5,000 eligible spend within one month of setup; renewal fee INR 499, reversed after INR 50,000 spend in the preceding 12 months.
- The page lists a fuel surcharge waiver, purchase protection and buy-one-get-one coffee at eligible outlets inside select malls. Numeric purchase protection terms were not exposed in the rendered material and are not asserted.
- Page copy includes an older fuel band. The database uses the newer issuer MITC/change notice effective 2026-06-15 for the current INR 500–3,000 transaction band and INR 100 statement cap.
