# SBI Card ELITE Business: issuer comparison (27 Oct 2026)

Compared the current SBI sitemap and MITC/KFS records with the product URL and SBI’s 5-Dec-2019 ELITE Business card booklet.

## Verified and recorded

- The current SBI sitemap still lists ELITE Business, but its linked product page returns 404. The current fee schedule does not name the variant, so exact joining, annual and renewal fees and a waiver are not populated. Its 2019 booklet stated annual/renewal fees “up to INR4,999,” which is historical, not evidence of current fees.
- Current MITC gives a 2.5% cash advance fee with INR500 minimum. It gives 3.75% monthly finance for unsecured cards, but issuer evidence reviewed does not establish whether this product is secured or unsecured, so the product record’s finance rate is now null. Forex for ELITE Business is also not established by current materials.
- Archived 2019 card terms identify Mastercard and historical earn/benefit terms. Removed the unsupported current Visa Signature network claim. Current network/tier, launch date, rewards, benefits and business-specific eligibility remain unverified; historic reward and benefit statements are clearly identified as historical notes and not current benefits.
- Removed the application URL because it returns 404. The issuer sitemap’s continued listing alone does not establish current issuance or current card benefits.
