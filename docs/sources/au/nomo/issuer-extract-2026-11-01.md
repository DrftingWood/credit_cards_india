# AU NOMO — issuer comparison (2026-11-01)

Compared the current NOMO product page and FAQs, current AU Card Member Agreement (16-May-2026), AU lounge policy effective 10-Apr-2026, current KFS/Schedule of Charges v1.9 (30-Sep-2026), and AU Rewardz FAQ.

## Core terms

- FD-secured Visa card. One-time joining fee INR 199 + GST; no annual fee from year two. Forex 0.99%. General current AU charges: finance 3.75% monthly, cash advance 2.5% or INR 500 minimum.
- Current product page requires single-owner AU FD of at least INR 20,000, cumulative/reinvesting and auto-renewal, 24-month tenure; 100% lien. The 16-May-2026 agreement gives secured-card limit up to 80% of FD, with actual amount at bank discretion; partial FD withdrawal is unavailable while card/dues remain.
- Resident Indian; age 18–99 per card page; income and score floors not published.

## Rewards

- Base: 2 points/INR 100 retail, 1 point/INR 100 utility and insurance. Total cap 5,000 points per statement cycle. Welcome 500 points on first transaction within 30 days.
- Quarterly milestone: 500 points at INR 25,000 eligible retail and 1,000 total points at INR 50,000, per current product page; thresholds are tiered, not additive. Fuel and cash excluded.
- Point value is unresolved: NOMO FAQ states INR 0.25 per point; AU’s general Rewardz FAQ states 0.25 paise (INR 0.0025), a 100x conflict. Value remains null.
- General AU reward redemption minimum 200 points and INR 99 + GST per successful redemption; points expire two years from statement month; closure prevents redemption. Exclusions/capping and redemption timing follow AU reward-program terms.

## Lounge and fuel conflicts

- Current AU spend-based lounge policy effective 10-Apr-2026 specifies two visits per calendar quarter for NOMO after INR 50,000 net retail spend in the previous calendar quarter; primary cardholder only. NOMO product-page main feature section also says INR 50,000. A stale FAQ on the same product page still says INR 20,000 previous-quarter spend; modeled value follows current policy and main feature section.
- NOMO product page specifies a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on INR 400–5,000, capped at INR 100. AU KFS/Schedule of Charges v1.9 (30-Sep-2026) groups NOMO under a INR 150 monthly cap. Card-specific page value is retained in the data with the newer general schedule conflict disclosed pending clarification.
- Railway lounge: two visits per quarter for primary and add-on cardholders under card-page terms; INR 2 validation charge and guests charged.
