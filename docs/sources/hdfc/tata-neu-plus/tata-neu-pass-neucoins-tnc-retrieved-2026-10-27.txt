1) How do I get additional 5% Tata NeuCoins on selected categories via Neu Pass/Tata Neu Reward
   Membership on Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Cards?
      •   Only the primary card holder will get additional 5% Tata NeuCoins on selected categories via NeuPass / Tata
          Neu Rewards Membership on transactions done via Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card.

      •   For this benefit you need to login to the Tata Neu App/website with the primary cardholder’s Mobile Number
          given during the credit card application and complete the transaction using the Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit
          Card.

      •   Eligible Categories where additional 5% Tata NeuCoins are applicable, and its terms and conditions will be
          updated on Tata Neu from time to time.

      •   For the additional 5% Tata NeuCoins via NeuPass / Tata Neu Rewards, the mobile number on Tata Neu, the
          mobile number used on the partner brand app / website, and the mobile number of the NeuCard used for
          payment must all be the same (i.e. the primary cardholder’s number), as shown below:

      •   For select brands / categories, you may be asked to activate your benefits on the Tata Neu app before you
          transact on the partner brand App/Website, wherever applicable, simply follow the instructions on the Tata
          Neu app to activate and avail your benefits. Please ensure that you:
           a) Log into Tata Neu with the Primary Cardholder Mobile Number
           b) Activate your benefits on Tata Neu for next 24 hours (Benefit Period)
           c) Log into the partner brand app/web with the Primary Cardholder Mobile Number
              o In case you are already logged in the partner brand app/web with different mobile number, then
                  ensure to re-login with primary card holder mobile number to avail the benefit
              o It is suggested to check the Activation Status of your benefit in Tata Neu App, before initiating the
                  payment on the partner brand app/website and re-activate the benefit in Tata Neu App, in case the
                  Benefit Period has expired
           d) pay using the Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card within the Benefit Period
              o re-activate the Benefit Period by visiting Tata Neu App, before initiating your transaction in case
                  Benefit Period has expired.

      •   It will take up to 45 working days from the date of issuance of the card, for us to enroll you to NeuPass / Tata
          Neu Rewards Membership on the Mobile Number as per your Credit Card Application. Transactions done
          before enrolment for the NeuPass / Tata Neu Rewards Membership will not accrue this benefit.
