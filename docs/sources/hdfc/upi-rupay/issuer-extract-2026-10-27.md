# HDFC Bank UPI RuPay Credit Card review (27 October 2026)

Rechecked HDFC's [current card page](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/credit-cards/hdfc-bank-upi-rupay-credit-card), [current fees page](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/credit-cards/hdfc-bank-upi-rupay-credit-card/fees-and-charges), linked card-specific T&Cs (27-Mar-2025), HDFC co-branded MITC v3.9 (April 2026) and personal MITC v4.15 (updated 17-Oct-2026). Current/previous issuer PDFs and retrieval-date copies are preserved in this directory and `../_shared/`.

## Current terms encoded

- HDFC describes this as a virtual RuPay floater/add-on card, requiring an existing or newly issued primary HDFC Visa, Mastercard or Diners card. No physical card is issued. It can be linked to PayZapp, GPay, PhonePe, Mobikwik, PayTM, BHIM, Slice and other supported apps. The current product page and card terms do not specify a RuPay tier; the old Platinum value was removed. No exact issuer launch date was found, so the old 2023-06-10 date was cleared.
- Joining and annual membership fees are INR 99 plus tax; annual fee is waived from year two after at least INR 25,000 eligible retail spend in the prior annual year. Cash advances, balance payments, charges and EMI do not qualify. The co-branded MITC says the card fee row applies from 04-Oct-2023; this is not treated as an exact product launch date.
- The card-specific current Fees & Charges page says 3.6% monthly interest. The current HDFC personal MITC v4.15 and co-branded MITC v3.9 give 3.75% for other variants. The card-specific live fee page value is represented, with the official-source disagreement documented. Cash advance fee is 2.5% or INR 500, whichever is higher. The current general MITC schedule supports a 3.5% foreign-currency markup; the UPI card fees page does not separately state an exception.
- Earn CashPoints under the reward-points scheme: 3% on grocery/supermarket MCC 5411, dining MCC 5812/5814, and all PayZapp transactions; 2% on utility MCC 4900; 1% on other eligible transactions. Each feature has its own 500 CashPoint calendar-month cap. Transactions below INR 100 are ineligible. Points are calculated on settled transactions in the calendar month and post in the first week of the next month; PayZapp CashPoints may post separately. Both UPI and regular card transactions qualify.
- Exclusions include fuel, rent, EMI, insurance, government and education transactions, wallet/gift/prepaid-card loads and voucher purchases (including MCC 6540), cash advances, outstanding-balance payments, card fees and charges. HDFC's live page separately announces a 1% fee from 1-Oct-2025 on fuel transactions above INR 15,000, capped at INR 4,999 per transaction. It also says UPI payments have no fuel surcharge; whether the separate fee is UPI-only or applies to all fuel transactions is unclear.
- CashPoints have a face and statement-credit value of INR 0.25 each and expire after two years. HDFC's current live page says statement redemption starts at 500 points; the linked 27-Mar-2025 terms say 2,000 points. This minimum remains unresolved and unmodeled. Those card terms say statement-credit redemption has no handling fee; catalogue redemption costs INR 99 plus tax. Travel redemption is at INR 0.25/point, up to 50% of booking value and subject to a monthly 50,000-point limit. Select catalogue vouchers can use up to 70% points.
- Published credit benefits: up to 50 days interest-free when eligible; INR 25,000 annual renewal-fee waiver; virtual-only card. No lounge-access benefit or numeric fuel-surcharge waiver is published. HDFC's fuel-fee wording conflict is documented under benefits.
- HDFC publishes the add-on-card requirement and no separate salary, age or minimum credit-score threshold on the card page; those values remain null.

## Corrections made

Corrected joining/annual fees from zero to INR 99; set annual renewal threshold to INR 25,000; changed monthly finance rate from 3.75% to the current specific card-fee-page value of 3.6% while retaining the 3.75% MITC conflict; cleared the unsupported Platinum RuPay tier and exact launch date; removed the unsupported INR 0.22 realized CashPoint value in favor of the sourced INR 0.25; refreshed point caps, minimum eligible transaction, exclusions, current redemption conflict, add-on eligibility, and fuel-fee disclosure; and removed the unsupported fuel-surcharge waiver value.
