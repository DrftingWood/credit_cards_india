## Corrections and updates

- Corrected launch date from 1-Oct-2021 to 3-Feb-2021.
- Corrected the network from Mastercard World Elite to a multi-network offering. SBI's current AURUM pages show Visa Infinite and Mastercard World privileges and publish domestic lounge lists for both. The card schema has one network/tier slot, so these are null with a `multi-network` tag.
- Current joining/annual fee is INR9,999; renewal waiver threshold INR12 lakh annual spend; forex 1.99%; current general finance charge up to 3.75% monthly; cash advance 2.5%, INR500 minimum. The current fee sources do not publish an effective date, so the record's 1-Nov-2024 effective start remains flagged as unverified.
- Confirmed 4 AURUM Reward Points/INR100 non-fuel spend, INR0.25 per point, and AURUM Rewards Portal catalog redemption. Removed the unsupported statement-credit redemption and 0.22 realized-value estimate. Points do not expire under ordinary use, but SBI forfeits balances after 13 months without a transaction and applies other account-status forfeiture rules.
- Added exclusions supported by card terms and SBI notices: fuel MCCs 5172/5541/5983/5542, wallet-load MCCs 6540/6541, rent MCC6513, government MCCs9399/9311, digital-gaming MCCs5816/7993/7994, cash advances, fees and EMI.
- Reinstated the current page's five spend voucher benefits: INR1,500 per INR1 lakh monthly spend; annual milestone vouchers of INR5,000 at INR5 lakh, INR10,000 at INR10 lakh, INR9,999 at INR12 lakh and INR20,000 at INR20 lakh. The INR12 lakh level also aligns with the annual-fee reversal condition. Voucher brand/claim terms are not identified on the current product page.
- Updated welcome points condition to payment of first annual fee: 40,000 points, issuer-valued at INR10,000.
- Confirmed domestic lounge visits 4/quarter; unlimited international cardholder access through DreamFolks; one complimentary guest visit per quarter; 16 golf rounds/year, 12 lessons/year; quarterly spa; concierge; four monthly BookMyShow tickets worth up to INR1,000; Club Marriott, WSJ, Mint and IPV memberships; and current District dining discount.
- Confirmed fuel-surcharge waiver at 1% for INR500–4,000 transactions, capped INR250 per statement cycle.
- Added issuer-advertised insurance amounts: air accident INR3.5 crore; travel delay/baggage INR1 lakh; lost-card/fraud INR10 lakh; and two flight-cancellation refunds/year. Detailed insurance policy conditions are not linked from current product/benefit pages, so the entry records the advertised amounts and flags the limits of the evidence.
- Removed aggregator-only INR40 lakh income threshold. Kept invitation-only status and issuer policy age bands; no card-specific income or credit-score minimum is published.

## Caveats

SBI's current fee pages confirm amounts but not the effective date currently used for the latest fee period. Insurance conditions and the milestone voucher issuer/claim rules are not published on the product page. The schema's single network and tier fields cannot represent both Visa Infinite and Mastercard World variants.
