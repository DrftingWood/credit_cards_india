## Finding and record change

The Beyond-specific terms expressly provide unlimited complimentary cardholder access: section 9.1 covers participating domestic and international airport lounges within India, with four guest entries per calendar year; section 10.2 covers participating Priority Pass lounges outside India, also with four guest accesses per calendar year. Both terms separately describe primary and supplementary cardholder eligibility. The terms impose FUM qualification and note lounge participation/operator availability.

The product page's savings-value summary lists 8 domestic and 6 international visits, but those figures are valuation-summary quantities. They conflict with the operative Beyond-specific terms and the same page's unlimited-access headline; they are not the entitlement limits. Updated `data/cards/standard-chartered/beyond.yaml` to represent unlimited cardholder visits (`visits_per_cycle: null`) and describe annual guest allowances in notes.

This review resolves the lounge-access conflict only; other details retain their 2026-10-25 issuer review and were not rechecked.
