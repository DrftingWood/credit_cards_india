# SBI Card ELITE: issuer comparison (27 Oct 2026)

Compared the current SBI ELITE product page with the current KFS/MITC, cardholder agreement and reward cap policy, current lounge/priority-pass information, issuance policy and issuer-hosted contemporary launch article.

## Verified and corrected

- SBI’s current product page lists annual/renewal fees at INR4,999, a 1.99% forex markup, 3.75% monthly finance (current MITC for unsecured cards), 2.5%/INR500 cash-advance fee, no add-on fee and a INR10-lakh annual renewal waiver. The exact effective date of the current fee schedule is not published. The launch article from 6-Jul-2016 records a INR4,999 joining fee; current product page/KFS do not separately date its start.
- The exact card launch date is 6-Jul-2016, not 1-Sep-2015. Current product page lists Mastercard, Visa and American Express network privileges; no single network tier is established, so network/tier are left null and multi-network is tagged.
- Base earn is 2 Reward Points per INR100. Current page gives 5X dining, departmental-store and grocery earn, i.e. 10 total points per INR100 including base. The cardholder agreement caps cumulative accelerated earn at 10,000 points per month, after which base 2 points per INR100 continue. Corrected the old 1X/effective 5 and unlimited cap.
- Current agreement excludes cash advances, balance transfers/related services, financial charges, fuel, wallet MCCs 6540/6541, points+pay and other listed transactions. SBI’s current rent and government exclusion PDFs name ELITE for rent MCC6513 and government MCCs9399/9311. Merchant identification is best effort. Points expire after 24 months.
- Current product page states 4 points = INR1 for statement credit. Current agreement requires statement-credit redemptions in multiples of 2,000 points and charges INR99 plus tax per redemption batch. Removed the unsupported INR0.22 realized value and catalog rate; retained catalog redemption without a fixed value.
- Current issuer page supports annual milestones of 10,000 points at INR3 lakh and INR4 lakh, 15,000 at INR5 lakh and INR8 lakh (up to 50,000 points/year); INR5,000 welcome voucher; two domestic lounge visits/quarter; six international Priority Pass visits/year outside India, max two/quarter; free movie tickets worth INR6,000/year at max INR250/ticket; one-year ITC Silver membership after first transaction within 60 days; fuel waiver 1% on INR500-4,000 fuel transactions capped at INR250/statement; concierge; and INR1-lakh fraud-liability cover with the stated 48-hour pre-report / seven-day post-report window.
- Issuance policy supports salaried age 21-65 and self-employed age 25-70. No card-specific income floor or credit-score cutoff is published; removed the aggregator income requirement.
