# Utkarsh SuperCard issuer review (27 Oct 2026)

## Sources reviewed
- Product page: https://www.utkarsh.bank.in/personal/cards/credit-card/supercard
- MITC (21 May 2026): https://www.utkarsh.bank.in/xsite/assests/personal_credit_card/SuperCard_Most_Important_Terms_%26_Conditions_MITC.pdf
- KFS (21 May 2026): https://www.utkarsh.bank.in/xsite/assests/personal_credit_card/SuperCard_Key_Fact_Statement_KFS.pdf

## Record changes
- Removed unverified launch date and unsupported fuel-surcharge waiver.
- Confirmed no joining or annual membership fee; captured 3.5% monthly interest for unpaid dues, 5% cash-advance fee (domestic minimum ₹25), 3.5% forex markup, and current differentiated cash-interest terms in source notes.
- Updated cashback to 1% on eligible UPI and non-UPI spend from 11 Aug 2025; UPI requires super.money and is capped at ₹500 per statement month; non-UPI capped at ₹10,000 per month; minimum transaction ₹100.
- Removed Myntra/Flipkart accelerator from active rewards because the MITC campaign validity ended 30 Jun 2026 unless extended in-app.
- Recorded age 18+, FD-backed eligibility, and the ₹100 minimum deposit subject to bank discretion. Physical-card fee varies by deposit band.

## Unresolved / caveats
The product page continues to advertise preferred-merchant cashback language while the newer MITC sets a campaign end date. No in-app extension was available to verify. Cashback exclusion details and redemption mechanics are documented in the MITC; the normalized database schema cannot express all caps and conditions in structured fields. Verify current terms again at the next issuer review.
