# Paytm AU — issuer comparison (2026-11-01)

Compared the current AU Paytm product page and FAQs, AU/Paytm co-launch announcement dated 21-Oct-2026, AU KFS/Schedule of Charges v1.9 (30-Sep-2026), and current AU card-wide charges/rewards disclosures.

## Current offer and rewards

- RuPay, lifetime free. Current AU product page confirms no joining/annual fee, primary age bands (salaried 21–60, self-employed 25–65), add-on over 18 and resident Indian.
- The 21-Oct-2026 AU/Paytm announcement identifies up to 2% on eligible Paytm UPI spends and 5% on Paytm Travel bookings and Paytm Gold purchases. Up to 12 annual family lounge visits on eligible Paytm-booked round-trip flights, QR entry, and access for up to four family members are also announced.
- AU card page says 5% on Paytm app spends generally, while the newer co-launch announcement names Travel and Gold specifically. The dataset narrows the 5% rule to those named categories. Detailed UPI eligibility, reward caps, exact earned-point tiers, and detailed lounge booking terms remain unpublished in public sources reviewed.
- Paytm Gold redemption is INR 1 per point; AU page states INR 0.50 per point for other categories. No ordinary base earn rate or cap is published. No unsupported value or cap is inferred.
- General AU exclusions and applicable charges are retained with their source caveats. KFS gives current finance charge 3.75% monthly, cash advance 2.5%/INR 500 minimum, and Paytm AU fuel-waiver cap INR 100/month (1% on INR 400–5,000). Forex markup is not card-specifically published in the current AU markup list.

## Lounge scope limits

The product page alone advertised lounge benefits for cardholder and companions via Paytm Travel without a count or conditions. The newer October announcement establishes up to 12 annual complimentary visits and up to four family members on eligible round-trip bookings. It does not publish a complete booking/route/fare eligibility schedule or clarify whether companion entries count against the 12-visit total. The data records the headline and leaves these detailed conditions open.
