# Canara Bank RuPay Select — issuer evidence extract

Verified 2026-10-26 from the [current RuPay Select card page](https://www.canarabank.bank.in/pages/rupay-select-credit-card), whose footer shows a 2026-04-01 update, and Canara Bank's [Credit Card Variants & Features comparison PDF](https://www.canarabank.bank.in/documents/d/guest/credit-20card-20variants-20and-20features) (local copy: `canara-credit-card-variants-features-2026-02-09.pdf`).

## Terms transcribed

- Product is RuPay Select. The issuer page lists two domestic self lounge visits per quarter and two international self visits per year. The February 2026 comparison limits RuPay Select domestic lounge access to four visits per annum.
- Issuer page lists NIL enrollment and annual fees; the February 2026 comparison PDF separately says a one-time INR250 primary-card enrollment fee and INR400 annual add-on fee. Since these sources conflict, the current product page was followed for primary joining/annual fees, and the discrepancy is retained here for follow-up.
- The issuer page lists a 2.5% monthly revolving charge (30% APR), forex markup up to 3%, cash withdrawal fee 3% with INR150 minimum, and INR300 inactivity fee when annual turnover is below INR100,000.
- Reward schedule: 2 points per INR100 POS/e-commerce, fractions excluded; face value INR0.25 per point; redeemable for cashback or goods via Canara Rewardz / ai1.
- Fuel surcharge reimbursement: 1% fuel surcharge, INR400 minimum transaction, INR100 maximum per billing month; requires at least INR2,500 in other purchase spend that month. Reimbursement posts the following month.
- Current page advertises 24x7 concierge and states minimum independent gross income of INR10 lakh per year. It does not specify age, credit score or residency criteria.

## Limits and conflicts

The issuer page has duplicated product content and a fee table with imperfectly aligned rows. Its specific entries above were checked against the page's rendered order. The February 2026 comparison also supports points and RuPay lounge quota. The product page says card offers/features/charges can change; no exact effective date for the current values is published. `effective_from` is therefore null in the record, with review date stored separately. The INR0.18 realized point value previously recorded had no issuer support and was removed. Other unsupported excluded categories were removed rather than inferred.
