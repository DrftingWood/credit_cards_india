## Corrections and confirmations

- Business-essential cashback is 5% on utility, telecom, government and tax payments, capped INR 500/month. The dataset previously showed INR 250; corrected to INR 500.
- The FAQ states 4 Reward Points per INR 150 (excluding fuel, wallet loads and EMI), 8 domestic lounge visits annually (maximum two per quarter), and annual milestone awards of 10,000 points at INR 5 lakh and 15,000 total at INR 8 lakh.
- Joining/renewal fee is INR 1,000 plus applicable tax. First-year membership fee waiver: INR 75,000 non-EMI spend in the first 90 days. Renewal waiver: INR 1 lakh non-EMI spend in a 12-month period.
- FAQ states redemption to cashback at 100 points = INR 0.50, minimum redemption 2,500 points; voucher and air-mile values vary. Generic unsupported redemption estimates were replaced with this issuer figure.

## Limits

HDFC FAQ includes IRCTC in addition to utility, telecom, government and tax payments; canonical categories do not represent IRCTC distinctly. Full merchant-code mapping is not published. Supplier-payment interest terms are platform-specific.
