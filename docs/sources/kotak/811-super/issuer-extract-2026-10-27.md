# Kotak 811 Super Money current issuer review (27 Oct 2026)

## Sources checked

- [Kotak811 super.money card product page](https://www.kotak811.bank.in/credit-cards/811-super-money-credit-card): FD requirements/credit limit, eligibility, fees and current cashback structure.
- [Cashback terms and conditions](https://www.kotak811.bank.in/credit-cards/811-super-money-credit-card/terms-and-conditions): rate, platform, cap, minimum transaction, redemption and exclusions.
- [Kotak811 Super Money Key Fact Statement (version 12-Mar-2026)](https://www.kotak.bank.in/content/dam/Kotak/files/key-fact-statement/key-fact-statement-kfs-kotak-811-super-money-creditcard.pdf): joining/annual fees, finance charge, cash advance availability, FX markup and other charges.

## Record changes

- Cleared unsupported 1-Jun-2024 launch/effective date and RuPay Classic tier. Current issuer identifies the card as RuPay, without naming a sub-tier.
- Confirmed lifetime-free annual fee; joining fee ₹249 only for a physical card when FD is below ₹5,000 on activation. Minimum FD is ₹1,000, with credit limit up to 90% of FD. Finance is 3.5% monthly, FX markup 2%, and cash withdrawal is not permitted.
- Corrected reward model: 1% UPI through super.money app (₹100 minimum, ₹500 monthly cap); non-UPI Myntra 5%; Flipkart 2% (₹100 max cashback/transaction); other non-UPI platforms 1% (₹100 minimum, ₹10,000 monthly cap). Overall maximum cashback is ₹11,000 per monthly cycle. Old claim of 1% on all UPI and 5% Myntra omitted Flipkart and incorrectly represented UPI route/coverage.
- Cashback requires active card and Kotak811 Savings Account. Calendar-month cycle, round-down to 2 decimal places, redeem through super.money app after 35 days to linked default savings account. Cashback is nontransferable and can be forfeited on extended arrears/card or account closure/freeze.
- Removed unsupported rent/MCC reward exclusions and general 1% reward earn. Current terms exclude loan repayment, wallet loads, utilities/bills, card bill payment and EMIs. Current T&C say other non-UPI platforms earn 1% subject to the specified minimum/cap.
- Added current issuer eligibility: age 18+, resident Indian, Kotak811 Savings Account, valid KYC and minimum FD ₹1,000. Issuer says no CIBIL score or income proof required; issuance remains at bank discretion.

## Unresolved or ambiguous

- Product page has inconsistent fee wording: its headline/table may imply lifetime free, while its detailed fee table and KFS state ₹249 joining fee for physical cards where activation FD is below ₹5,000. Record captures this conditional charge.
- Cashback T&C place Myntra and Flipkart under one non-UPI partner category with ₹500 per-cycle cap language, but the page isn't explicit whether the cap is shared or applies per merchant. Model records the stated ₹500 against Flipkart and flags possible shared scope; verify with issuer before treating Myntra as uncapped.
- Cashback exclusion text is reported with list formatting that may omit any unpublished category conditions; merchant narration and MCC can affect eligibility.
- KFS includes additional transaction fees (rent, wallet load, utility, education, gaming, fuel) and dynamic currency conversion; schema does not model each fee type separately, so these are not encoded as standard card benefits/reward rules. Current charge details are in KFS.
- Exact launch date and a separate loyalty currency/value do not apply or are not published; cashback is redeemed as money through the app.
