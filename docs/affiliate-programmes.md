# Affiliate programmes for credit-card referrals

Access date for every source below: **2026-10-20**. Payout rates on these
programmes change often (several pages say "subject to change"), so re-check a
programme's own page before signing up or quoting a number.

Context: the site is small, new, has low traffic, runs on a vercel.app subdomain,
and is owned by an individual. "Official" means the programme's or network's own
page. Third-party sources are labelled and are weaker.

The site has **no affiliate links today**. The footer and `/about` carry a
disclosure worded to stay true before and after any link exists. If links are
added, the homepage claims "no affiliate links" and "₹0 affiliate income"
(`site/app/page.tsx`) must be changed in the same release, and links that can
earn money should be labelled where they appear.

## Detail

### BankBazaar

- No "partner with us" or affiliate sign-up page was found on bankbazaar.com. Its
  third-party disclosure page lists lenders it works with, not affiliates.
- SBI Card's RBI digital-lending disclosure lists "A&A Dukaan Financial Services –
  BankBazaar" as a credit-card sourcing platform since 21/12/2015. BankBazaar is
  a partner of the banks, not a programme open to publishers.
- Through **Cuelinks**: "Bank Bazaar Credit Card", CPL, "Payout Upto ₹225.00 /
  Lead". Leads are tracked at a lower commission first and corrected after
  validation. 1-day cookie. Text links and banners only; email, social, SEM,
  YouTube and in-app promotion disallowed. Missing transactions are not accepted,
  creatives need the merchant's approval, and any bank penalty caused by the
  publisher's miscommunication is passed to the publisher.
- Through **Admitad**: "BankBazaar CreditCard CPA IN" is shown as closed and
  limited to coupon, cashback and deal sites (third-party directory).
- A third-party blog quotes ₹500–₹1,500 per card for BankBazaar; it gives no
  source and is not used here.

### EarnKaro

- **Eligibility:** email, mobile number and password to sign up; a bank account to
  withdraw (FAQ). Members must be 18+ (terms, s. 5.2). EarnKaro's bank pages say
  anyone with a website, blog or social media account can join and that no
  documents are needed. Traffic minimums, PAN, GST and TDS are not published.
- **Payout model:** fixed slab per card.
  - AU Bank (Altura, Altura Plus, LIT, SwipeUp): ₹1,540–₹2,200, paid "After Card
    Activation"; the applicant needs a 700+ score, ₹25k/month income and to be
    21–60. Confirmed up to 75 days after activation.
  - IndusInd: up to ₹2,000; confirmed up to 75 days after activation. Tracks on
    web and mobile web only, not in apps or deep links.
  - IDFC FIRST: WOW up to ₹350; FIRST, Power, Power Plus and Club Vistara up to
    ₹750; SWYP up to ₹1,225. (The page's "up to ₹1,400" headline contradicts its
    own table.) 30-day cookie; confirmed within 75 days of activation.
  - No EarnKaro page found for SBI, HDFC, Axis or ICICI cards.
- **Payout:** minimum withdrawal ₹10 (terms, s. 7). NEFT to an Indian bank
  account, free, processed three times a week. Validation "can take up-to 90 days
  or more" (terms, s. 6.5).
- **Caveats:** zero tolerance for paid ads and brand bidding (s. 3.2). Earnings
  are cancelled if the transaction is cancelled and can be forfeited for misuse.
  Higher slabs are negotiable on request.

### Cuelinks

- **Eligibility:** "Free to join for all website owners, bloggers… social media
  influencers". Terms (last updated 1 Oct 2025): 18+, approval at Cuelinks' sole
  discretion, domain and traffic sources must be declared. PAN is required, plus
  GSTIN "if applicable"; "KYC and tax compliance is mandatory for payout release";
  payouts are net of TDS. No traffic minimum is published.
- **Payout:** ₹500 minimum for Indian bank accounts. You raise an invoice in the
  panel; payments go out on Fridays once the money has been "collected from the
  Merchant". Validation is Net-60.
- **HDFC credit card campaign:** up to ₹1,260/lead (₹2,195.10 for the Swiggy
  card). Paid only on activation plus a first transaction of ₹100+ within 30–35
  days. ₹320 deducted per physical KYC; video KYC done more than 50 km from the
  applicant's address is rejected. Applicants need a 780+ bureau score and
  new-to-credit applicants are excluded. 30-day cookie. Email, social and SEM
  disallowed.
- **Cuelinks' own blog (18 Mar 2026, promotional):** SBI ₹1,890 on dispatch; AU
  ₹1,312.50–₹1,500 on dispatch with a 4.55% validation rate; Axis ₹540–₹1,890 on
  activation with a 38% validation rate; HDFC ₹1,800–₹2,700 new-to-bank and
  ₹1,050 existing customers on first transaction; HSBC Platinum ₹3,037.50; ICICI
  ₹375 on approval; IDFC FIRST ₹1,012.50 on dispatch plus fee payment, 1-day
  cookie; RBL ₹1,050. The same post warns that several cards earned in the same
  window generally void commissions and that assisted applications are deducted.

### Direct bank programmes

- No public publisher or affiliate programme was found on the sites of HDFC,
  SBI Card, Axis, ICICI, AU or IDFC FIRST. Their card offers reach publishers
  through networks (Cuelinks, EarnKaro, INRDeals, vCommission, Admitad).
- Axis has a **customer** credit-card referral programme paying ₹500–₹1,000 in
  reward points. It is for cardholders, not websites.
- SBI Card's official sourcing-partner list (SBI YONO, Federal Bank, OLA Money,
  BankBazaar, Paytm) is corporate partners, not affiliates.
- Not found: an Impact.com programme for any Indian bank card; an official
  Paisabazaar card affiliate programme (its "Refer & Earn" pays ₹10 per
  credit-score sign-up, and its Cuelinks campaign is paused); a publisher
  programme on BankKaro (the CashKaro group's card offers reach publishers
  through EarnKaro).

## What applies to every option

- Commission is paid on **dispatch, activation or first transaction**, not on
  clicks or applications. Validation rates can be low (Cuelinks reports 4.55% for
  AU and 38% for Axis).
- Campaigns filter applicants: bureau score 730–780+, no new-to-credit, income
  floors. Physical KYC is often penalised.
- Paid search and brand bidding are banned almost everywhere. Many card campaigns
  also ban social media and email; some ban cashback and deal traffic.
- Cookies run from 1 day (BankBazaar and IDFC FIRST on Cuelinks) to 30 days.
- Expect 2–5 months from application to cash.
- Tax: Cuelinks publishes PAN-mandatory and TDS-deducted; vCommission requires
  PAN. EarnKaro and INRDeals do not publish their PAN or TDS rules.
- None of the programmes says a vercel.app subdomain disqualifies a site, but
  Cuelinks and vCommission screen applications, and a custom domain will help.

## Disclosure rules that apply once links go live

Checked 2026-10-20. Not legal advice; the primary texts were not read in full
(sources are law-firm and portal summaries unless marked).

- **CCPA, Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisements and Endorsements
  for Misleading Advertisements, 2022** (notified 9 June 2022). An endorser must
  disclose a material connection with the advertiser, and the endorsement must
  reflect a genuine, reasonably current opinion. Penalties reach ₹10 lakh (₹50
  lakh for repeat violations).
- **ASCI, Guidelines for Influencer Advertising in Digital Media** (applies to
  content from 14 June 2021). A "material connection" includes any monetary or
  other benefit, and affiliate deals are generally read as one. Disclosure is
  required even when the review is unbiased. A disclosure that appears only on an
  "about" page, in a bio, or at the end of content is "likely to be missed";
  accepted labels include "Ad", "Sponsored" and "Collaboration".
- **What this means for the site.** The footer and `/about` disclosure added on
  this branch is accurate while there are no affiliate links. Under the ASCI
  placement rule it would not be enough on its own once links exist: each
  affiliate link or card that carries one needs a visible label next to it (for
  example "Affiliate link: we may earn a commission"), and the homepage "no
  affiliate links" claims must go at the same time.
- **RBI.** The RBI Credit Card and Debit Card Issuance and Conduct Directions,
  2022 govern banks and their direct selling / marketing agents. Nothing found
  in this pass applies them directly to an affiliate website that only refers
  readers. If a programme asks the site to collect applicant data or act as a
  sourcing agent, re-check these rules first.

## Reading of the options for this site

Not a decision, just what the evidence suggests. EarnKaro has the lowest bar
(self sign-up, ₹10 minimum) and published per-card slabs, but no pages for the
four largest issuers. Cuelinks and INRDeals cover HDFC, SBI and ICICI, at a ₹500
minimum, with PAN and approval screening. There is no direct bank route and no
BankBazaar programme to join. Any of them would need card-level link mapping, and
because payouts depend on approval and activation, low-score or new-to-credit
readers would earn nothing however good the recommendation.
