## Current fees

The live product/fees pages list INR 2,500 joining and renewal fees, renewal waiver at INR 4 lakh in an anniversary year, 2% foreign currency markup, and 2.5%/INR 500 minimum cash advance fee. The 2025 Business MITC lists 3.75% monthly finance charges for non-premium variants including BizPower. This contradicts the database's 3.6% finance rate; corrected to 3.75%.

## Rewards effective 2026-05-15

The current product page and feature-change notice set core earn to 5 Reward Points per INR 200 on eligible spends, excluding fuel, wallet, education, rent and EMI. Selected business spends earn 5X, capped at 7,500 points per billing cycle, after at least INR 25,000 of non-tax eligible spend in a statement cycle (IT/GST spend excluded from the unlock threshold). Once unlocked, GST/advance tax transactions can earn accelerated rewards, subject to the transaction limit. The first two income-tax and first two GST transactions per billing cycle earn points; additional transactions do not.

Selected categories listed by the current page: IT/GST payments, SmartPay/PayZapp bills, MMT MyBiz flights/hotels, Nuclei business software, Google/Meta Ads and Reliance Digital. Historical 2024 terms show 5X as 20 points per INR 150 and a 5,000-point cap; these were superseded by the 2026 notice.

Current redemption table: statement credit INR 0.15 per point; SmartBuy flights/hotels up to INR 0.50; product catalogue up to INR 0.35; business catalogue up to INR 0.65; airline conversion up to 0.5 miles per point. Statement-credit redemption minimum is 2,500 points. Overall points are capped at 60,000 per statement cycle; unredeemed points expire after two years.

## Benefits effective 2026-07-01

- Quarterly voucher: INR 5,000 MakeMyTrip or Reliance Digital voucher after INR 2.5 lakh quarterly retail spend, excluding petrol, IT, GST and EMI.
- Domestic lounges: two visits per calendar quarter and one domestic flight-cancellation voucher per quarter, unlocked after INR 75,000 spend in the previous calendar quarter.
- International lounge access: six visits per year via Priority Pass; application available after four retail transactions.
- Welcome benefit: Amazon Prime membership or INR 1,500 Amazon voucher after one IT/GST, travel or eligible advertising transaction within 30 days. Since 2025-10-25, first-time cardholders only; excludes upgrades, migrations and conversions.
- Other live page claims include INR 9 lakh credit liability cover, zero lost-card liability if reported immediately, and optional paid business insurance starting at INR 3,785/year.

## Eligibility

Current page: self-employed Indian citizen age 21-65, annual ITR above INR 12 lakh. Salaried criteria and credit-score cutoff were not published on the reviewed issuer page.
