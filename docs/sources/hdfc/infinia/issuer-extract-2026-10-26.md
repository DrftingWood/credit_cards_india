## Confirmed current claims

- Metal Edition joining and annual fee: INR12,500 + tax; renewal waiver at INR10 lakh spend in the preceding 12 months. The issuer distinguishes older Infinia cards sourced before 2021-10-18 (different fee schedule), so current terms apply to Metal Edition/newer issuance.
- Foreign-currency markup: 2%; revolving/cash-advance finance rate: 1.99% monthly (23.88% annual). HDFC MITC exempts Infinia and Infinia Metal from the common cash-advance transaction fee; interest still applies to cash advances.
- Fuel surcharge waiver: 1%, qualifying transactions INR400?INR100,000, capped at INR1,000 per statement cycle; GST on surcharge is not reversed.
- Base earn: 5 Reward Points per INR150. Current page says up to 10x on SmartBuy, and names Insurance, Utilities and Education among retail earn categories. Insurance accrual is capped at 10,000 points/month; utility and telecom at 2,000/month each. Education via third-party apps does not earn. Rent, government, e-wallet loads and Easy EMI are excluded in the MITC.
- Current issuer redemption table: INR1/point for flights/hotels and listed Apple/Tanishq purchases; up to INR0.50 for products/vouchers; INR0.30 as statement credit; up to 1 Airmile/point. Statement-credit redemptions are capped at 50,000 points/month; flights/hotels/airmiles at 150,000/month; total redemption at 200,000 points/statement cycle. Thus the modeled best-path value is INR1 and the cash-credit realization is INR0.30; neither is a universal value.
- Product is invite-only. HDFC states continued Infinia programme access from 2026-04-01 requires INR18 lakh primary-plus-add-on spend or INR50 lakh relationship value; this is a retention condition, not a published application cutoff.
- Product page advertises unlimited domestic lounge access for primary/add-on holders and unlimited international access through Priority Pass; it does not state a guest quota. Guest count remains unknown.
