## Recorded issuer evidence

- **Fees:** INR 1 joining fee, no annual fee, 2% forex; generic current MITC records 3.75% monthly finance charge and 2.5%/INR 500 minimum cash advance.
- **Rewards:** 1 point per INR 100 base; 5 total points/INR 100 on qualifying online, dining and utility transactions, capped at 1,000 accelerated points per statement cycle. Annexure exclusions, INR 100 minimum transaction, UPI restriction and two-year expiry are in the shared terms. Cashback is INR 0.25/point with a 1,000-point minimum. UPI earns core only.
- **Benefits:** three domestic lounge visits per quarter after INR 40,000 eligible spend in the prior quarter; 500 welcome points after INR 5,000 in 60 days; 4,000 quarterly milestone points when eligible quarter spend exceeds the higher of INR 100,000 or the assigned credit limit; fuel waiver; INR 5 lakh professional-indemnity cover for practicing CMA members with a valid Certificate of Practice, INR 15 lakh air-accident death cover and INR 5 lakh non-air accidental-death cover; FITPASS PRO and add-on cards.
- **Eligibility:** active ICMAI membership; resident Indian; age 21-75; annual income/ITR at least INR 3 lakh. Minimum bureau-score threshold is unpublished.
